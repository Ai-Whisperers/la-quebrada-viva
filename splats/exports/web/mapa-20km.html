Surface water (audited) JRC + OSM + DEM

Surface water audit cross-checks each OSM water feature against:
· JRC Global Surface Water (1984–2024 occurrence — pixels detected as water in 40 years of Landsat)
· Copernicus GLO-30 DEM (slope + elevation — wetlands sit in flat depressions)
· OSM waterways (within 200 m of a tagged stream?)
Verdict shown on click: ✓ verified (JRC ≥ 20% + flat), ~ seasonal (10–20%), ? ambiguous, ✗ likely-mis-tagged (JRC < 5% AND steep slope).

For the Escobar 20 km box: only 3 persistent waterbodies detected by JRC (no large natural lakes — Atlantic Forest catchments are steep and well drained). Most OSM water=stream polygons are point-depictions of small streams, not lakes.
