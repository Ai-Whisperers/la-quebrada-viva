About this map

Property perimeter from Wesley's on-the-ground GPS walk (Guru Maps iOS, 06-22 + 06-28): 17 corners, walked in order, 4.26 km perimeter, 71.4 ha projected. Handheld GPS accuracy ±3–5 m per point — not survey-grade.

Timestamps recovered from the live Guru Maps share (KML · 20 timestamps). Two sessions: 5 pts on 06-22 (north ridge), 15 pts on 06-28 (full perimeter). Hit ▶ Replay walk above to watch the perimeter being captured in time order.

10 km box (-57.13 / -56.93 lon, -25.70 / -25.52 lat) centred on the GPS centroid. Built from:
· Sentinel-2 L2A NDVI (mosaic of 2 MGRS tiles)
· Copernicus GLO-30 DEM + D8 flow accumulation
· JRC Global Surface Water 1984–2024
· MapBiomas Paraguay Collection 2 (2023)
· Hansen GFC v1.12 forest loss / gain
· OpenStreetMap Overpass (2026-07-05): 30,166 features

Icons courtesy of Guru Maps — the same bookmark pins Wes uses in his iOS app appear here so the map reads identically to his phone view.
