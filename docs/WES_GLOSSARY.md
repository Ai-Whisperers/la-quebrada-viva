## E. Wes's own rules (the 10 design rules)

Wes articulated **10 design rules** that govern everything. They are
in [`MASTER_BRIEF.md`](./_reconciled/MASTER_BRIEF.md) §14 and mirrored
in [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) §"Things to refuse / push back on":

1. **Landscape first** — buildings follow the land, never the reverse
2. **Low-impact footprint** — cabins sit lightly
3. **Local materials where possible** — bamboo, lapacho, earth, stone
4. **Native species in landscaping** — no ornamental imports
5. **Sonja is the canonical cultural/worker/price authority** — defer to her
6. **Cultural authenticity over efficiency** — tatakuá not gas oven
7. **Reforestation, not just preservation** — actively replant native species
8. **Family-anchored** — the place holds the family together
9. **8-foot corredor around the whole house** — Paraguayan tradition
10. **Open community, not gated** — gate + camera only
