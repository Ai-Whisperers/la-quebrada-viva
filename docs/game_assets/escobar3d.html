5-tier LOD by camera height

The terrain auto-switches based on your altitude, not distance: Surface (<350 m, 1.5 m/px + 500× hillshade), Parcel (350–1500 m, 1.5 m/px), Escobar (1500–6000 m, 15 m/px), Regional (>6000 m, 60 m/px). Vertical exaggeration ramps 220× → 450× as you descend into Surface.
