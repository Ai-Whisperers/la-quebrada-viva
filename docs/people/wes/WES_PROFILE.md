## 6. How Wes makes decisions (the pattern)

### The "wish → AI → human → choice" loop

Wes articulated this himself. He uses a **3-step pattern** for almost every major decision:

```
Wes articulates the wish
        ↓
AI generates 3-5 candidate options (with tradeoffs)
        ↓
Sonja / human reality-checks (cultural, financial, legal)
        ↓
Wes picks 1 final choice
```

Examples he's used:
- **Project name** (W0.6): Wes mentioned candidates → W03 check did domains → Sonja + Ivan input → Wes picks (pending)
- **Materials research** (W0.5): Wes picks 5 of 15 → AI does 5 → results → Wes decides which become canon
- **Cabin typologies**: Wes sketched 18 → AI rendered → Ivan reviewed → Wes picked final designs
- **Site layout**: B02 (auto-fill, awaiting Wes input) — interactive tool → drag houses → adjust

**Implication for AI Whisperers:** never give him 1 option. Always give 3-5. The decision is his.

### What speeds up his decisions

| Speed-up | Example |
|---|---|
| **External constraint** (legal deadline, market window) | Escribana Anexo I was due 5 May — forces action |
| **Money quote** (specific € amount) | €300-500 attorney call is a concrete step |
| **Sonja's recommendation** | Per Rule 5, her input usually closes the question |
| **Concrete visual** | He responds faster to rendered images than text |
| **Other people's experiences** | "The German town visited", "Yellowstone", "Bali bamboo prefab" — he references |
| **A/B/C ranking** | "pick one of these three" is faster than "open-ended brainstorm" |

### What slows him down
