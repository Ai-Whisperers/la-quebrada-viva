## Decision criteria (what makes a good name)

For this project, a name must:

1. **Sound good in 3 languages** — NL, ES, EN (Wes's clients + Wes himself + Sonja's PY network)
2. **Be memorable** — under 3 syllables for verbal use
3. **Work for 4 different BV names** — BV-PY-1 (land), BV-NL (finance), BV-PY-2 (Fase 1), BV-PY-3 (Fase 2-3)
4. **Be domain-available** — at least .com or .com.py (paraguayan ccTLD)
5. **Be trademark-clear** — no major conflicts in tourism / hospitality
6. **Work in URL, email, business card** — no special chars, accents OK
7. **Convey "natural, family-anchored, multi-generational"** — the brand ethos
8. **Not be too clever** — must work for an ESL Paraguay employee to say

## The 3 FINAL recommendations

After all 100 candidates + Wes's stated preferences, my honest top 3:

### 🥇 #1 — **Villa del Cielo** ("Villa of the Sky")

**Why:**
- Matches Wes's explicit preference: "Voorkeur voor 'mooi Spaans' boven 'yellowstone-achtig'"
- Beautiful in 3 languages (NL speakers will say "Vee-ya del See-eh-lo", EN speakers will get close, ES speakers say it natively)
- Aspirational without being pretentious — "sky" suggests openness, possibility
- Works for 4 BVs: **Villa del Cielo S.A. (PY-1 land), Villa del Cielo Holding B.V. (NL finance), Villa del Cielo Resort S.A. (PY-2 Fase 1), Villa del Cielo Expansion S.A. (PY-3 Fase 2-3)**
- Domain likelihood: 6/10 (villa + del + cielo are common words; may need variant like villadelcielopy.com or villa-del-cielo-resort.com)

**The honest roast:**
- "Villa" might feel single-house, not park — but in ES hospitality, "Villa" often refers to a small estate (e.g., Villa del Sol, Villa Serena). Acceptable.
- The "del Cielo" suffix is romantic; Sonja may love or hate it. Run by her.
- Might be confused with Mexican hotels (Villa del Sol is everywhere). Differentiate with **"Villa del Cielo — Escobar Paraguay"** subtitle.

**Verdict:** **Best match for Wes's stated preference.** Test the domain tonight (`villadelcielo.com`, `villadelcielo.com.py`).

---

### 🥈 #2 — **Riverstone Valley**

**Why:**
- Wes's first instinct ("stolen from Yellowstone, it's Riverstone Valley. Boom. Boom. Boom.")
- Strong all-around brand: works in NL, EN, ES
- "Stone" = durability theme, matches the parcel's stone escarpment
- "Valley" = matches our geography (we're in a quebrada/valley)
- Works for 4 BVs: **Riverstone Valley S.A. (PY-1 land), Riverstone Valley Holding B.V. (NL), Riverstone Valley Resort S.A. (PY-2), Riverstone Valley Expansion S.A. (PY-3)**
- Domain likelihood: 8/10 (riverstone + valley is fresh)

**The honest roast:**
- Wes's own words: "But it's not sure." — so he didn't commit
- Wes then said "voorkeur voor 'mooi Spaans' boven 'yellowstone-achtig'" — preference for Spanish over English/Yellowstone-style
- So Riverstone Valley is **his gut** but **not his preference**. Picking it means overruling his stated preference in favor of his first instinct.
- The 4-BV structure works, but "Riverstone" doesn't translate to ES (would stay as English)
- The **"Yellowstone" association is American** — may not convey "Paraguayan authenticity" which is part of his brand

**Verdict:** **Strongest brand, weakest cultural authenticity.** Pick if Wes wants international tourism brand. Reject if he wants PY authenticity.

---

### 🥉 #3 — **Valle de Lapachos** ("Valley of the Lapachos")

**Why:**
- Place-specific: lapacho is Paraguay's national tree, blooms pink in Sept-Oct
- All-PY: "Valle" + "de" + native tree = authentic
- Works for 4 BVs: **Valle de Lapachos S.A., Valle de Lapachos Holding B.V., etc.**
- Domain likelihood: 5/10 (compound name may need shortening for URL)

**The honest roast:**
- Pronunciation challenge: NL/EN speakers will struggle with "Lapachos" (lah-PAH-chos)
- "Valle" sounds like a literal valley — we're in a quebrada, not a valley. Misnomer.
- The 4-BV cascade works but full name is long: "Valle de Lapachos Resort S.A." (over 30 chars)
- Could be shortened to just **"Lapachos"** — single word, brand-able, iconic. But then "losing" the "Valle" descriptor.

**Verdict:** **Strongest authenticity, weakest URL.** Pick if Wes prioritizes PY authenticity + native-tree theme over international tourism brand.
