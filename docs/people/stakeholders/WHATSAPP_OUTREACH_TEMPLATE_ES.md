# Messaging Outreach Template — ES (PY-domestic brokers)

> **Use for:** Mapfre PY, La Consolidada, Seguros Atlántida, and any other PY-domestic insurer. **Send via Wes's own Messaging**, not Erebus/Ivan (per `WES_HOW_WE_WORK.md`).

---

## When to use this template

- Wes has a personal contact at the broker (from Kiki's network), OR
- First cold outreach after LinkedIn profile confirm, OR
- After a brief phone introduction, the broker asks for written context

**Always:**
1. Send as **single brief message** (no wall of text)
2. Attach `INSURANCE_PROPERTY_DATASHEET.md` as PDF — not as link to Google Drive
3. Include opt-out line at the end
4. Don't send padron / escritura scan / contract details — those need in-person
5. **Voice note follow-up** if no reply in 3 business days (PY brokers prefer voice)

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't dump the full datasheet body in the chat — attach as PDF
- ❌ Don't mention NL holding / 4-BV structure on first contact — that's for the formal quote call
- ❌ Don't send any PHI (escritura scan, padron, contract) on Messaging
- ❌ Don't ask for "best price" — ask for "indicative quote, first round"
- ❌ Don't write in English (Los brokers PY responden en español primero)

## After the broker replies

1. Move conversation to a 30-min phone call
2. Send the datasheet + 5-10 photos of the property
3. Ask for **binding indication** within 4 weeks
4. Schedule follow-up; keep Ivan / Erebus in the loop
