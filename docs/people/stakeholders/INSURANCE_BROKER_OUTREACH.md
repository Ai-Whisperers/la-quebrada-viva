## 3. Outreach objectives (Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3)

### Tier 1 — must-have answers
- **Availability:** can they quote 62-ha forest park in Paraguay AT ALL?
- **Coverages:** fire, storm/hurricane, general liability, public liability (guests)
- **Indicative annual premium:** at < 0.2% of €5.5M = €11k/year target
- **Quote timeline:** when can they deliver a binding indication? (4-6 weeks realistic)

### Tier 2 — sharpens the deal
- **Wildfire mitigation requirements:** do they require firebreaks, water points, on-site guard? (per R01 fire safety plan)
- **Storm/hurricane deductible:** PY storm season Oct-Apr, what's the deductible vs full replacement?
- **Coverage for:** under-construction structures (mid-build, wooden frames), workers comp, food poisoning (restaurant)
- **Multi-year discounts:** 2-3 year bind vs annual

### Tier 3 — operational
- **Claims process:** in-country adjusters? Messaging claims?
- **Premium financing:** quarterly, monthly?
- **Compliance:** INFONA, MADES, bomberos inspection documented as required?

## 4. If brokers say "no" or "too expensive"

| Failure mode | Fallback |
|---|---|
| Marsh/Aon declines PY risk entirely | Try **fronting policy** — NL insurer + PY reinsurance carrier (Kiki's network again) |
| All PY brokers quote >€40k/year | Consider **captive insurer** in Uruguay (free trade zone) |
| Liability uninsurable | Self-insure liability (€2-5k/year reserve) + bind fire/storm only |
| Storm uninsurable | Reduce wooden structures; build cob/concrete-only (R01 risk mitigation + recommended in `WARNINGS.md §3`) |
