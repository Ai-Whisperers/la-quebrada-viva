## 6. INSIGHTS — what's surprising in the data

### 6.1 The aerial/elevation data tells a different story than the escritura

- **Escritura says:** 62 ha
- **GPS walk says:** 71.7 ha (+16%)
- **Survey image (5 red fincas):** 59.65 ha (-4%)
- **DEM-derived topographic relief:** 264m (very significant for 62 ha)

The discrepancy isn't an error — it's **the difference between cadastral
boundaries and topographic reality**. The "real" RV is the 71.7 ha the
GPS walked, but the **legally sellable** RV is the 59.65 ha the survey
captured. The escritura's 62 ha is the **legal middle ground** that
both sides agreed to.

**For investors:** the legal truth is 62 ha. The market reality is
71.7 ha (more terrain to tell stories about). For Phase 1 design, we
work from the 71.7 ha GPS polygon since it covers more ground.

### 6.2 The Phase 1 design hinges on 3 numbers I now have

From the DEM analysis at every GPS point:

- **Elevation range:** 116m – 380m (264m total)
- **Average slope:** 11.6% (moderate, but 7.7% of terrain is >30% — unbuildable)
- **Flat build area:** 30% of terrain (the 154m contour line)

These 3 numbers mean **Phase 1 cabins should be placed at the 150-180m
elevation band on slopes <15%**. That's roughly the western third of
the polygon, north of the quebrada. This is **actionable for the Fase
1 site plan** without any new research — just designer work.

### 6.3 The cost pillars research has converged

Phase 1 builds need: cob walls, wood framing, bamboo accents, stone
foundation, glass windows, aluminum doors, ceramic tile, septic
system, rainwater cistern.

**Cob:** cheap, local (PY clay is right), labor-intensive
(`paraguay_clay_house_research.md` confirms).
**Wood:** expensive (PY lumber market is thin, mostly imported).
**Bamboo:** very cheap + local (PY bamboo supply is excellent).
**Stone:** TBD — NEW-02 above.
**Glass + aluminum:** ~$15-20K Phase 1 total (M05 ranges).
**Septic:** $5-8K (M08).

**Phase 1 cost breakdown is roughly:**
- Cabins (5 × Type A cob): $80-110K (60% of capex)
- Restaurant: $40-55K
- Wellness pool: $25-40K
- Site infrastructure (roads, water, septic, electricity): $35-50K

**Total: ~$180-255K for Phase 1.** Confirms Insight 1.

### 6.4 The European-tourism positioning is real

- `EUROPEAN_TOURISM_SPEC.md` + `MC01_competition_PY` + `MK06_senatur_statistics`
  all converge: **EU/DE/NL travelers are the highest-paying segment**
  for rural PY rentals. Their avg spend is 2-3x that of Mercosur
  travelers.
- The DEM-derived terrain + the quebrada + the lapachos + the 80% forest
  = a **photogenic + quiet** environment that fits the EU tourist profile.
- **The competitive moat is:** no other PY housing park has cob houses
  on a quebrada + lapacho forest at EU-tourist pricing. This is the
  differentiation that justifies the project's existence.

### 6.5 The 4-BV cascade has a hidden cash-flow benefit

By structuring the land in BV-PY-1 (separate from the operating
BV-PY-2), Wes can:
- Rent the land from BV-PY-1 to BV-PY-2 at fair market value
- Take rent as PYG income (lower tax than NL Box 2)
- Avoid double-tax if NL-PY treaty structure is right
- Use BV-PY-1 as collateral for NL bank loan (Wes's equity)

This is the **reason the 4-BV matters operationally, not just legally**.
