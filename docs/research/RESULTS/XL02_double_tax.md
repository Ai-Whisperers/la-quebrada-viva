## Strategies to use

**1. Maximize participation exemption (NL side):**
- BV2 holds ≥5% of BV3 ✓
- BV3 is operating company (not passive holding) ✓
- BV2 has substance (Wes + Thijs oversight, decisions, capital) ✓
- Net result: 0% NL tax on received dividends (per L19)

**2. Reduce PY withholding (PY side):**
- Direct holding > 30% → 5% (vs 15% default) ✓
- Use BV2 as single foreign holder (consolidates) ✓
- Apply for reduction via annual filing ✓

**3. Use BV2 substance (anti-abuse):**
- BV2 must have real presence (Wes + Thijs's time, decisions)
- BV2 has separate bank account, accounting
- BV2 has capital + active role (not just holding)
- Documentation: invoices, board minutes, contracts ✓

**4. Avoid aggressive structures:**
- No round-tripping (BV2 → BV3 → BV2 for no reason)
- No related-party transactions that look artificial
- No "letterbox" companies
- Real economic substance

**5. Use Dutch unilateral relief (box 2 vs box 3):**
- Some income can be classified as "savings" (box 3) instead of "substantial interest" (box 2)
- 36% box 3 deemed return vs 27% box 2 effective rate
- Sometimes favorable
- Subject to substance tests

**6. Use Wes's individual structuring:**
- Wes personal vs BV2 holdings
- Trust or family office structure (for higher wealth)
- Anti-abuse rules apply
