## What LQV is NOT

- ❌ Eco-luxury generic resort
- ❌ "Ecopark" (politically loaded term in PY)
- ❌ A single "barndominium" or "B&B" — it's a park
- ❌ Eco-budget hostels
- ❌ Generic luxury hotel
- ❌ "Coworking + nature" hub (that's Wes's separate AI work)

## What LQV is (precise)

**Project Type:** Eco-vacation-rental park with cabined lodging + restaurant + events venue
**Market segment:** European (Dutch, German) travelers + Asunción expats + San Bernardino weekend market
**Style:** Authentic eco-luxury (not generic luxury, not eco-budget)
**Differentiation:** Paraguayan-first, European-quality, family-anchored community
**Phase 1 (Y1):** 5 cabins + reception + restaurant
**Phase 2 (Y2):** 10 more cabins + restaurant expansion + wellness pool
**Phase 3 (Y3):** 15 more cabins + full amenities
**Phase 4 (Y4+):** Events venue (wedding + corporate) + Senja's 60th weekend

## 4-BV structure (operational)

**Per 4ENTITY_BV_CASCADE.md:**
- BV1 (Land, PY S.A.) — long-term land equity
- BV2 (Finance, NL BV) — Dutch investor interface
- BV3-5 (Fase 1-3, PY E.A.S.) — operational, independent
- **Machinepark cascade:** machines bought by BV3 sold to BV4 at cost-plus

## Why this structure is the right one

| Alternative | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Single PY entity | ❌ Risk concentration, no investor protection |
| Foreign-owned directly (Wes 100%) | ❌ Tax + operational mess |
| Trust (fideicomiso) | ❌ Complex, not well-suited for hospitality |
| NL operating + PY property | ❌ Double tax + operational complexity |
| **4-BV cascade (this one)** | ✅ Independent phases, equipment recovery, NL interface |

## Risk profile

**Worst case:** Fase 1 fails, BV3 goes bankrupt, BV1 (land) is protected from operational debt. Wes + Thijs still own the 62 ha. Fase 2-3 can restart with different operator.

**Best case:** All phases operational, machinepark rolls forward, Wes + Thijs have $5M+ in equity + annual revenue.

**Most likely:** 18-24 months to positive cashflow, 4-5 years to stable, 5-7 years to scale.
