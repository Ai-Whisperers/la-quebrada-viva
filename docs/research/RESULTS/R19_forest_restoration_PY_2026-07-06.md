## Executive summary

Paraguay's Atlantic Forest restoration is **active** — Guyra Paraguay + Moisés Bertoni + WWF-PY are the major players. The LQV's 90% mature canopy means **restoration is mostly maintenance**, not replanting. But there's **grant money** available from INFONA + international carbon programs. LQV qualifies for at least 2-3 of these programs.

## Reforestation methodologies applicable to LQV

| Method | Time to canopy | Cost/ha USD | Best for LQV? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Miyawaki** (dense native planting) | 10-15 yr | $8,000-15,000 | No (small-scale, for gardens) |
| **Enrichment planting** (under canopy) | 5-10 yr | $3,000-6,000 | Yes — for the 32 ha "secondary" forest area |
| **Native species nursery + manual planting** | 10-20 yr | $5,000-10,000 | Yes — for cleared areas (if any) |
| **Natural regeneration** (fence off + wait) | 20-40 yr | $500-1,000 | Best — for the 90% mature canopy |
| **Direct seeding** (hand-seeding native species) | 8-15 yr | $2,000-4,000 | Yes — for marginal gaps |
| **Assisted migration** (climate-resilient species) | 15-25 yr | $6,000-12,000 | Long-term climate adaptation |

**For LQV**: the **32 ha of "secondary" forest** (the 30.9 ha buildable cluster area + edge forest) is where enrichment planting makes sense. **$3,000-6,000/ha × 10 ha to actually plant = $30,000-60,000** for a meaningful restoration project.

## Carbon credit math

**LQV forest**:
- 62 ha × ~110 tonnes biomass/ha (Atlantic Forest, mature) = ~6,800 tonnes total biomass
- Of that, ~50% is carbon by mass = ~3,400 tonnes C = ~12,500 tonnes CO2 equivalent
- Annual increment from natural growth: ~2-3% = ~250-375 tonnes CO2/year
- **Avoided deforestation credit**: 12,500 t CO2 one-time × $10/t = **$125,000 one-time**
- **Annual increment credit**: 300 t CO2/year × $10/t = **$3,000/year ongoing**

**Project economics**: a Verra VCS project costs $30,000-50,000 to set up + audit. Payback in Year 1 ($125K) + ongoing ($3K/year). **Verra VCS makes sense for LQV in Year 2-3.**
