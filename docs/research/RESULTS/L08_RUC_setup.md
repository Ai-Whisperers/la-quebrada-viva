**Method:** MEM + training data
**Confidence:** High

## What is RUC

**RUC = Registro Único del Contribuyente** (Single Taxpayer Registry)
- Issued by **SET (Subsecretaría de Estado de Tributación)** — the PY tax authority
- Equivalent to US EIN or Spanish NIF
- Required for: any business, any commercial activity, opening a business bank account
- Cost: free (no fee, just paperwork)
- Lead time: 1-2 weeks (online) or 2-4 weeks (in-person)

## What you need to apply

For each BV (4 total in the cascade):

| Required doc | Where to get | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Constitutive act (escritura de constitución) | Lawyer drafts, escribano certifies | Public deed at a notaría |
| Bylaws (estatutos) | Lawyer drafts | Approved by the founding partners |
| RUC form (Marangatu or SET portal) | Online at marangatu.set.gov.py | Or in-person at SET |
| Personal ID of all partners/directors | RUC for individual partners | RUC for individuals too |
| Proof of address of the BV | Recibo de ANDE/ESSAP/aguateros, contrato de alquiler | In practice: company address proof |
| Timbrado | Same SET portal, separate application | For invoice issuance |

## Process for 1 BV (apply × 4)

### Step 1: Reserve the company name (1 day)
- Free, online at SCV (Superintendencia de Compañías y Valores)
- Name must be unique, can't conflict with existing PY companies
- **Try the name reservation BEFORE finalizing the BV name** (tied to W0.3)

### Step 2: Get the constitutive act (1-2 weeks)
- Hire a lawyer in PY (~$500-1,000 per BV)
- Lawyer drafts estatutos + escritura de constitución
- Notarized at an escribanía
- **Cost: $500-1,000 per BV + notaría fees**

### Step 3: Register with SCV (1 week)
- Submit the constitutive act
- SCV approves the entity formation
- Get the "Personería Jurídica" document
- **Cost: $100-200 (SCV fees)**

### Step 4: Apply for RUC at SET (1-2 weeks)
- Online via Marangatu portal (recommended, faster)
- Or in-person at a SET office
- **Required docs:** constitutive act, bylaws, personal IDs of partners, proof of address
- **Cost:** free
- **Lead time:** 1-2 weeks online, 3-4 weeks in-person

### Step 5: Apply for Timbrado (1-2 weeks)
- For invoice issuance
- Separate from RUC
- Online at Marangatu
- **Cost:** free
- **Lead time:** 1-2 weeks

### Step 6: Open business bank account (1 week)
- Bring RUC + Timbrado + estatutos + personal IDs
- **Recommended bank:** Banco Itaú Paraguay (commercial, foreigner-friendly)
- **Initial deposit:** ~Gs. 5,000,000-10,000,000 ($700-1,400) for the operational BVs
- **Lead time:** 1 week

## Special case: foreign directors (Wes is NL)

**Wes is a Dutch national. He needs:**
- RUC for individual (foreign national) — easier than BV RUC
- **Apostilled passport** (NL stamp for international use)
- **Cédula Paraguaya** if residing in PY (optional)
- For BV where Wes is director: same requirements, but show passport + apostille
