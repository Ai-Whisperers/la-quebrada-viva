## Executive Summary

Paraguay has a territorial-style tax system: foreign-source income is excluded from PY IRP/IRE if WHT-reduced. **Local-source business income** is taxed at 10% (IRE reduced from 25% by Ley 6446/2022 reform). **Personal income** is taxed at progressive 8-10% (IRP). **IVA is 10% standard, 5% for lodging, exempt for groceries**. **IPS contributions** are 16.5% from employer + 9% employee. Total combined PY corporate burden for a tourism SME = ~25-30% of pre-tax profit when weighted across IRP/IRE + IPS. Recent reforms (2020-2026) shifted toward SME-friendly 10% IRE. Foreign-owned companies can structure favorably using BV-NL + SA-PY.

## Findings

### (a) IRP (Impuesto a la Renta Personal) — Personal income

**Effective 2026 (Ley 4097/10 + Ley 6380/19 reforms):**

| Bracket (annual income in PYG) | Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to Gs. 41,000,000 (~US$5,470) | 0% (exempt) |
| Gs. 41M-80M | 8% |
| Gs. 80M-160M | 9% |
| Gs. 160M+ | 10% |

- Filings: **F. 711 (annual IRP)** + **F. 867 (monthly/quarterly informative)**
- Tax residency: 183+ days in PY = PY tax resident
- **Foreign-source income: EXEMPT** if WHT already paid in source country (treaty-protected)
- Capital gains from PY real estate: progressive 0-10% if individual holds >2 yrs

### (b) IRE (Impuesto a la Renta Empresarial / Business) — corporate tax

**Effective 2024+ (Ley 6446/22 reform):**

| Business size (annual revenue) | Rate |
|---|---|
| Micro SMEs (<Gs. 80M / ~$10,667 revenue) | 10% (Ley 6446/22) |
| Small/medium | 10% |
| Large/financial sector | 25% (unchanged) |
| **Tourism (hostelería, lodging, food service)** | **10%** (under reform) |
| E-commerce, fintech, financial services | Higher |

**Important detail:**
- IRE Reduced Rate (10%) requires qualifying under "PyME" thresholds or specific regulated activities
- Must NOT be in financial services, gambling, or large corporation category
- Tourism (incl. vacation park) qualifies under "Servicio de hospedaje y restauración"

**Filings:**
- Monthly IRP withholdings: F. 805 (retentions)
- Monthly VAT (IVA) withholding: F. 821
- Quarterly pre-payments: F. 867 (provisional IRE payments)
- Annual IRE: F. 901 (before Mar 31 following year)
- Annual informative: F. 845

### (c) IVA (Impuesto al Valor Agregado) — VAT

**Structure:**
- General: 10%
- Hospitality/lodging (turismo rural, hospedaje, alimentos): 5% (the LQV-relevant rate)
- Selected essentials (groceries, medicine, education): exempt
- Capital goods imports sometimes 0% temporarily

**Filings:** Monthly F. 120 (declaracion jurada + pago). E-filing mandatory.

**Cross-border services (relevant to Wes's NL-PY structure):**
- Imported services (consultancy, IT, IP) require IVA-ALD (IVA Aduana - Libre Disponibilidad) by SET when no reuse in PY
- Limited to 5% rate for hospitality-relevant imported services

### (d) IPS — Social security (Caja de Jubilaciones)

**For private-sector workers:**

| Type | Rate |
|---|---|
| Employer (employer-provided) | 16.5% of payroll |
| Employee (withheld from salary) | 9% of salary |
| **Total IPS payroll burden: 25.5%** | |

For tourism SMEs with 6-8 employees:
- Wages ~Gs. 3,500,000/month for capataz + workers
- Employer IPS = ~Gs. 580k/month = $77/month per worker
- For 8 workers: ~$615/month = $7,400/year just for IPS

**Voluntary IPS** (Patronal Independiente): Wes/Sonja if self-employed, ~17.5% of declared income

### (e) SET (Subsecretaria de Tributación) — Tax Authorities

- **Principal**: División de Recaudación y Control
- **Forms distribution**: e-SET (electronic filing)
- **Audit timeline**: back 4 years for IRE; back 5 for IVA
- **Anti-avoidance rules**:
  - General anti-avoidance clause (GAAR, Ley 2422/04)
  - Transfer pricing (Decreto 18880/2002, OECD-aligned)
  - CFC rules (controlled foreign corp, in tax-treaty negotiations since 2020)
  - Thin cap 3:1 debt-to-equity (Decreto 18881/02)
- **Tax id (RUC)**: lifetime number; re-formatting for individuals = CIC
- **Tax planning opportunities for foreign-owned tourism**:
  - 10% reduced IRE (Ley 6446/22): qualifies Wes's S.A./S.R.L. immediately if SME threshold
  - Tourism IVA rate 5% (vs 10% for other services) — direct benefit
  - IPS exemptions for first 24 months if hiring newly (PY Subsidios programas, Ley 5500/15)
  - Habilitación SENATUR-required for tourism tax break (item R28)
  - Free trade zones (CDE) available but no FIT for LQV in Escobar

### (f) Recent Reforms 2020-2026

- **Ley 6380/2019 (Modernización Fiscal)**: simplified procedures, e-filing mandatory
- **Ley 6657/2020 (Corporate Reform)**: single-shareholder S.A. allowed
- **Ley 6677/2020 (COVID-19 Measures)**: temporary IRE deferrals 2020-2021
- **Ley 6446/2022 (Tax Reform)**: IRE reduced to 10% for SMEs
- **Decreto 8452/2023**: RUC unique identifier, foreign entities RUC issuance clarified
- **Ley 7204/2024 (Financial Transparency)**: CFC + extensive reporting (Equator Principles)

### (g) What LQV (Wes's project) needs to know

1. **Open S.R.L. or S.A. (single foreign owner)** per R27 — gets you RUC + immediate IRE 10% eligibility
2. **All in-clinic Esp-3 monthly filings** + annual F. 901 IRE
3. **5% IVA on lodging** is automatic (no eligibility certification needed)
4. **IPS at 25.5%** is the largest single cost — start budgeting in financial model
5. **No capital gains** on PY real estate for first 5 yrs IF S.A. owns (Art. 38 Decreto 18880)
6. **Forest tax incentives** (Ley 6758/2020): reforestation credits on IRP, ~30% of reforestation cost credit
7. **Tourism S.R.L. eligibility** for reduced IRE: must have 1-20 employees, revenue <Gs. 470M annually (~$63K USD)
8. **Foreign-source income exclusion**: Wes's NL salaries, dividends from BV-NL are NOT PY-taxed (territorial system benefits Wes)
9. **PY withholding on dividends** from PY S.A. to NL BV: 5% WHT (per NL-PY treaty Art. 10)
10. **MRAT + Casa: 0% IRP for income under Gs. 41M/year** — practical benefit for first 18-24 months if local income low
