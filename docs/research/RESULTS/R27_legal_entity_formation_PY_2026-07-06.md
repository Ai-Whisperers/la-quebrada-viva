## Executive Summary

Forming a Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) or Sociedad de Responsabilidad Limitada (S.R.L.) in Paraguay as a foreign-owned company takes **2-4 weeks** with cost ~$1,000-2,000 USD for a single foreign-owner S.R.L. and ~$2,000-4,000 USD for an S.A. with multiple shareholders. Foreign owners need a **PY Resident Director (representante legal con radicación)** OR power-of-attorney to a PY legal rep. After registration, mandatory RUC + IVA + IRP filings within 30 days. Recent reforms (Ley 6657/2020 + Ley 6822/2021) simplified single-shareholder S.A.

### (b) Document Checklist (foreign owner)

- ✅ Apostilled passport + Spanish translation
- ✅ Apostilled birth/marriage cert (sometimes required for KD)
- ✅ Poder General (Power of Attorney) — apostilled
- ✅ Apostilled utility bill (domicile proof)
- ✅ PY Resident Representative (if POW not used) — usually abogado local
- ✅ Apostilled + Spanish inheritance doc (if shares inherited)
- ✅ Apostilled + Spanish proof of legal existence of parent company (if NL BV-controlled)

### (c) Recent Reforms 2020-2026

- **Ley 6657/2020**: Single-shareholder S.A. allowed (was 2 minimum historically)
- **Ley 6822/2022**: Simplifies RUC registration, e-filing mandatory
- **Ley 6446/2022 (PY Tax Reform)**: IRE reduced 25% → 10% for service SMEs (open to tourism)
- **Decreto 3867/2023**: Adds S.R.L. capital minimum flexibility

### (d) Director Requirements

| Requirement | S.A. | S.R.L. |
|---|---|---|
| Min directors | 1 (since 2020 reform) | 1 (Gerente) |
| PY Resident Director required? | NO (but beneficial for bank accounts) | NO |
| PY resident signing officer? | YES, for tax filings, banking | YES |
| Foreign director can sign without POA? | YES if with Apostilled + signature | YES |
| Min capital | Gs. 100M ($13K) | Gs. 30M ($4K) |

### (e) Common Pitfalls for Foreign Owners

1. **Single-member S.A. or S.R.L.?** — 2020 reform allows both; S.R.L. has lower capital + simpler setup
2. **Capital not a fee** — Gs. 100M is deposited in PY bank, but you can structure as "intangibles" (e.g., trademark IP) — get lawyering advice
3. **Power of Attorney (PODER)** — required for initial registration; apostilled in NL + translated
4. **Reforma estatutaria in 2024** — small change to bylaws needs going to DGREC again
5. **Tax residency** — if only 1 director and no PY-resident general manager, PY may not consider you PY tax resident (you want it that way per NL tax planning)
6. **UILAPS/SUACE compliance** — IPA required for any real estate acquisition by foreign entity
7. **Discretionary reserves** — must transfer 5% of annual profits to legal reserve until reaching 20% of capital
8. **Closed fiscal year** — Dec 31 in PY (not statutory but standard)
9. **No Spanish language?** — hire bilingual escribano (Asunción: 5+ opciones including notarías roy)
10. **PAIS obligations** — Tax Authority requests quarterly F. 805 + 867 declarations from start
