## What is Anexo I

**Per CLOSING_DAY_PREP.md:**
> "Anexo I (technical descriptions: linderos, rumbos, medidas) is in Peña's hands — sellers were supposed to deliver ~6 May per Cl. OCTAVA (ii)."

In plain language: Anexo I is the legal document that describes each of the 6 fincas (parcels) in technical detail:
- **Linderos:** boundaries (which property borders which)
- **Rumbos:** directions (compass bearings of each boundary line)
- **Medidas:** measurements (area, perimeter in meters/hectares)

This is the document that proves the seller owns what they're selling, and exactly what is being sold. Without it, the escritura cannot be completed properly.
