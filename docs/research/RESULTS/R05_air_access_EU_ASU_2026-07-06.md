## Executive summary

EU travelers reach ASU primarily through **1-stop connections via Madrid (MAD)** or **São Paulo (GRU)**. Direct flights are limited: only **Air Europa operates nonstop MAD-ASU** (once weekly). No direct flights from AMS, FRA, CDG, or ZRH to ASU in 2026. Round-trip prices from EU in shoulder season: **£1,200-1,400 (~$1,500-1,800)** for 1-stop, €1,500-2,000 from MAD direct.

### Why ASU vs Buenos Aires (EZE) or São Paulo (GRU)?

- **ASU**: closer to LQV site (2hr to Escobar vs 18-20hr from EZE, 14-16hr from GRU + Paraguay border crossing). Fewer direct EU flights.
- **EZE**: more direct flights from EU (AMS, FRA, CDG, MAD all have nonstop), but PY entry requires land border from Argentina or domestic AR-PY flight.
- **GRU**: more direct flights, but PY entry requires border crossing from BR.

**Recommendation for LQV guests**: market fly-to-ASU, but offer transfer coordination. Most NL/DE guests will route through MAD (LATAM/Iberia partner) or GRU (LATAM/TAM).

### Airline options from EU (1-stop)

- **KLM + LATAM/KLM partner** (AMS via GRU or EZE)
- **Iberia + Air Europa** (MAD direct or 1-stop)
- **Lufthansa + LATAM** (FRA via GRU)
