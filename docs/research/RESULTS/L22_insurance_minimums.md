## Insurance coverage required for vacation rental

**By coverage type:**

| Type | What's covered | Typical limit | Annual cost USD |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Property (fire + storm + flood + vandalism)** | The buildings (30 cabins + restaurant + reception) | $1-2M aggregate | $8,000-15,000 |
| **General liability (3rd party injury)** | Guest injury, property damage to 3rd parties | $1-2M per occurrence | $2,000-4,000 |
| **Business interruption** | Lost revenue from covered claim (fire, storm) | 6-12 months revenue | $1,500-3,000 |
| **Workers' comp (IPS alternative)** | Employee injury on the job | statutory | $1,000-2,000 |
| **Vehicle** (Tundra/Hilux + kitchen delivery) | Collision + theft + fire | actual value | $500-800 |
| **Cyber** (booking system + guest data) | Liability for data breach | $500K-1M | $300-600 |
| **Umbrella** (excess liability) | Catastrophic events | $5M+ | $1,000-2,000 |
| **Workers' liability** (limited PY) | Often covered by IPS | — | (included in IPS) |

**Total annual insurance for RV Fase 1 (5 cabins + restaurant):**
- Property: $8,000-15,000
- Liability: $2,000-4,000
- Business interruption: $1,500-3,000
- Workers (IPS supplement): $1,000-2,000
- Vehicles: $500-800
- Cyber: $300-600
- Umbrella: $1,000-2,000
- **Total: $14,300-27,400/year** (averages $20K)

**For full Phase 1 (30 cabins + restaurant + amenities): $40-80K/year**

**International insurers (via Mercosur or LATAM):**
- Marsh (via Latam hub)
- Aon
- Willis Towers Watson
- Zurich

**PY insurance market reality:**
- Local insurers are limited in coverage types
- Fire insurance for rural properties is hard
- Storm coverage is standard but with high deductibles
- Specialty risks (eco-tourism, wellness pools) often not covered locally
- **International brokers (via BR or AR) are the answer for specialty coverage**

## Recommended approach

**Phase 1: use 1 international broker + 1 local broker**
- International broker: primary coverage (fire, storm, liability, business interruption)
- Local broker: backup for vehicles + workers comp

**Phase 2-3: consolidate to 1 international broker**
- Get multi-line package
- Single point of contact
- Better pricing at scale

## Coverage requirements by phase

**Phase 1 (Y1, 5 cabins):**
- Property coverage: $500K aggregate (per building)
- Liability: $1M per occurrence
- Vehicle coverage: 1 vehicle (the Hilux)
- Workers comp: per IPS + supplement

**Phase 2-3 (Y2-3, 15-30 cabins):**
- Property: $2-3M aggregate
- Liability: $2-5M per occurrence
- Multiple vehicles
- Business interruption: 12 months
- Event coverage: weddings + corporate retreats

**Phase 4 (Y3+):**
- Full coverage, including wedding liability
- Event cancellation insurance
- Cyber coverage expanded
