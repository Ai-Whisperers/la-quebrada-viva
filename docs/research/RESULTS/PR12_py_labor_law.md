## Summary

Paraguay's labor law (Código Laboral + Ley 5407/2015 for domestic workers) is **relatively protective** of workers compared to many Mercosur neighbors. Live-in domestic workers have specific protections (max 48 hr/week, paid overtime, 1 day off per week minimum, aguinaldo in Dec + Jun). **For RV's Phase 1 staffing**, expect 5-10 employees including 1-2 live-in roles (Sonja + Jos or Thijs). **Budget ~USD 500-700/month all-in per live-in employee** including benefits.

## Key Data Points

- **Standard work week:** 48 hours max (8 hours/day × 6 days, or 9.6 hours/day × 5 days)
- **Overtime:** Paid at 1.5x first 2 hours, 2x after
- **Aguinaldo (Christmas bonus):** 1/12 of annual salary, paid in Dec + Jun
- **Vacation:** 12 working days per year after 1 year of service (escalates to 30 days after 5 years)
- **Severance (preaviso + indemnización):** 1 month per year of service
- **Live-in domestic worker specific (Ley 5407/2015):**
  - Max 48 hours/week
  - 1 day off per week minimum (Sunday preferred)
  - Cannot be required to leave the house during off-hours
  - Separate room with bed, ventilation, lighting, closet required
  - Food provided separately from main family
  - Aguinaldo + vacation + IPS (public health) all mandatory
- **Cost breakdown for live-in worker (PY):**
  - Salary: $400-500/month
  - IPS (public health): $50-80/month (employer + employee)
  - Aguinaldo (2x/year): 1 month salary
  - Vacation: included
  - Food + housing: ~$100/month equivalent
  - **Total all-in: ~$600-700/month**
- **PY minimum wage (2024):** ~$420/month (above which is typical for hospitality)
- **Sonja questionnaire coverage:** Should include her specific situation (is she employee, partner, sub-contractor?)
