> **For Wes.** Activities + experiences to differentiate LQV from San Ber mid-market. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**LQV's experience differentiation** vs the San Bernardino hotel/hostel cluster:
- **Activities that PY visitors actually want** (per research): nature walks, birding, horseback riding, yoga/meditation, farm-to-table dining, indigenous cultural experiences, water-based (river/stream swimming)
- **LQV unique**: quebrada (stream) access + cob-built cultural anchor + Sonja's cultural leadership + hovenier (forester) trail design

**Pricing for premium experiences**: $30-150/person add-on. If 30% of guests book one experience, that's $300-500 incremental revenue per guest per night.
