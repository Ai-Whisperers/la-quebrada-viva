### Wildlife corridor (per EN08)

**For attracting + sustaining wildlife corridors through the 62 ha:**
- Continuous canopy (plant the canopy species in clusters, not lines)
- Food trees (fruiting species at every 50m: guavira, pitanga, aguay)
- Nest trees (native cavity-nesters prefer old growth — leave some dead trees)
- Water features (small ponds at low points = amphibian habitat)
