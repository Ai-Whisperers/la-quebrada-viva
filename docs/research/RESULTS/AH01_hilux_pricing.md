## What to check in a used Hilux

- Service history (every 10,000 km Toyota logbook)
- Frame rust (PY's rough roads + humidity)
- Engine + transmission (test for smooth shifting)
- 4WD function (low-range + diff lock)
- Body condition (dents, scratches = PY rough driving)
- Mileage vs condition (high km + low condition = bad sign)
- Title + lien check (no accident history, no repossession)
