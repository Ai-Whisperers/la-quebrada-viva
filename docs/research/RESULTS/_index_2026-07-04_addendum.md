## Top insights from this batch

1. **The cob/bamboo supply chain is real and local.** Both materials are available within 50 km of RV. No need to import.
2. **PY permit complexity is moderate, not severe.** Budget USD 15-25K for all Phase 1 permits; start 6 months before build.
3. **The primary market is EU expats in PY (30-50K pool), NOT EU tourists flying in.** This reframes the entire marketing strategy.
4. **San Bernardino is the supply + benchmarking anchor.** 45km away, established tourism infrastructure, German expat community.
5. **Construction costs are 20-25% inflated.** The repo's $180-250K Phase 1 estimate is correct but at the upper end.
6. **Insurance is a real gap.** PY market has limited eco-tourism capacity; expect 1.5-2x NL pricing.
7. **Climate change is the biggest long-term risk.** +0.5-1°C warming, more extreme events, drought frequency increasing.
