## Findings

PY has 4 viable structural timber categories for the 5-cabin Phase 1 build:

### 1. Eucalyptus (grandis) — Itapúa sawmills
- **Density:** ~500-600 kg/m³ (soft, but workable)
- **Source:** Itapúa department (southern PY, 200-300 km south of Asunción)
- **Pricing landed Escobar:** ~Gs. 350,000-450,000/m³ ($45-60/m³) for structural grade
- **Lead time:** 5-12 days from order
- **Durability:** MEDIUM — heartwood lasts 10-15 years externally if treated; sapwood rots in 3-5 years
- **Treatment:** Required for any outdoor use — borate soak (2 weeks) + drying (1 week)

### 2. Native hardwoods (lapacho, cedro, curupay, yukyta)
- **Density:** 800-1100 kg/m³ (very hard, durable)
- **Source:** Atlantic Forest remnants, native concessions; mostly Paraguayan Chaco (north/west)
- **Pricing landed Escobar:** ~Gs. 800,000-1,500,000/m³ ($105-200/m³)
- **Lead time:** 7-21 days; quota-limited by INFONA forestry permits
- **Durability:** HIGH — 25+ years externally untreated; century-class interior framing
- **Treatment:** None required for structural; optional oil finish

### 3. Imported pine (pino Paraná, pino Brasil)
- **Density:** 400-500 kg/m³ (softest)
- **Source:** Brazilian Paraná state, imported via CDE
- **Pricing landed Escobar:** ~Gs. 250,000-380,000/m³ ($33-50/m³)
- **Lead time:** 14-30 days (depends on CDE customs processing)
- **Durability:** LOW — 5-8 years externally, requires full CCA (chromated copper arsenate) treatment for any structural use
- **Treatment:** Mandatory for outdoor; CCA treatment adds $8-12/m³

### 4. Bamboo (Guadua angustifolia — chusquea + native)
- **Density:** ~600-700 kg/m³ (treated)
- **Source:** Misiones / Caaguazú departments; also Asian imports via CDE
- **Pricing landed:** ~Gs. 200,000-300,000/m³ ($26-40/m³) raw culms
- **Lead time:** 5-10 days for fresh harvest; 4 weeks for treated
- **Durability:** HIGH when treated; 25+ years. (Cross-ref: c02_bamboo_impregnation.md)
- **Treatment:** Mandatory — borate + boric acid soak (already mapped in research)

## Recommended Phase 1 mix

For 5 cabins × ~3-4 m³ structure/cabin = 15-20 m³ total structural timber:

- **60% eucalyptus (Itapúa):** structural framing, joists, rafters = 9-12 m³ × $50/m³ avg = **$450-600**
- **25% bamboo (Guadua):** roof purlin, accent features, porch columns = 4-5 m³ × $33/m³ = **$130-165**
- **15% native hardwood (lapacho/cedro):** door/window frames, accent floor, feature wall = 2-3 m³ × $150/m³ = **$300-450**
- **Total Phase 1 timber budget:** **$880-1,215** (excluding treatment labor)

## Climate fit (PY humid subtropical per WES_WARNINGS.md)

- **Eucalyptus + PY humidity:** good match if treated — borate soak covers 10+ year external life
- **Native hardwood + heat-rain cycles:** excellent — naturally resistant to fungi via tannin content
- **Pine + PY storms:** fails within 5 years due to rot unless kiln-dried + CCA-treated ($15-20/m³ extra)
- **Bamboo + PY humidity:** excellent for treated culms; untreated colonized by hongos within 18-24 months

## Key Risks

1. **Itapúa sawmill consistency** varies sawmill-to-sawmill; pine-mixed batches common — always request "pure eucalyptus" certification.
2. **INFONA quotas** for native hardwood fluctuate seasonally; reserve 60-90 days lead time for guaranteed supply.
3. **CDE import customs** can delay imported pine by 2-4 weeks during high-traffic periods (Mar-May post-recaudación).
4. **Bamboo treatment errors** are catastrophic: if borate soak time is cut short, structural failure within 5 years. Mandatory QC step.
5. **Eucalyptus grading** — sapwood vs heartwood matters (heartwood 60-70% of cross-section; rest is sapwood that MUST be trimmed for structural use).

## Recommendation

**Phase 1 (5 cabins) optimal procurement:**
1. **Lead:** order 12 m³ eucalyptus from **Itapúa sawmill A (e.g., Maderas Itapúa or Cisneros Group)** — confirm grading + heartwood ≥50%.
2. **Simultaneously:** order 5 m³ treated Guadua bamboo from **Caaguazú bamboo co-op (per c03)** — borate pre-treated, kiln-dried.
3. **Reserve:** order 3 m³ lapacho for premium accents (door frames + bench seating).
4. **Total:** ~20 m³ for ~$1,000-1,200 delivered + treated.
5. **Skip imported pine entirely** — its $33/m³ price advantage disappears under PY humidity.

**Phase 2+ optimization:** if project scales (10-30 cabins), consider direct partnership with **Mennonite colony** for guaranteed 30% cheaper supply (cross-ref: EN15_Mennonite_wood.md).
