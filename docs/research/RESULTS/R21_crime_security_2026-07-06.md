# R21 — Paraguay Crime + Security Assessment

> **For Wesley van de Camp + Ivan.** Sources: expatsettle.com (2026 guide), UNODC Global Study on Homicide 2023, US State Department, UK FCDO, Transparency International CPI 2024, as of 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**Paraguay is one of the SAFEST countries in South America** for foreign property owners. US State Department Level 1 advisory (Exercise Normal Precautions — same as Germany, UK, Canada). Homicide rate **6.2/100k** — comparable to the United States, lower than Brazil (21.1), Colombia (25.4), Uruguay (11.2), Chile (6.7). **Rural Paraguarí is very low crime** — main risks are petty theft in cities + road accidents (78% of fatalities are motorcycle-related). **Kidnapping/extortion targeting foreigners is rare** — no widespread phenomenon like in BR/CO. Foreign property owners are NOT specifically targeted.

### Areas to AVOID in Asunción

- **Chacarita (Dr. Ricardo Brugada)** — informal settlement near presidential palace, avoid entirely
- **Bañado Sur/Norte** — flood-prone informal settlements along the river
- **Centro at night** — empties after business hours, poor lighting, muggings reported
- **San Jerónimo** — safety declines after dark
- **Market 4 area** — pickpocketing hotspot

### Main crime types + prevention

| Crime type | Frequency in PY | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| **Petty theft (pickpocketing, phone snatching)** | **HIGHEST RISK** | Cross-body bag, no phone display, keep in pocket |

### Police contacts

- **911** — general emergency (police, fire, ambulance)
- **141** — SEME ambulance (free public service)
- **Comisaría** — local police station (every neighborhood)
- **Tourist Police** in Encarnación: +595 71-204102
- Response time to rural properties: **30-60 min** (longer for deep rural like Paraguarí)
- **Private security** recommended for any rural property >5 ha

### What foreign property owners do (Paraguarí / San Pedro / Caaguazú area)

- **Gated community or walled compound** with electric fence
- **24/7 portero or guard service** (Gs. 2-3M/month ≈ $300-400)
- **Perimeter cameras + alarm** ($1,500-3,000 install)
- **Safe room or panic room** in main house (rare, $5K-15K build)
- **Dogs** (recommended, 2+ large breed)
- **Vehicle GPS tracker** ($20-50/month)
- **Trusted local contact** (escribano, lawyer, or staff member)
- **No public display of wealth** on social media
- **Variation in routine** (don't leave/return at exact same times daily)
