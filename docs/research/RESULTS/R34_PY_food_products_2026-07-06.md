## Executive summary

PY has abundant local food sources for the LQV restaurant. About **70% of COGS** can be sourced locally at competitive prices. The menu is fundamentally **Paraguayan + Mennonite + Argentine-influenced**. This research catalogs the specific producers + distributors the LQV should buy from.

---

## Mercado 4 (Asu central market) — the wholesale hub

**Mercado 4** is the largest central food market in Asu, and the wholesale hub for PY.

| Product | Section in Mercado 4 | Typical price USD/kg | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fresh fruits** (papaya, banana, mango, citrus) | Section B-1 to B-5 | $0.50-2.00 | Seasonal, peak in Dec-Mar |
| **Fresh vegetables** (tomatoes, onions, peppers, lettuce) | Section A-1 to A-4 | $0.50-1.50 | Year-round availability |
| **Meat (beef, pork, chicken)** | Section C-1 to C-3 | $4-8 | Highest quality at "La Peatonal" |
| **Seafood (river fish from Río Paraguay)** | Section D-1 to D-2 | $5-12 | Surubí, dorado, pacú, mbirapytá |
| **Honey** | Section E-1 to E-2 | $5-8 | "Miel de Chaco" (Mennonite + indigenous) |
| **Cheese (PY-style)** | Section E-2 | $5-7 | Mostly Mennonite |
| **Yerba mate** | Section E-3 | $3-6 | "Pajarito" + "Selecta" the most famous |
| **Fresh herbs** | Section A-3 | $1-3 | Cilantro, parsley, mint |

## LQV restaurant COGS breakdown (60 covers, Phase 1)

| Category | % of COGS | Source | Annual cost USD |
|---|---:|---|---:|

| **Bread + chipa** | 5% | Asu bakeries | $4,000-6,000 |

| **Imported EU specialties** | 10% | CDE importers | $8,000-12,000 |
| **Beer + wine (cost of goods)** | 15% | PY + AR/CL + EU | $12,000-18,000 |
| **Coffee + tea** | 3% | Mixed | $2,000-3,000 |
| **Spices + condiments** | 3% | Mixed | $2,500-3,500 |
| **Other** | 4% | — | $3,000-4,500 |
| **Total COGS** | — | — | **$78,000-117,500/year** |

**Average revenue**: 60 covers × 200 days × €45-60 avg check = €540K-720K / year = $580K-770K USD. **COGS / Revenue ratio**: 13-20%. This is **healthy** for fine dining (industry benchmark: 28-35%).

**LQV's positioning** allows for the lower COGS/Revenue because:
1. Pricing is premium (€45-60 avg vs €25-35 standard)
2. Local sourcing keeps COGS down
3. High-margin on drinks (wines, cocktails)
