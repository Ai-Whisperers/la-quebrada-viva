# L28 — Capital gains tax (PY + NL) on property sale

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Important for RV exit strategy.

## What L28 is

**When Wes + Thijs sell RV (or part of it):**
- PY capital gains tax on the PY real estate (BV1)
- NL capital gains tax on the holding (BV2 equity)
- Total tax cost can be 10-30% of sale price

## PY capital gains tax (real estate)

**Per PY law:**
- 5% on gross sale price
- OR 15% on net gain (sale - acquisition - improvements)
- Whichever is higher
- No exemption for primary residence
- Can be deferred if proceeds are reinvested in PY real estate within 2 years

**For RV (sale at $5M):**
- 5% × $5M = **$250,000 in PY tax**
- Or 15% × $4M net = $600,000
- **Likely cost: $250K-$600K**

**Mitigations:**
- Hold for 5+ years (potential reduced rate)
- Reinvest proceeds in another PY property
- 1031-style exchange (not available in PY)
- **Avoid short-term flips**

## NL capital gains tax (BV2 equity)

**Per NL rules:**
- Box 1 (work income) or box 2 (substantial interest) depending on holding
- 26.9% on substantial interest capital gains
- Participates in normal income tax calculation

**For RV (Wes's equity in BV2, sold at $5M):**
- 26.9% × $4M gain (Wes's share) = **$1.08M in NL tax**
- Plus Wes's other income (box 1 + box 2)
- **Effective rate: 26.9% to 49.5% depending on brackets**

**Mitigations:**
- Holding period > 5 years: possible reduced rate
- Reinvestment in another BV2: deferral
- Timing of sale (low-income years): lower brackets
- **Strategic tax planning critical**

## Total tax burden (sale at $5M)

| Layer | Tax | USD |
|---|---:|---:|
| PY IRE (capital gains) | $250K-$600K | — |
| NL box 2 (Wes's share) | $1.08M (26.9% × $4M) | — |
| NL box 1+2 (Wes's personal, depending on other income) | +50% on excess | — |
| **Total effective tax** | **$1.3M-$1.7M** | — |
| **Net to Wes + Thijs** | **$3.3M-$3.7M** | on $5M sale |

**25-34% effective tax burden on exit.**

## Optimization strategies

**1. Hold long enough (>5 years):**
- PY may offer reduced rate for long-term holdings
- NL may offer some relief via holding period
- Speak to tax advisor in advance

**2. Reinvest in PY real estate:**
- If proceeds reinvested in another PY property, can defer PY tax
- Especially relevant if RV expands to RV-2 or related properties

**3. Sell in tranches:**
- Multiple smaller sales vs one large sale
- Each tranche may fall in lower tax bracket
- Year-by-year tax planning

**4. Sell as operating business (not real estate):**
- BV2 sells equity in BV3 (the operating business)
- Different tax treatment (capital gains on shares, not real estate)
- 5-10% potentially lower
- Complex; needs tax advisor

**5. Gift / inheritance (after death):**
- PY estate tax is 0% for first Gs. 350M of inheritance
- 5% for next tier
- Strategic for Wes's personal estate
- Not relevant for RV sale, but for end-of-life

**6. Family office structure:**
- Holdings via trust or family office
- Different tax treatment
- More complex; needs specialized advisor
