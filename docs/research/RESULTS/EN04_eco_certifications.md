# EN04 — Eco-certifications (GSTC, Rainforest Alliance, Bird-Friendly, Carbon)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Important for marketing and ESG narrative.

## What eco-certifications are available to LQV

**Global Sustainable Tourism Council (GSTC):**
- Industry-standard sustainability criteria
- For tour operators, accommodations, destinations
- 4 pillars: sustainable management, socio-economic, cultural, environmental
- Cost: $1,500-3,000 for assessment + $2,000-5,000/year ongoing
- Process: 6-12 months
- Marketing value: significant (international travelers recognize it)

**Rainforest Alliance Certified:**
- Focus on biodiversity + livelihoods
- For tourism, agriculture, forestry
- Cost: $2,000-5,000 for assessment + ongoing
- Process: 6-12 months
- Marketing value: high (eco-conscious travelers know it)

**Bird-Friendly certification:**
- From BirdLife International
- Specifically for properties with significant bird habitat
- For LQV: potential (Atlantic Forest has high biodiversity)
- Cost: $500-2,000
- Process: 3-6 months
- Marketing value: medium (birding community, eco-tourists)

**Carbon Neutral certification:**
- Measure + offset your carbon footprint
- Multiple verifiers (Climate Neutral Now, Climate Partner, etc.)
- Cost: $1,000-5,000/year (offset cost is the bigger item, $5-15K/year)
- Process: 6-12 months
- Marketing value: high (very current in EU/DE)

**Other options:**
- B-Corp (broader, harder to get)
- Travelife (sustainability for accommodations)
- TourCert (German certification, well-known in DE)
- Green Pearls (luxury sustainable)
- BREEAM (UK origin, broader sustainability)
- LEED (US, but buildings-focused)
