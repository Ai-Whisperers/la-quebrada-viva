## Recommendation

**Primary: Wise (www.wise.com)**
- 0.4-0.7% fee, best FX margin
- EUR → PYG direct
- Multi-currency account
- ACH to BV bank account possible
- Business account available for BVs

**Backup: Revolut Premium**
- Free transfers on Premium
- For smaller recurring transfers
- Need Premium tier ($9.99/month)

**Avoid: Traditional bank wires (ABN AMRO, ING, Itaú)**
- 1-3% margin eats €300-3,000 on a €30-100K transfer
- Slow
- Painful paperwork

## Setup steps for LQV

1. **Wes:** Open Wise account, verify ID, link NL bank
2. **Wes:** Open Revolut Premium if he doesn't have it already
3. **Wes:** Set up multi-currency wallet (EUR, USD, PYG)
4. **For each BV:** Open a business Wise account (for operational flexibility)
5. **Verify PY bank (Itaú) can receive from Wise** (most can, but verify for BVI/BVI2)
6. **Establish a monthly transfer schedule** (e.g. €5,000/month for operating costs)

## What LQV needs to do for investors
