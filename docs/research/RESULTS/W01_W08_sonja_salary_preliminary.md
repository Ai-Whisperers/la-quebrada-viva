## Official minimum wage (PY, 2026)

Per Decreto 6225 (MTESS):
- **Until June 2026:** Gs. 2,899,048/month (~$420 USD at 7,000 Gs/USD)
- **From July 2026:** Gs. 3,044,000/month (~$435 USD)

**Source:** https://finiquitojusto.com/derechos-laborales/guia-salario-minimo-paraguay-2026/

This is the **absolute floor** for any legal employment. Construction workers in practice earn 30-100% above this (depending on skill + region).
