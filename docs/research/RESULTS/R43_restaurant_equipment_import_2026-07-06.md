## Executive summary

PY has limited domestic kitchen equipment production, so most restaurant equipment is **imported**. The import process takes 4-8 weeks and costs 30-50% above US/EU prices (due to shipping + customs + duties). The PY "**régimen de turismo**" (tourism special import regime) offers **exemptions** for tourism-related capital investments.

---

## The "Régimen de Turismo" import regime

**PY Law 1.276/98 + updates** established the **Régimen de Turismo** for tourism operators. The benefits:

- **Exemption from import duties** (typically 10-20% on kitchen equipment)
- **Exemption from IVA** (10%) on imported equipment for hospitality use
- **Simplified customs process** via SENATUR
- **Applies to**: hotels, restaurants, tourism operators, conference centers

**Eligibility for LQV**:
- Must register with SENATUR first (per L21_SENATUR_classification.md)
- Must demonstrate the equipment is for tourism/hospitality use
- File a "Solicitud de Admisión" with SENATUR

**Cost saving**: typically 15-30% on imported equipment vs normal import regime.

**For LQV (Phase 1)**: SENATUR registration is required BEFORE the equipment import. Include in Phase 1 setup.

## Used equipment market (PY)

PY has a **vibrant used restaurant equipment market** (Asu). Many restaurants close + refurnish each year.

| Source | Used equipment cost (vs new) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Locanto Py** (online) | 30-60% of new | Mixed quality |
