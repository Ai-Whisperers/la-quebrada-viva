## Summary

In Paraguay, **construction permits are issued at the municipal level** (Municipalidad), with environmental permits from **MADES** (Ministerio del Ambiente y Desarrollo Sostenible) for projects affecting natural areas. For rural land in Escobar district, the permit process involves: (1) Municipal construction permit, (2) MADES environmental impact assessment (if >1,000 m² or affecting watershed), (3) ANDE electrical approval (if connecting to grid), (4) ESSAP water approval (if connecting to municipal water — likely not applicable to RV).

**Critical finding:** PY's rural construction permit process is **less standardized than in urban areas**. Most rural homes (including cob + bahareque) are built without formal permits, especially in the Chaco. **This is risky for an investor-facing eco-park.** RV should formalize all permits from Day 1.

## Key Data Points

- **Primary authority:** Municipalidad de Escobar (local municipal government)
- **Secondary authorities:** MADES (environment), ANDE (electricity), MOPC (roads if any new construction)
- **Process for rural construction permit (Municipalidad):**
  1. Land title + RUC (tax ID)
  2. Architect/engineer plans (required for >500 m² total)
  3. Site plan + access plan
  4. Payment of municipal fee (~0.5-1% of declared construction cost)
  5. Issuance (typically 30-60 days if complete)
- **MADES process** (if environmental impact):
  - Required for: developments >1,000 m², deforestation >5 ha, water extraction >50,000 L/day, wetland disturbance
  - Process: 90-180 days, includes EIA (Estudio de Impacto Ambiental)
  - Cost: USD 5,000-15,000 for a full EIA
- **ANDE approval:** Required for any new grid connection or upgrade (trifasica)
- **Building code:** Paraguay lacks a comprehensive building code; **engineer's stamp is the de facto standard**
