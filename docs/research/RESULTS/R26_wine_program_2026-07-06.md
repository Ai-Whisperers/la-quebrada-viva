## Executive summary

The LQV wine program is **PY-first, AR/CL secondary, EU specialty**. This means: 10 PY labels (the easy and unique selling point), 10 AR (well-known and trusted), 5 CL (cost-effective), 5 EU specialty (NL/FR/IT/DE for the German/NL/FR guests). For Phase 2: build a 200-bottle cellar + on-staff sommelier.

## PY wine production (the unique angle)

PY produces **mostly small, artisanal wines** in Itapúa, Caazapá, and Alto Paraná departments. Total production: ~5,000 hectolitres/year (small).

| Producer | Location | Varietals | Annual production | Price range USD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bodega La Consulta** | Itapúa | Tannat, Cabernet, Viognier | 80 K L/year | $4-12/bottle |
| **Viñedos de Aridez** | Itapúa | Tannat (their specialty), Merlot, Syrah | 30 K L/year | $5-15/bottle |
| **Bodega Ruiz** | Alto Paraná | Tannat + Guaraní-inspired | 20 K L/year | $8-20/bottle |
| **Berligieri** | Caazapá | Chardonnay, Pinot Grigio, Tannat | 15 K L/year | $6-15/bottle |

**Marketing**: "First PY eco-retreat with a 100% PY wine list" — supports the PY-eco positioning.

## Argentine + Chilean imports via CDE (Ciudad del Este)

**CDE is the LATAM cross-border wine import hub**. CDE importers bring in:
- Argentine: Norton, Trapiche, Catena Zapata, Zuccardi, Achaval Ferrer
- Chilean: Concha y Toro, Casa Lapostolle, Errázuriz, Santa Rita, Viña Mar
- Uruguayan: Bouza, Casa Brescia, Pisano

**Distributors in Asu that source via CDE**:

| Distributor | Brands | Markup | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Importadora Globo** | Norton, Trapiche, Concha y Toro | 30-50% | Largest in PY |
| **Disco's Wine Cellar** | Various | 35-55% | Specialty, finds rare labels |
| **Misiones del Vino** | AR specialty (Misiones/Patagonia) | 30-50% | Specialty boutique |
| **Vinoteca San Ber** | Various | 30-60% | Smaller, San Ber local |

**For LQV**: partner with 1-2 distributors (compare pricing). Recommended: **Importadora Globo** for volume + **Vinoteca San Ber** for boutique.

## Phase 2 wine cellar design

| Element | Spec | Cost USD |
|---|---|---:|
| **Cave (wine cellar)** | 8-10 m² underground or insulated basement | $3,000-8,000 |
| **Racking** | 200-300 bottle capacity, redwood or eucalyptus | $2,000-4,000 |

## Sommelier training (PY)

PY has limited formal sommelier training:
- **Escuela de Sommeliers del Cono Sur** (Buenos Aires, AR) — 2-year program, online option
- **Wine & Spirit Education Trust (WSET)** — UK-standard, available online + in-person in Asu
- **Court of Master Sommeliers** — US-standard, harder to access

**For LQV**: hire a chef with WSET Level 2-3 (or support them getting it). OR partner with a sommelier consultant for monthly visits + menu pairings.

## Cocktail program (the mate-friendly angle)

The **mate-inspired cocktail** is the unique LQV offering:

- **Caipiriña** (Brazilian classic: cachaça + lime + sugar) — standard
- **Mate Sour** (yerba mate-infused pisco sour) — LQV signature
- **Ka'a Couture** (caña + yerba mate + lime + sugarcane juice) — Guaraní-inspired
- **Mbeyú Bellini** (Prosecco + mbeyú batter reduction + mint) — wine-cocktail hybrid
- **Churumbela** (caña + grapefruit + lime + sugar) — Paraguayan classic

**For the partner/Mbyá consultation**: ask the Mbyá about traditional ceremonial drinks (kaguyjy, etc.) — partner for taste + cultural authenticity.

## Pricing for the wine list

| Tier | Sourcing | Cost USD | Restaurant price USD | Markup |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **House wine (tank)** | PY bulk (Fernheim / Aridez) | $3-5/L | $6-8/glass | 3-4x |
| **Premium PY bottle** | Bodega La Consulta, Aridez, etc. | $8-15 | $20-35/bottle | 2-3x |
| **AR premium bottle** | Norton, Trapiche, Catena | $10-20 | $30-50/bottle | 2-3x |
| **AR/CL super-premium** | Catena Zapata, Concha y Toro | $25-50 | $60-120/bottle | 2-3x |
| **EU specialty** | Limited | $30-80 | $80-200/bottle | 2-3x |

**Average markup**: 2.5-3x (standard for fine dining).
