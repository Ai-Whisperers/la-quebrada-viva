# R-LQV02 — Cob + Earthen Construction Materials + Suppliers

> **For Wes + LQV cob cabin builders.** Deep dive into cob material supply chain. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY cob material supply chain** is **underdeveloped** vs US/UK. Wes's cob cabin will rely on:
1. **Subsoil from site** (free) — must be tested (clay+sand+gravel composition)
2. **Sand** (delivered, Gs. 80-120K/m³ ~$11-16 USD)
3. **Straw** (Gs. 50-80K/bale ~$7-11 USD)
4. **Lime plaster** (imported, Gs. 30-50K/kg ~$4-7 USD per kg)

**Total material per cabin** (35 m³ cob + 800 kg lime + 4 bales straw): $1,500-2,500 USD. Labor is the bigger cost ($3,800-5,500 USD per cabin).

## 14 data points

### Subsoil (clay-sand-gravel mix from site)

1. **Testing requirement**: simple lab test (Gs. 100-300K per test, ~$13-40 USD). Measures clay %, sand %, gravel %
2. **Optimal cob mix**: 20-30% clay, 70-80% sand, water + straw. PY soils vary widely
3. **Source**: must come from site or nearby. Need a "borrow pit" agreement with landowner + permit
4. **Quality concern**: topsoil has organic matter (will shrink). Need subsoil at 0.5-1.5m depth
5. **Volume needed per cabin**: 35 m³ = ~50 cubic yards

### Sand (delivered)

6. **Source**: most streams in Paraguarí have river sand. Commercial sand from Caaguazú or Cordillera
7. **Price**: Gs. 80-120K per m³ delivered (~$11-16 USD). Quality: washed river sand > pit sand
8. **Truck size**: typical PY truck carries 6-8 m³. 5 cabins × 35 m³ = 175 m³ total sand needed

### Straw

9. **Source**: wheat straw from Alto Paraná or Misiones. Less available locally near Escobar
10. **Price**: Gs. 50-80K per bale (~25 kg, $7-11 USD)
11. **Need per cabin**: 4 bales × 5 cabins = 20 bales ($140-220 USD total)
12. **Alternative**: sisal fiber, sugarcane bagasse (PY has both)

### Lime plaster (exterior + interior)

13. **Source**: imported (BR, AR). No significant PY producer
14. **Brand examples**: Secil Portugal (imported), Hidrófuga AR. Brands: "Cal Natural", "Cal Hidratada"
15. **Price**: Gs. 30-50K per kg ($4-7 USD per kg). Need ~1,000 kg per cabin (interior 500 + exterior 500)

### Other materials

16. **Foundation stone**: Piribebuy sandstone Gs. 35-50K/m³ (cheapest)
17. **Wood for doors/windows**: Eucalyptus Itapúa $45-60/m³
18. **Roofing**: metal galv. trapezoidal Gs. 25-35K/m² installed

## Sourcing strategy

- **Bulk sand + straw contract**: from Paraguari-area supplier, 30-day terms
- **Lime plaster**: order 5 tons in advance from BR distributor (4-6 week lead)
- **Subsoil**: negotiate borrow pit agreement with adjacent landowner + Municipalidad permit
- **Storage**: build covered material storage on site before construction starts
