# WP01 — Wes + Thijs partnership agreement (formal)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Personal partnership doc for Wes + Thijs.

## What WP01 is

**Wes (75%) and Thijs (25%) are co-owners of LQV via escritura (per 2026-06-27).**
**Per escritura + D10 (personal success metric) + D9 (4-BV vs hybrid):**
- The partnership needs formal documentation
- Covers: profit split, decision authority, dispute resolution, exit
- Critical for: 4-BV cascade, investor relations, family succession

## What the partnership agreement should cover

**1. Ownership:**
- 75% Wes, 25% Thijs (per escritura)
- Capital contributions to date
- Future capital call obligations
- Dilution on new investment

**2. Decision-making authority:**
- Major decisions (>$10K): both partners agree
- Operational decisions (<$10K): any partner
- Tiebreaker: Wes + arbitration if needed
- Specific authorities: Wes (technical/AI), Thijs (financial), Sonja (cultural)

**3. Profit distribution:**
- 75/25 split on all distributions
- Tax-efficient: through BV2 (NL holding)
- Reinvestment vs distribution decision
- Annual or quarterly

**4. Roles + responsibilities:**
- Wes: technical vision, AI leverage, NL network, investor relations
- Thijs: financial, financial planning, NL tax
- Sonja: cultural, local network, operational anchor

**5. Capital calls:**
- Both partners fund proportionally
- If one can't fund, dilutes the other
- Default: Wes + Thijs each contribute up to $X/year as needed
- Maximum: 2x annual

**6. Exit provisions:**
- One partner can buy out the other at fair market value
- Price: 3rd party valuation
- Timeline: 6-12 months to complete
- Right of first refusal (other partner has first option)
- Vesting: full ownership after 5 years

**7. Dispute resolution:**
- Mediation first (informal)
- Arbitration (3rd party)
- NL courts (for international aspects) or PY courts (for local)
- Final binding: arbitration

**8. Death + disability:**
- Buy-sell agreement (other partner buys out at fair value)
- Insurance: $1-2M life insurance on each
- Trust structure for inheritance

**9. Tax planning:**
- NL box 2 vs box 3 (per L33)
- 4-BV cascade structure (per 4ENTITY_BV_CASCADE)
- Family office for higher wealth
- Sonja's 60th + estate planning

**10. Confidentiality + non-compete:**
- LQV business info confidential
- No competing eco-lodge in PY within X years
- Penalty for breach

**11. Sonja's role:**
- Not a partner but essential to operations
- Defined: cultural compass, local network
- Compensation: salary (per W01-W08) + equity grant (per L32)

**12. Insurance + key-person:**
- Wes + Thijs: life insurance (per WP01)
- Sonja: key-person insurance (per L32)
- Disability insurance

**13. Modifications:**
- Annual review
- Unanimous written consent for major changes
- Quarterly business review

**14. Dissolution:**
- Asset division: 75/25
- Debt division: 75/25
- Operating business transfer: defined
- Land: held in BV1, continues as JV or sold
