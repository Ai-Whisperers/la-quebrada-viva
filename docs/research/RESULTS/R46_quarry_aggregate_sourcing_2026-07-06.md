## Sand sources (specific)

Sand is the **most-used material** for cob + plaster + concrete. PY has 3 main sand sources:

1. **River sand (washed)** — Best quality. From Río Paraguay or Río Paraná. Cost: $8-12/m³. Delivery: $30-50 extra to Escobar.
2. **Crushed stone sand (artificial)** — From limestone/dolomite quarries. Cost: $6-10/m³. Consistent quality.
3. **Plastering sand (fine)** — Washed river sand or quarry sand, sieved. Cost: $10-15/m³. For lime plaster.

**For LQV cob construction**: **washed river sand** is preferred (cleaner + more binder surface). Budget for ~$40/m³ delivered to LQV.
