## Context for the outreach

Wes is developing a 62-ha eco-retreat in Escobar, Paraguarí, Paraguay. Phase 1 = 5 cabins (~70 m² each, cob construction). Wes needs:

- Per-cabin cost quote (material + labor)
- Crew availability for Q4 2026 - Q1 2027 build window
- Reference projects (similar scale)
- Approach to PY climate (humidity, rainy season May-Sep)
- Engineering sign-off process (foundation specs, compression tests)

The 2 primary contacts per research:

1. **Roberto Abente** (Asunción) — most active cob/earthen builder in PY, ~20 small projects 2018-2026
2. **Cooperativa Ñandutí** (Caaguazú, possibly Itauguá based on web) — 5-6 trained cob artisans, organized labor pool

**Caveat from research**: only ~15-20 cob-trained builders in PY nationwide. Tight market. If Roberto isn't available, Cooperativa Ñandutí is the backup.

### Compare quotes
- Per-cabin cost (€ target: €18,200 / $19,500 / Gs. 146M per the M_COB_01 estimate)
- Crew size + composition
- Schedule commitment
- Engineering sign-off included?
- Insurance (¿ellos tienen RC o necesita Gaby/Wes contratar aparte?)

### Decision trigger
- If both quote < €20K/cabin: pick Roberto (most experienced, more flexibility)
- If Roberto > €25K/cabin: try Cooperativa Ñandutí (typically 20-30% cheaper, organized labor pool)
- If neither < €30K/cabin: reconsider cob as primary material (timber-frame might be more cost-effective at that price point)

---

## Red flags to watch for

- Quote significantly below €15K/cabin (probably missing labor cost or contingency)
- No mention of PY climate-specific construction details
- No engineering sign-off process (cob needs compression testing)
- Long lead time (>6 months) — suggests they're booked up
- No PY references (might be a new entrant without cob experience)
