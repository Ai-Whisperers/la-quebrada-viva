## Executive summary

**LQV's €220-300 ADR baseline is well-positioned** in the LATAM eco-retreat market. Below mid-tier Awasi Iguazú ($1,500-2,500/night) and well above mid-range Paraguayan properties (€100-200/night). The target market is the "premium-but-not-luxury" tier, with strong value-for-money positioning due to the all-inclusive F&B + wellness program.

## LQV pricing strategy

**Phase 1 (5 cabins)**:
- **Peak (Dec-Feb)**: €280-320/night
- **Shoulder (Sep-Nov, Mar)**: €220-260/night
- **Low (May-Aug)**: €180-220/night
- **Average**: €230-270/night

**Year 3 (30 cabins, established brand)**:
- **Peak**: €350-400/night
- **Average**: €290-340/night

**This is consistent with**:
- 25% below Awasi Iguazú positioning (LQV doesn't have Awasi's brand recognition yet)
- 30-50% above standard PY eco-lodges (LQV's positioning is premium)
- Comparable to mid-tier Iberá/Pampas estancias in AR

## What this means for the financial model

The FINANCIAL_MODEL_2026-07-06.xlsx Sensitivity sheet shows break-even at ~25% occupancy at €220 ADR. **Pricing 30% higher at €290 + same 25% occupancy = ~3.5x higher revenue**. But pricing above €350 will hurt occupancy (Law of Demand).

**Recommended pricing strategy**: €220 baseline, €290 peak, with **occupancy targets**:
- Year 1: 25% (low), 35% (good)
- Year 3: 35% (low), 50% (good)
