## L15 — Banking for B2B + dual PYG/USD accounts

### What LQV needs
- PY business account for LQV BV (likely the Fase 1 BV)
- Dual PYG + USD account (for international revenue)
- Wire transfer capability (for investors + payouts)
- Online banking + API access
- Card acceptance (links to L14)

### Bank options in PY (2026)

| Bank | Strengths | Weaknesses | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Banco Itaú Paraguay** | Largest bank, full digital, USD account, B2B focus | Higher fees | Primary operational account |
| **Banco Continental** | Strong local presence, lower fees | Less digital | Secondary account |
| **Banco Familiar** | Family-friendly, good service | Smaller scale | Personal account (Wes+Thijs) |
| **Banco GNB** | Good for business, lower fees | Less international | Backup |
| **Banco Familiar + Sudameris** | Local SME focused | Limited international | Personal accounts |

### Recommendation
- **Primary operational account:** Banco Itaú Paraguay (BV bank account)
- **Personal account (Wes+Thijs):** Banco Familiar or Continental
- **Wire transfer to NL:** use Itaú (best international wires in PY)

### What to bring when opening BV account
- RUC (tax ID) — see L08
- Escritura de constitución (need lawyer for this)
- BV's RUC + powers of attorney for Wes/Thijs
- Initial capital deposit (typically Gs. 5-10M for BV)
- Personal IDs of all directors (Wes, Thijs, any NL directors)

### Cost
- Account maintenance: Gs. 50,000-200,000/month
- Wire transfer out: Gs. 150,000-500,000 per wire
- USD account: Gs. 100,000-200,000/month additional

**Total operational banking: ~$1,500-2,500/year**

## L16 — Billeteras móviles (Tigo Money, Personal Pay) for staff + tips

### What they are
- Tigo Money = Tigo's mobile wallet, used by ~30% of PY unbanked
- Personal Pay = Telecom Argentina's wallet in PY
- Zimple, Wally, Billetera Personal = newer entrants
- All work via QR code + phone number transfers
- Most PY staff (especially in rural areas) prefer these over bank accounts

### For LQV
- **Staff wages:** 5-7 of 7 workers in Fase 1 may prefer billeteras móviles over bank accounts
- **Tips:** restaurant/bar tips are 90% in cash or wallet
- **Guests:** some EU guests use wallets too (Revolut, N26)

### Tigo Money (recommended primary)
- Free to receive
- Low transfer fees (1-2% or fixed small fee)
- Works for staff without bank accounts
- QR code acceptance at the property
- Tigo personal SIM = Tigo Money automatically

### Personal Pay (recommended secondary)
- Same general approach
- Slightly better international support
- Linked to Personal cell plan

### Cost for LQV
- Receiving payments: free
- Sending to staff wages: free or 1-2% fee
- Customer tips via QR: free
- Setup: zero (apps, link to phone number)

**Total: ~$0/year** (this saves LQV money on wages and tips vs card fees)

## L17 — FX transfer costs NL → PY (Wes + Thijs remitting)

### What LQV needs
- Wes (NL) needs to transfer EUR to PY BV account regularly
- Thijs may also transfer from NL accounts
- Investors may also wire from NL

### Options (2026, ranked by cost)

| Service | Fee | FX margin | Speed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wise (formerly TransferWise)** | 0.4-0.7% | mid-market + 0.05% | 1-2 days | Best for small-amount recurring |
| **Revolut** | 0% (free tier) or 0.5% (premium) | mid-market (weekday) | same day | Good for regular transfers |
| **N26 (DE)** | 0% (Premium) | mid-market | 1-2 days | Free if Wes/Thijs use N26 |
| **Traditional bank wire** | €15-50 flat | 1-3% margin | 2-5 days | Worst option |
| **Crypto (USDT)** | 0.1-0.5% | volatile | minutes | For Wes-savvy transfers only |
| **Cash carry on flights** | 0% (but insurance risk) | n/a | instant | For small amounts only |

### Recommendation
- **Primary:** Wise (best overall for EUR → PYG/USD transfers)
- **Backup:** Revolut (if Wes already uses it for personal)
- **Avoid:** Traditional bank wires (poor FX margins, high fees)

### Cost for LQV
**Assume €50K/year in transfers (Fase 1 + investors):**
- Wise: €50K × 0.6% = **€300/year in fees**
- vs traditional bank: €50K × 2.5% = **€1,250/year in fees**
- **Savings: ~€950/year by using Wise over bank wires**
