# EN15 — PY wood + reclaimed wood from Mennonite colonies

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Cost-effective alternative materials research.

## What RV could source

**Per D6 (natural building) + M11 (paint) + M15 (cabin materials):**
- The build materials can include PY-grown wood + reclaimed wood from Mennonite colonies
- This is **separate from the cob/bottle/bamboo** main RV aesthetic
- This is for interior furniture, deck wood, accent features

## What PY produces

**Hardwoods (high quality, suitable for furniture + structure):**
- **Lapacho negro** (Handroanthus heptaphyllus): premium, very hard
- **Lapacho amarillo** (Handroanthus impetiginosus): premium, hard
- **Yvyraro** (Pterodon emarginatus): very hard, durable
- **Cedro** (Cedrela fissilis): classic, premium
- **Guatambu** (Balfourodendron riedelianum): very hard, common

**Mennonite colonies' wood products:**
- Loma Plata: large timber operations, primarily agricultural (Mennonites grow and process their own wood)
- Filadelfia: similar
- **Reclaimed wood from demolished buildings:** increasingly popular in PY (older Mennonite structures are 30-50 years old, often being replaced)
- **Salvaged hardwoods from tree-removal operations:** sustainable + cost-effective

## Cost comparison

| Source | Cost (per m³) | Quality | Sustainability |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Lapacho (new) | Gs. 3,000,000-5,000,000 | Premium | Some plantations |
| Reclaimed Mennonite hardwood | Gs. 800,000-1,500,000 | Same as new | High (recycled) |
| Salvaged wood | Gs. 500,000-1,000,000 | Variable | Highest |
| Imported ipe (BR) | Gs. 4,000,000-6,000,000 | Premium | Lower (transport) |
| Treated pine (PY) | Gs. 800,000-1,200,000 | Mid | Mid |

**For RV's use cases:**
- Reclaimed Mennonite hardwood: 60-70% cost savings vs new
- Quality is equal (often better — older trees were slower-grown)
- Sustainability story: "we saved this wood from a landfill"
- Marketing: "authentic, local, sustainable"

## What RV could use it for

**Phase 1 (Y1, 5 cabins):**
- Floor (per M10): reclaimed hardwood flooring
- Decking: reclaimed hardwood
- Furniture: reclaimed + new mixed
- Window frames: per M05 (mostly aluminum + glass)
- Doors: reclaimed hardwood doors from Mennonite demolitions
- Cabinetry: local hardwood (lapacho, guatambu)

**Per cabin (5 cabins):**
- 1,000 m² reclaimed hardwood flooring: $2,000-4,000/cabin = $10-20K total
- 200m² decking: $1,000-2,000/cabin = $5-10K total
- 5-10 doors from reclaimed: $500-1,500/cabin = $2.5-7.5K total
- Custom furniture: $1,000-3,000/cabin = $5-15K total
- **Total per cabin: $20-45K in reclaimed wood**
- **Fase 1 total: $100-225K**

Compare to new wood (M04 + M15 + others): $250-450K. **Savings: $50-225K** (20-50%).

## Where to source

**1. Mennonite colonies (Loma Plata, Filadelfia):**
- 2-3 hour drive from Asunción
- Direct from colonies
- Best prices
- Need to visit + build relationship

**2. Demolition contractors (Asunción):**
- Often have reclaimed wood for sale
- Smaller quantities
- Variable quality

**3. Mercado 4 (Asunción, weekends):**
- Smaller pieces (furniture, decor)
- Variable
- Good for small items

**4. WENNER HOLZ or similar PY importers:**
- Imported European-style wood
- Premium
- Not local

**5. RV's own property (selective harvest):**
- Could selectively harvest a few trees from the property itself
- But environmental permits needed
- Not recommended for Fase 1
