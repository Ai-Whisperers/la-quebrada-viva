## Pool design (per D6 in DREAMLIST_NL)

- **Type:** Natural water, no chlorine
- **Water source:** Rain-fed (cistern + roof collection)
- **Treatment:** Reed-bed filtration + UV sterilization (no chemical)
- **Size:** 1-2 pools, ~50-80m² surface each (4-6m × 10-15m)

## Equipment needed

### 1. Pump system

**Natural pool pumps are different from chlorinated pools:**
- Lower flow rate (slower circulation)
- Higher head pressure (deeper, sometimes larger filter)
- Should be variable-speed (energy efficient)
- **Recommended:** 1-2 HP variable-speed pump

**Brands in PY:**
- Pentair (US, available via importers)
- Hayward (US, available)
- DAB (IT, available)
- Local Chinese (cheap, lower reliability)

**Cost for LQV:**
- 1-2 HP variable-speed pump: $400-800
- Installation: $100-200
- **Total:** **$500-1,000 per pool**

### 2. Filter system (natural pool)

**Natural pools use biological filtration, not sand/DE filters:**

**Components:**
- **Skimmer box** (collects surface debris): $50-100
- **Pump basket pre-filter:** $20-50
- **Biological filter zone** (planted area, ~30% of pool surface): built into pool design
- **UV sterilizer** (kills pathogens, no chemicals): $300-600
- **Sand pre-filter** (for sediment): $200-400
- **Skimmer net + skimmer basket**: $30-80
- **Return jets** (4-6 per pool): $20-40 each = $100-200

**Total filtration system:** **$700-1,400 per pool**

### 3. Plumbing (PVC + fittings)

**For natural pool circulation:**
- 2" PVC pipe (50-100m): Gs. 15,000-25,000/m = $2-3.50/m
- Fittings (elbows, T's, valves): $100-300
- **Total plumbing:** $200-500 per pool

### 4. Liner (or natural bottom)

**Options:**
- **EPDM rubber liner** (most common for natural pools): $8-15/m²
- **Bentonite clay** (natural waterproofing, more traditional): $5-10/m²
- **Concrete shell + waterproof plaster**: $15-25/m²
- **Natural pond bottom** (clay-rich soil, no liner): $0 but high risk of leaks

**Recommended:** EPDM rubber liner (most reliable). 50m² pool = **$400-750**

### 5. Plants (the bio-filter)

**Reed-bed plants for natural filtration:**
- **Common reed (Phragmites australis):** 4-6 plants per m²
- **Water mint (Mentha aquatica):** 1-2 per m²
- **Cattail (Typha):** 1 per m²
- **Pickerel weed (Pontederia):** 1 per m²

**Cost:** $200-500 for 50m² worth of plants + substrate (gravel + sand)

### 6. UV sterilizer (key for no-chlorine)

**Recommended:** Low-pressure UV lamp
- Flow rate: matches pump capacity
- Lifespan: 8,000-12,000 hours (~1 year continuous)
- Cost: $300-600 + $50-100/year for replacement lamps

**Why this is critical:** Without UV (and without chlorine), pathogens can grow. UV is the single most important "insurance" piece for no-chlorine natural pools.

### 7. Water testing kit

**Need:** Weekly testing for pH, bacteria (E. coli, coliforms), clarity
- **Pool test kit (basic):** $30-50
- **Lab testing (bacteria):** $50-100 per test, monthly recommended
- **Annual cost:** $700-1,200 (lab tests + consumables)

## Comparison to conventional chlorine pool

| Aspect | Chlorine pool | Natural pool (LQV choice) |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment cost | $1,000-2,000 (filter) + chemicals | $2,000-4,000 (biological + UV) |
| Operating cost/year | $500-1,000 (chlorine, acid) | $700-1,300 (UV lamps, tests) |
| Maintenance | Weekly chemical testing + dosing | Weekly bacteria testing + plant care |
| User experience | Chlorine smell, dry skin, eye irritation | Natural water, no smell, healthier |
| Marketing | "Swimming pool" | "Natural swimming pond" (much higher perceived value) |
| Wes's design fit | Standard | Matches LQV aesthetic perfectly |

**LQV's natural pool is 2-3x the build cost but:**
- 0 chemical smell = better for weddings + wellness retreats
- "Natural swimming" = higher nightly rate ($220+ for 2p Luxe Spa)
- Marketing differentiator (rare in PY)
- Better for the "eco" positioning

**ROI:** If the natural pool adds $20-30/night to a Luxe Spa cabin × 180 nights/year × 7 Luxe Spa cabins = $25-37K/year additional revenue. Pays for the pool in 2-4 months.
