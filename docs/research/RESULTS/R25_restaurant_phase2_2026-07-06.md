> **For Wesley van de Camp.** Generated 2026-07-06 by Erebus. Builds on `D6_wellness_pool.md` + `M22_kitchen_equipment_import.md` + `R24_chef_recruitment_2026-07-06.md`.

## Executive summary

**Phase 2 expansion** from 60 → 120 covers is **Year 2-3 work**. The expansion requires:
- 2× kitchen capacity (equipment + ventilation + cold storage)
- Additional seating (covered deck + indoor dining)
- Wine cellar / beverage program
- Possibly outdoor pizza oven + grill
- 2-3 additional FOH staff

**Cost estimate**: $80,000-150,000 USD for the expansion (depending on equipment quality).

## Permits needed for the expansion

| Permit | Cost USD | Time | Authority |

| **Fire safety re-inspection** | $500 | 1 month | Municipalidad |
| **Municipalidad commercial permit amendment** | $1,000 | 1 month | Municipalidad |
| **SENATUR reclassification** (rural → hotel if going >60 covers) | $2,000 | 1-2 months | SENATUR |
| **Environmental impact study update** (if kitchen expansion >1,000 m²) | $3,000 | 3-6 months | MADES |
| **Infona forest clearing permit** (if expansion touches the 32 ha "secondary" forest) | $500 | 1-2 months | INFONA |

**Total permit cost**: ~$8,500 + 6-12 months (parallel where possible)

## Design considerations

**Option A: Indoor expansion (extend the restaurant shell by ~40 m²)**

- Adds 20 indoor covers
- Cost: $15,000-25,000
- Weather-protected year-round
- Simple permit
- Cons: still feels "indoor"

**Option B: Outdoor deck (40 m² eucalyptus platform + covered roof)**

- Adds 30-40 outdoor covers
- Cost: $25,000-40,000
- Forest views, quebrada soundscape
- Marketing gold (the jungle dining experience)
- Cons: weather-dependent, 6-9 month wet season impact

**Option C: Hybrid — small indoor extension + outdoor deck**

- 20 indoor + 30 outdoor = 50 additional covers
- Cost: $35,000-50,000
- Best of both worlds
- Recommended
