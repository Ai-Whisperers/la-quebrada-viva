## 14 data points

1. **SENATUR tourism modernization grants** — co-funds up to 50% of eco-tourism infrastructure. Max grant ~$20-50K USD per project
2. **MADES (Environment) reforestation incentive** — pay-per-hectare for native tree planting on degraded land. LQV qualifies (62 ha with cleared areas)
3. **INFONA forest management grants** — for sustainable forestry plans
4. **SNP (Secretaria Nacional de Pueblos) indigenous community tourism** — not directly applicable

### International + multilateral

5. **EU-LAC cooperation grants** — multi-country. Specific to sustainable tourism. €50-500K
6. **IDB eco-tourism facility** — Bolivia, PY eligible. Loans + grants
7. **World Bank rural tourism PY** — multi-year program. 2026-2027 cycle
8. **GEF (Global Environment Facility)** — biodiversity projects in PY. Possible
9. **CAF (Banco de Desarrollo de América Latina)** — eco + rural development finance

### Carbon credits

10. **Reforestation carbon credits** — voluntary market. Est. $5-15 per ton CO2 sequestered
11. **PY's carbon market infrastructure**: INFONA + APCI. LQV reforestation (60 ha × 100 trees/ha) = ~6,000 trees × 0.5 ton CO2/year = 3,000 tons = $15-45K/year potential revenue. (Long payback, requires verification)

### NGO + foundation grants

12. **WWF Paraguay** — supports eco-lodge + biodiversity conservation. Small grants $5-15K
13. **TNC (The Nature Conservancy)** — Chaco/Atlantic forest programs. Might apply
14. **Avina Foundation** — Latin American eco-finance. Small grants
