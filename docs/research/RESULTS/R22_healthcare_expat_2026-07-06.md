# R22 — Healthcare for Expats in Paraguay

> **For Wesley van de Camp + Ivan.** Sources: expatsettle.com (2026 healthcare guide), Sanatorio Migone official site, Asunción Times hospital guide, ASA State Dept fact sheet, as of 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

Paraguay's private healthcare is **high quality, 50-70% cheaper than US/EU**, and most expat-friendly doctors speak English. **Top private hospitals**: Hospital La Costa, Sanatorio San Roque, Hospital Bautista, Sanatorio Migone Battilana — all in Asunción. **Insurance**: $50-150/mo for comprehensive coverage; international options (SafetyWing from $45) or local (Asismed, Mapfre, Sancor). **Nearest hospital to LQV (Escobar, Paraguarí)**: Hospital Regional de Paraguarí (basic public) or 90 min drive to Asunción for private. **Air ambulance to Buenos Aires or São Paulo**: $15,000-40,000 per flight, available via Sancor + international insurers.

### Nearest hospital to Escobar (LQV site)

- **Escobar district** is a small rural municipality in Paraguarí department
- **Nearest hospital**: Hospital Regional de Paraguarí (public, ~30 km from Escobar) — basic emergency, no English
- **Nearest private hospital**: Sanatorio San Roque or Migone in Asunción — **~100-110 km, 90-120 min drive**
- **Recommendation for LQV**: pre-arrange private ambulance transfer (~Gs. 1.5-2M = $200-270 one way) + identify English-speaking doctor at Sanatorio San Roque for telemedicine consult

### Air ambulance to Buenos Aires or São Paulo

- Available via Sancor Seguros international tier + Control Risks
- **Typical cost**: $15,000-40,000 per flight (depends on aircraft type + medical team)
- **Most common destination** for serious cases: **São Paulo** (Hospital Albert Einstein, Hospital Sírio-Libanês — world-class)
- **Buenos Aires** is closer (~1 hr flight) but Brazilian hospitals are considered better for complex cases
- **Insurance coverage**: usually requires international-tier plan, often pre-authorization needed

### Vaccine requirements for foreigners

- **Yellow fever**: required if traveling from endemic areas (some PY departments are endemic). Not required for EU arrivals directly.
- **COVID-19**: no current requirement (2026)
- **Routine**: MMR, Tdap, Hepatitis A/B recommended
- **PY entry**: no vaccines required for EU/NL/DE/US passport holders (yellow fever only if visiting endemic areas like Alto Paraná)

### Emergency response time to rural properties

- **Asunción metropolitan**: 5-15 min (private ambulance $200-500)
- **San Bernardino**: 10-20 min
- **Paraguarí city**: 15-30 min
- **Escobar rural (LQV site)**: **30-60 min** for public SEME (141), faster with pre-arranged private ambulance
- **Recommendation**: LQV should pre-arrange a private ambulance service contract (~$200-400/month retainer) for guaranteed fast response

### Medical tourism (dental/cosmetic specifically)

- **PY dental tourism**: small but growing — 50-70% cheaper than US
- **Cosmetic surgery**: Asuncion is a niche medical tourism destination (especially for BR/AR patients)
- **Sancor + Asismed** cover most elective procedures after waiting period
- **LQV opportunity**: dental/medical tourism packages (5-7 day stay + dental procedure + recovery in eco-cabin) — not researched in detail, but pricing delta vs US/EU is 60-70%
