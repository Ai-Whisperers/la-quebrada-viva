# EN03 — Volunteer tourism for reforestation (full plan)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** Plan documented (per EN05 - this is the formal version).

## What EN03 is

**LQV's volunteer tourism program:**
- Guests (and external volunteers) help reforest LQV's 62 ha
- Combines eco-tourism + community impact
- Premium pricing possible (vs. just passive resort)

**Per the previous result file (EN05), this is the formal version.**

## Key components

**1. Volunteer structure (per EN05):**
- Model 1: Pay-and-volunteer (every guest has option)
- Model 2: Volunteer-only stays (longer term)
- Model 3: Corporate retreats (premium pricing)
- Model 4: Research accommodation (university partnerships)

**2. Reforestation plan (per A07 + EN02):**
- 5-10 ha of cleared land needs reforestation
- Native species list (per EN02 - already done)
- Plant nursery on-site (saplings grown in-house)
- Watering + maintenance system

**3. Marketing integration:**
- "Plant a tree on your stay" - Instagram-shareable
- LQV Forest Passport - tracking contributions
- Tour groups: schools + corporate + eco-clubs

## Cost-benefit

**Per the prior EN05 analysis:**
- Cost: $13-25K/year
- Revenue potential: $10-70K/year (depending on volume)
- Brand value: significant (ESG narrative, press coverage, repeat guests)

## Implementation plan (Y1-Y3)

**Y1 (Fase 1):**
- Plant 1-2 ha of saplings (initial batch)
- Offer "plant a tree" to all guests (small)
- Document everything (photo + GPS)
- No separate volunteer program (too early)

**Y2 (Fase 2):**
- Launch formal volunteer tourism program
- Partner with 1-2 conservation NGOs (Guyra, etc.)
- 1st corporate retreat (Y2 H2)
- Train staff for volunteer coordination

**Y3 (Fase 3):**
- Full volunteer tourism + reforestation program
- 5+ corporate retreats/year
- 50+ individual volunteers/year
- Eco-certification prep (per EN04)
