# L09 — Fire safety plan for LQV (R01 + new) [W1.2 / insurance hard gate]

**Method:** MEM + Brazilian fire safety standards
**Confidence:** Medium
**Date:** 2026-06-30

## Why this is critical

**LQV's risk profile:**
- 82% Atlantic Forest canopy
- Wooden/bamboo structures (LQV signature style)
- 30 cabins + restaurant + reception (high-occupancy)
- PY dry season (Aug-Oct) is high fire risk
- Lightning common in PY

**Fire is the single biggest risk to LQV's survival.** One ignition could wipe out 10+ cabins in 30 minutes.

## What LQV needs (per insurance underwriting + safety)

### 1. Firebreaks (cleared strips)

- **Width:** 5-10m (5m minimum, 10m for high-risk areas)
- **Location:** Around all structures + at property boundaries
- **Width at building interface:** 3-5m around each cabin
- **Width at property boundary:** varies (fire-resistant material instead of cleared)

**Cost for LQV Fase 1:**
- Tractor clearing: Gs. 50,000-100,000/hectare = $7-14/ha
- ~10 ha of firebreak = $70-140 (cheap)
- **Total: $100-200**

### 2. Water points

- **Fire hydrants or water storage** at strategic points
- **Combine with cistern system** (F15) — same water infrastructure
- **4-6 hydrants** at key locations (near cabin clusters, restaurant, reception, property entrance)

**Cost for LQV:**
- Hydrants: $200-500 each
- 4-6 hydrants: $800-3,000
- Plumbing: $500-1,500
- **Total: $1,300-4,500**

### 3. Access roads for fire trucks

- **Maintained** for fire truck access (2.5m wide minimum)
- **Clearances** (3m height, 3m width)
- **Turning radii** (8-10m at intersections)
- **Bridges** (load-bearing, marked with weight limit)

**Cost:** Already part of the road system (per F-series). Fire-specific requirements:
- Signage: $100-300
- Mirror at blind corners: $200-500
- **Total: $300-800**

### 4. Detection (smoke + heat)

- **Smoke detector** in each cabin: 1 per cabin + 1 in common areas = 6 detectors
- **Heat detector** in kitchen (commercial): 1
- **CO detector** in cabins: 5
- **Total: 12 detectors**

**Cost:**
- Smoke: $20-50 each
- Heat (commercial): $100-200
- CO: $25-50 each
- **Total: $300-700**

### 5. Suppression (extinguishers + response)

- **ABC fire extinguishers** (1 per cabin + 2 in restaurant + 1 in reception)
- **Class K extinguisher** in commercial kitchen (for cooking oils)
- **Fire blankets** in kitchen
- **Hoses** (connected to water hydrants)

**Cost for LQV Fase 1:**
- ABC extinguishers: $50-150 each
- 8 extinguishers: $400-1,200
- Class K: $200-400
- Fire blankets: $30-50 each, 3-4 needed = $100-150
- Hoses (50m each): $100-200 each, 2-3 needed = $300-500
- **Total: $1,000-2,250**

### 6. Response (people + protocol)

- **On-site staff training** (1 day, all staff)
- **Fire drill** quarterly
- **Evacuation plan** posted in each cabin
- **Local volunteer fire brigade** contact (Paraguarí city has one)
- **Mutual aid** with San Bernardino or Paraguarí
- **Evacuation assembly points** (3-4 in the property)

**Cost:** Training time + signage + 1 fire drill per year = $200-500/year

### 7. Detection technology (advanced)

For Fase 2/3:
- **Smoke detection cameras** (AI-powered, 4-camera system)
- **Wildfire detection** (satellite-based or watchtower)
- **Weather station** (monitors for high fire risk days)

**Cost for advanced system:** $5,000-15,000

## Insurance underwriting implications

**Likely insurance requirements:**
- Firebreaks of 5-10m around all structures
- Water storage for fire suppression (50K-100K liters)
- Detection systems (smoke, CO, heat)
- Suppression equipment (extinguishers, hoses)
- Trained staff + evacuation plan
- **All of these are REASONABLE cost** for a $5.5M build

**Without these:** insurance may be unavailable, OR available with high premium, OR with high deductible.
