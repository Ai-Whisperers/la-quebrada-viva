## Summary

San Bernardino is Paraguay's historic **resort town** at the **Ypacaraí Lake**, founded by German immigrants in the 1880s. It's the #1 domestic weekend-escape destination for Asunceños (90-minute drive). The town has ~10-15 small hotels/posadas + Airbnbs, plus numerous restaurants. **It's the cultural + culinary supply base for our RV project** (45 km from parcel).

**Key insight:** San Bernardino's value to RV is NOT as a competitor (it's an hour's drive away, not adjacent to our parcel) but as a **strategic position** — it's the closest tourist destination, has the supply ecosystem (restaurants, hotels, expat community), and is the "before/after" anchor for RV visitors who want a multi-day experience in the region.

## Key Data Points

- **Location:** 45 km from RV parcel, ~1h drive
- **Ypacaraí Lake:** largest lake near Asunción, popular for water sports, weekend escapes
- **Demographics:** Originally German colony (1885), now mixed population with significant German expat descendants
- **Tourism infrastructure:** ~10-15 small hotels (2-4 star), 20+ restaurants, weekend market
- **Population:** ~10,000 residents; peaks to 30,000+ on weekends/holidays
- **Notable:** "Areguá" pottery + ceramics across the lake from San Ber; **Areguá is the artisan village**
- **Climate advantage:** Cooler than Asunción due to lake + elevation (~80m above sea level)
- **What it offers RV:** Weekend-trip precedent, restaurant supply chain, expat labor pool, comparison shopping for cabin designs
