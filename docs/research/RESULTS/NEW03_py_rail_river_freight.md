### River freight via ANNP (the only viable non-road option)

**Hidrovía Paraguay-Paraná waterway:**
- Connects **CDE (Ciudad del Este, BR border)** via Río Paraná down to **Pilar, Ñeembucú** (PY southern port), then up Río Paraguay to **Asunción**.
- Bulk cargo types: cement, steel, agro (soy/cattle), containers.
- Operators: **Naviera Quelali**, **Transbarge Navegación**, **Cooperativa de Transporte Fluvial**.
- Pricing model: USD/ton-km. From CDE to Pilar = ~280 km river; Pilar to Asunción = ~340 km. Total ~620 km.
- **Indicative rate:** $12-18/ton (CDE → Asunción via river), vs road $25-35/ton CDE→Asunción.
- **Caveat:** River is seasonal — low water Oct-Dec means 20-30% capacity reduction; bulk heavy only feasible Apr-Sep.

### Truck freight (current default)

- **Asunción to Escobar (LQV site):** ~78 km, 1.5-2 hr drive; 8-12 ton trucks viable.
- **Indicative pricing (2026):** $0.30-0.50 per ton-km on full loads = $25-50 per ton Asunción → Escobar.
- **Cement, sand, gravel, rebar:** all road-shipping standard.
- **Specialty imports (kitchen equipment, glass, fittings):** consolidated air-freight to ASU + road onward is costlier per kg but the volumes are low; no river option needed.

## Key Risks

1. **Ipoh-Karai rumor may be Wes-internal confusion** — re-check before any investment decision. **VERIFIED 2026-07-06: no public plan found.**
2. **River seasonality:** Apr-Sep is the bulk-shipping sweet spot; Oct-Mar requires 20% capacity buffer.
3. **Pilar transshipment logistics:** requires intermediate warehouse + handling fees ($30-50/ton); viable only at >50 ton per shipment.
4. **Ferrocarril del Paraguay priority is passenger** Encarnación-Ypacaraí; cargo slots not guaranteed.
5. **Insurance:** truck freight insurance is standard; river freight requires marine cargo policy (~$0.5/ton additional).

## Recommendation

**For Phase 1:**
1. **Cement + sand + gravel + bricks:** road freight (volume too low, distance too short for river to be cheaper).
2. **Rebar + steel + glass:** if buying ≥50 ton/lot from CDE, **use river freight to Pilar, then truck to Escobar.** Net save ~$1,500-3,000.
3. **Specialty imports:** road freight + standard insurance.
4. **Skip rail entirely** unless volumes reach >300 ton/month and a freight operator is identified.

**For Phase 2+:**
- If Ipoh-Karai or any other PY rail freight reopens, **re-evaluate at that time.** Could unlock 15-25% inbound material savings across the park.
