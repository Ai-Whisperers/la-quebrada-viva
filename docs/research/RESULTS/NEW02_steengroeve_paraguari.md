## Findings

Paraguay has quarry-friendly geology concentrated in **Cordillera department** (north of Asunción), **Caaguazú** (east), and the **Paraguarí formation** (south — same sandstone the LQV parcel sits on). Three quarry types relevant to Phase 1 cob construction:

1. **Sandstone (areniscas)** — matches LQV site geology. Soft, easy to cut, classic cob interior wall material. Available in Piribebuy (Cordillera), Paraguarí town, Sapucai (Caaguazú).
2. **Itacurubí / Itapucumi (limestone+iron concretions)** — Cordillera's signature stone. Hard, durable, used for foundations, boundary walls, exposed aggregate. Quarries near Itá, Piribebuy.
3. **Granite / basalt (basaltos)** — eastern departments (Alto Paraná, Caaguazú). Used for structural elements; too hard for hand cob prep but ideal for foundations and landscaping.

## Permit / Regulatory considerations

- **Ley 422/73 (Forestal)** — extraction permits from INFONA if >1 ha disturbed. Small cob construction use (< 500 m³) typically exempt.
- **Ley 294/93 (Impacto Ambiental)** — required if site >2 ha disturbed; quarries usually handle this themselves.
- **Municipalidad de Escobar** — no specific construction-stone extraction permit needed if you're the buyer (not the miner).
- **INAA water** — distant from perennial streams; no permission needed for sandstone extraction in upland quarries.

## Key Risks

1. **No on-site sample testing done yet** — actual color, hardness, grain may vary. Mitigation: request 1 m³ sample, evaluate compression + capillary absorption before bulk order.
2. **Seasonal flooding of access roads** to Paraguarí quarries (Dec-Apr) can interrupt supply. Mitigation: stockpile in dry season.
3. **MADES inspections for stacked cob material** on-site during construction — typically OK but verify with capataz.
4. **Pricing volatility** — cement price context: 2026 cement ~480-580 PYG/kg = $65-80/ton = $0.07/kg. Stone is the cheaper side; expect 5-10% annual escalation matching inflation.
5. **Quarry consolidation** — Cerros del Paraná group owns several Cordillera quarries; competition is limited but quality consistent.
