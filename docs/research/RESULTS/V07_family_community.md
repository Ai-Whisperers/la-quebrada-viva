# V07 — Family-anchored community model (formal)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM + Wes's recordings. The family/community positioning.

## What V07 is

**Per Wes's audio E (A07 family-community):**
> "A nice park. Children can go around. Maybe a daycare center. You..."

**This is LQV's specific positioning vs:**
- Pure eco-lodge (no kids)
- Dutch corporate daycare (cold + formal)
- Resort (formal + sterile)
- Retreat (transient, no community)

**LQV is:** A family-anchored community where guests (and staff) are part of a living place, not just staying in a hotel.

## What this means in practice

### Cabin cluster design

**For Fase 1 (5 cabins):**
- Group cabins in clusters of 2-3, not 5 separate
- Each cluster has a small "shared courtyard" or playground
- Walking paths between clusters (5-min walk)
- Shared campfire / BBQ area between clusters
- Optional shared kitchen (for group bookings)

### For Fase 2-3 (10-25 more cabins):

**Central community hub:**
- 1 community building (1 large common area)
- Kitchen + dining for group events
- Workshop / craft room
- Reading / games room
- Small playground + outdoor play area
- Optional 1-2 staff casitas (live on-site)

### For Y2-3 (Sonja's 60th prep):
- Multi-family weekend format (12-15 families)
- Each family has own cabin
- Shared meal times (breakfast + dinner)
- Optional activities together (forest walk, swim, meal)
- Family-bonding focus, not just adult-couple

## Why this works for the European market

**Per V04 (European market research):**
- European families prefer "village-style" stays over hotel-style
- Slow-stay is rising (especially post-COVID)
- Multi-gen travel (grandparents + parents + kids) is growing
- Eco-awareness is high (especially NL + DE)
- "Real life experience" > "resort luxury"

**LQV matches this perfectly:**
- 30 cabins (not 5-10, but not 100+ either)
- Family-anchored
- Eco-credentials
- Local authenticity
- Real community, not staged

## Risks

**Risk 1:** Some guests want pure luxury, no kids
- Mitigation: designate 2-3 cabins as "adults only" with explicit policy

**Risk 2:** Families can be noisy
- Mitigation: "family zones" vs "quiet zones" + 10pm quiet hours

**Risk 3:** Staff turnover with community-building
- Mitigation: 5-7 year staff retention incentive (per W20 retention plan)
