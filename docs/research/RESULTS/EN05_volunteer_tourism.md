# EN05 — Volunteer tourism for reforestation (W1.1 item)

**Method:** MEM + training data
**Confidence:** Medium
**Date:** 2026-06-30

## What LQV's reforestation program could be

**Per D7 (family-anchored community) + A07 (Atlantic Forest regeneration):**

**The opportunity:** Volunteer tourism = paying guests help reforest LQV's 62 ha as part of their stay.
- **Today:** Volunteer tourism is a $100M+ global market (per VolunteerWorld data)
- **PY opportunity:** Almost no competition (Wes has first-mover advantage)
- **Audience:** Eco-conscious travelers, gap-year students, sabbaticals, corporate retreats
- **LQV differentiator:** Active reforestation + comfortable lodging + great food

## Volunteer tourism models

### Model 1: Pay-and-volunteer (most common)
- Guest pays standard cabin rate
- During their stay, contributes 2-4 hours to reforestation (planting, watering, etc.)
- "LQV Forest Passport" or certificate of contribution
- **Pays LQV standard rate** + low-cost reforestation
- **Best for:** Fase 1, simple to implement

### Model 2: Volunteer-only stays (less common)
- Special discounted rate for volunteers (e.g. $30/night + 6 hours/day)
- Targets long-term stays (1-4 weeks)
- **Best for:** Long-term, family-volunteer, gap-year, corporate retreat

### Model 3: Corporate volunteer retreats
- Companies pay premium for team-building volunteer experiences
- 10-20 people, 3-5 day stays
- Format: stay + work + reflection
- **Pays $300-500/person/day** (premium)
- **Best for:** Fase 2-3 (after main operations stable)

### Model 4: Researcher accommodation
- University ecology students stay at LQV
- Research local forest, fauna, soil
- Modest fees ($20-40/night) + university funds research
- **Best for:** Continuous research program, builds academic partnerships

## What LQV needs (Fase 2-3 for full implementation)

- 1 arborist/forestry manager (already planning per O09)
- Volunteer coordination (1 staff member can do this)
- Partnership with 1-2 conservation NGOs (Guyra Paraguay, WWF, local universities)
- Insurance for volunteer work (basic liability)
- Tools (shovels, gloves, saplings, irrigation)
- On-site food (FT10 chef provides)

## Cost estimate

**Year 1 (basic, "Pay-and-volunteer"):**
- Volunteer coordinator role: 0.3 FTE = $5,000-8,000/year
- Tools + plants: $2,000-5,000/year
- Insurance: $1,000-2,000/year
- Total: $8,000-15,000/year for ~50-100 volunteer days
- **Cost per volunteer day: $150-300**

**Year 2-3 (full program):**
- Same costs + partnership fees: $3,000-5,000/year
- Marketing: $2,000-5,000/year
- **Total: $13,000-25,000/year**

## Revenue potential

**Pricing:**
- Model 1: full cabin rate + "passport" badge (no discount)
- Model 2: $30/night + 6 hours/day
- Model 3: $300-500/person/day for 5 days
- Model 4: $20-40/night + university funds

**Year 1 (50-100 volunteer days, mostly Model 1):**
- 50 × $150/night (cabin rate) = $7,500
- 50 × free upgrades + extra fees = $1,000
- 20 Model 3 retreats × 15 people × 5 days × $400 = $60,000 (more aspirational)
- **Total Y1 revenue from volunteering: ~$10,000-70,000**

**This isn't huge revenue but it's massive for marketing + ecosystem restoration + community engagement.**

## Volunteer tourism risks

- **Injury risk:** tree planting, machete work, water
- **Insurance:** basic liability covers most
- **Quality control:** if volunteers do bad planting, replant cost
- **Time cost:** Wes's time supervising, training
- **Reputation risk:** if a volunteer gets hurt

## Recommended for LQV

**Fase 1:** Model 1 (Pay-and-volunteer) only
- All guests have option to participate 2-4 hours
- Not a "volunteer program" per se, just "guests can help if they want"
- Arborist (per O09) leads the activities
- Low risk, low cost, positive impact

**Fase 2:** Add Model 3 (corporate retreats)
- Work with 1-2 corporate clients per year
- Premium rate covers arborist's time + program
- Builds marketing story

**Fase 3:** Add Model 2 + 4 (volunteer-only + research)
- Continuous volunteer pipeline
- University partnerships (Wes's contacts per AUDIO D)
