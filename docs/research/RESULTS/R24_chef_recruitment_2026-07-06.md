## Executive summary

## International chef visa (foráneos)

If hiring a foreign chef (NL/DE/AR/BR):
- **Work permit** (radicación laboral): $200-500 fee, 2-4 month process
- **Employer must demonstrate**: no qualified PY national available (usually rubber-stamp)
- **Salary requirement**: minimum $1,500/month for the visa
- **Validity**: 2 years initial, renewable

**For LQV**: not needed in Phase 1 (PY has enough chefs). Consider for Phase 3 if a specialist is required.

## Specialty: Guaraní-traditional cuisine

**The opportunity**: LQV's Mbyá partnership + the indigenous place-names (Mbopicua, Ybyraty) create a unique opportunity to develop **a Guaraní-inspired menu** based on:
- **Yvyra'ã** (corn varieties)
- **Mandi'o** (cassava) in many forms
- **Kuratu** (small fish from Río Paraguay)
- **Ka'i** (monkey bread fruit, with permission)
- **Jate'i ka'a** (yerba mate in food preparations)
- **Mbyá traditional recipes** (sopa paraguaya, chipa guazú, mbeyú, pira caldo)

**Marketing**: "First eco-retreat in LATAM with a Guaraní-inspired menu" — comparable to Awasi's Andean menu or Casa de Uco's Mendoza wine-pairing.

**For Phase 1**: hire a head chef who has worked with Mbyá or Paraguayan rural cuisine. Likely candidates: chefs who worked at Tier 1 Asu restaurants (Lido Bar, Bolsi, Talleyrand) + rural cuisine exposure.
