## Brand identity (to be designed after name pick)

### Visual identity recommendations

**Color palette (suggested for the eco + European + Latin theme):**
- Earth tones: forest green, warm brown, terracotta
- Accent: cream, gold, light gray
- Avoid: bright primary colors, neon, pure black/white

**Typography:**
- Primary: Serif for headlines (e.g. Playfair Display)
- Body: Clean sans-serif (e.g. Inter, Helvetica, Lato)
- Avoid: trendy display fonts (change frequently)

**Photography style:**
- Natural light (golden hour, overcast)
- Wide landscape (property context)
- Detail shots (cabin textures, food)
- People with consent (guests, Wes, family)
- Real, not staged

### Voice / tone

**For Dutch/German audience:**
- Warm, personal, story-driven
- Specific (not generic)
- Honest (don't oversell)
- Sustainable (eco-credentials, not greenwashing)

**For LATAM audience:**
- Same warmth + personal
- Spanish
- Specific to PY
- Family-friendly (vibrant)

**For expats (in PY):**
- Same
- Bilingual
- Local context
