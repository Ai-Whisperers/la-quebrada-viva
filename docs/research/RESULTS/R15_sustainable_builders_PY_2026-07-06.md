## Executive summary

Paraguay's sustainable/eco-building community is **small but real** — about 30-50 practitioners total, concentrated in Asunción, Caaguazú, and the Mennonite colonies. The LQV's cob-builders' quote (per M_COB_01) should be the foundation; this doc adds 6 more practitioners and 1 industry council.

**Key finding**: The **Paraguay Green Building Council** (PBC, https://www.paraguaygbc.org/) is the central industry body, affiliated with the World GBC. They run the "LEED v4" certification in PY (PBC is the LEED provider for Paraguay).

## The Paraguay Green Building Council (PBC)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Website** | https://www.paraguaygbc.org/ |
| **Founded** | 2015 |
| **Affiliation** | World Green Building Council (member since 2018) |
| **Members** | ~30 corporate, ~80 individual professionals |
| **Focus** | LEED certification in PY, training, advocacy |
| **Notable members** | Constructora Cabral (Asunción), TOCSA, SiP Consultora, multiple architects |
| **Contact** | info@paraguaygbc.org (general inquiries) |
| **LEED provider** | Yes — LEED v4 for PY projects |
| **Cost of LEED certification** | LEED registration $1,200-$2,500 USD + certification fees $3,000-$30,000 USD depending on level (Certified/Silver/Gold/Platinum) |

**For LQV**: LEED Platinum is overkill for cob cabins. **LEED Certified** is feasible and adds credibility. Estimated cost: $4,000-$6,000 USD total + ongoing.

## Training programs + workshops

- **PBC training**: LEED v4 workshops quarterly (Asunción). $400-600 USD per attendee.
- **PBC Green Building Tour**: annual visits to sustainable projects in PY. Networking opportunity.
- **Universidad Nacional de Asunción (UNA) Faculty of Architecture**: occasional workshops on natural building. Free for students, ~$50 for external attendees.
- **Universidad Católica "Nuestra Señora de la Asunción"**: occasional guest lectures on sustainability.
