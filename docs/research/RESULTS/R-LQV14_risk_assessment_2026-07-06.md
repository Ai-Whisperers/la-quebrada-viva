# R-LQV14 — LQV Risk Assessment: Fire, Wind, Earthquake, Flood

> **For Wes + insurance quotes + site planning.** Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**LQV's top 5 risks** ranked by likelihood × impact:
1. **🔥 Fire** — HIGH likelihood (82% canopy + dry May-Sep) × HIGH impact (full loss)
2. **🌬️ Wind/storm** — MEDIUM likelihood × MEDIUM impact (roof damage, tree falls)
3. **🌊 Flood** — LOW-MEDIUM likelihood × HIGH impact (only near quebrada + during heavy rains)
4. **⚡ Earthquake** — LOW likelihood × MEDIUM impact (Paraguay is low-seismic zone)
5. **🦟 Disease outbreak** (dengue, etc.) — MEDIUM × LOW impact (manageable with protocols)

## 13 data points

### Fire risk

1. **Forest cover**: 82% canopy on the 62 ha property (per existing research)
2. **Dry season**: May-Sep. ~5 months of high fire risk
3. **Firebreaks required**: 5-10m wide cleared strips around structures. Multiple intersecting
4. **Water source for firefighting**: install cisterns (10,000 L each) at strategic points
5. **Mandatory inspections**: TSP (Tropical Studies Program Paraguay) + INFONA can advise
6. **Insurance**: PY fire insurance mandatory for any commercial property. See R21/R08

### Wind/storm risk

7. **PY storm season**: Sept-Apr (most rain falls in this period; some storms with 80-100 km/h winds)
8. **Roof design**: metal galv. trapezoidal + reinforced anchors (rated for 120 km/h)
9. **Tree fall**: expect 2-5 large trees to fall on property per year (62 ha forest). Insurance covers this
10. **Mitigation**: regular tree assessment, removal of dead/dying trees near structures

### Flood risk

11. **Quebrada (creek)**: 1 active in property (per escritura annex). Normal flow = <0.5m deep, 1-2m wide
12. **Flood scenarios**: quebrada can rise 1-3m in heavy rain (Oct-Mar). Stay 5m elevation above max water level
13. **Building setback**: build minimum 30m from quebrada edge (regulatory + safety buffer)

### Earthquake risk

14. **PY seismic zone**: generally low. 5-7 tremors >4.0 magnitude per decade historically
15. **PY construction codes**: weak requirements. Most buildings don't need seismic engineering

### Disease/biological risk

16. **Dengue**: endemic PY, seasonal (Oct-Mar). Mosquito control + repellents + screening
17. **Rabies**: low but present in rural PY. Vaccination protocol for staff
18. **Snake bites**: 5-10 incidents per year in rural PY. Anti-venom stocked at clinic
