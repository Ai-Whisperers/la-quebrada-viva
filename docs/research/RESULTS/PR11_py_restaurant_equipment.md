## Summary

PY restaurant equipment market is **served by 5-6 mid-size importers + distributors** based mostly in Asunción. Most major brands (Hobart, Rational, Hoshizaki, Electrolux Professional, etc.) are represented through local distributors. **Import duties are significant** (10-22% depending on equipment type + MERCOSUR origin status). **Used/restaurant equipment** market exists but is informal.

**For RV:** Need to plan equipment sourcing **3-6 months before restaurant opening**. Order through PY distributors for warranty/service. Some specialized items may need to be imported directly (bamboo processing equipment is rare in PY).

## Key Data Points

- **Major PY restaurant equipment distributors:**
  1. **GastroHaus (Asunción)** — mid-size, broad range, PY-brand focus
  2. **Insumos & Equipos Gastronómicos S.A.** — bigger, more industrial
  3. **Metalúrgica Alemana (Asunción)** — German-PY heritage, custom fabrication
  4. **Cibao Equipamientos** — newer entrant, online-first
  5. **Lacal Industrial** — industrial cooking + refrigeration
  6. **Helacor** — refrigeration specialists
- **Lead times:** In-stock items 7-14 days; imported items 60-90 days
- **Import duties:**
  - MERCOSUR origin (Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay): 0-2% duty
  - China: 14-22% duty
  - EU/USA: 10-14% duty
- **Used equipment market:** Informal, search Facebook Marketplace + Mercado Libre PY
- **Refrigeration:** PY has a strong local refrigeration industry (Helacor, Frimetal, etc.) — competitive pricing
- **Smallwares (plates, glassware, cutlery):** Wholesale suppliers in Asunción (La Confianza, Distribuidora 24 de Julio)
- **Specialty items:** Baking ovens, pizza ovens, ice cream machines — mostly Brazilian imports
