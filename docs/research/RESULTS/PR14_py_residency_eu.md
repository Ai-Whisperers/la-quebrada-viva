## Summary

Paraguay offers **several residency options for EU citizens** including the **MERCOSUR Residence Agreement** (for EU citizens + MERCOSUR nationals — easier path), the **Investor Visa** (for those investing USD 5K+ in PY), and the **Permanent Residency** (after 3 years of temporary residency). **Naturalization** is possible after 3 years of permanent residency.

**For Wes:** The MERCOSUR Residence Agreement is likely the best path — it gives **immediate legal residency + work rights + path to citizenship**, requires only proof of income/financial solvency (~USD 1,500/month equivalent) + clean criminal record. **Processing time: 3-6 months**.

## Key Data Points

- **MERCOSUR-EU Residence Agreement** (since 2009):
  - **Eligibility:** EU citizens + MERCOSUR nationals (PY/BR/AR/UY)
  - **Requirements:**
    - Valid passport
    - Police clearance (Interpol check)
    - Birth certificate (apostilled)
    - Proof of financial solvency (~$1,500/month or savings)
    - Health certificate
    - PY health insurance
    - No criminal record in last 5 years
  - **Benefits:**
    - 2-year residency (renewable)
    - Work rights
    - Open bank account
    - Buy property
    - Bring family (spouse, children under 18)
    - Travel in/out of PY freely
  - **Path to permanent residency:** After 2 years
  - **Path to citizenship:** After 3 years of permanent residency
- **Investor Visa:**
  - **Minimum investment:** USD 5,000 in PY (real estate, business, etc.)
  - **Same benefits as MERCOSUR-EU**
  - **No work permit** (investor doesn't need one)
- **Real estate ownership:** Foreigners can own rural land directly with RUC; some restrictions on border zones
- **Comparison to NL:** PY residency is ~10x easier to obtain than NL; tax rate is 10% IRP vs NL 37-49% Box 2
- **Tax implications:** Wes becomes PY tax resident; worldwide income subject to PY tax (with credits for NL-paid tax)
