# AH03 — Used vs new for bouwfase auto (cost-benefit)

**Method:** MEM (NPV analysis)
**Confidence:** Medium
**Date:** 2026-06-30

## Decision: Tundra/Presio (or similar) for Fase 1 bouwfase — new or used?

### Context
- RV needs 1 vehicle for Fase 1 (5 cabins, 1 year build)
- Tundra = $50-70K USD new, ~$30-40K 2-3 year used
- Presio (Hilux) = $40-55K new, ~$25-35K used
- Other options: Land Cruiser, Hilux, Ranger

### NPV analysis (3-year horizon)

**Option A: New Tundra @ €55K**
- Initial: -€55,000
- Annual operating (fuel, maintenance, insurance): -€6,000/year × 3 = -€18,000
- Resale value at year 3: +€30,000 (60% retention)
- **NPV: -€43,000**

**Option B: Used Tundra (2-yr) @ €35K**
- Initial: -€35,000
- Annual operating: -€5,000/year × 3 = -€15,000 (lower for used)
- Resale value: +€18,000 (50% retention, less for used)
- **NPV: -€32,000**

**Option C: New Presio (Hilux) @ €45K**
- Initial: -€45,000
- Annual operating: -€5,000/year × 3 = -€15,000
- Resale value: +€25,000 (60%)
- **NPV: -€35,000**

**Option D: Used Presio (2-yr) @ €30K**
- Initial: -€30,000
- Annual operating: -€4,000/year × 3 = -€12,000
- Resale value: +€15,000 (50%)
- **NPV: -€27,000**

**Option E: Rent (3-year contract) @ €1,500/month = €54,000 total**
- Initial: €0
- Annual operating included in rent
- Resale: €0
- **NPV: -€54,000 (highest cost over 3 years)**

### Comparison

| Option | Total 3-yr cost | NPV | Risk | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| A: New Tundra | €73,000 | -€43,000 | low | newest, best warranty |
| B: Used Tundra | €50,000 | -€32,000 | medium | 1-yr warranty left, mechanical risk |
| C: New Presio | €60,000 | -€35,000 | low | cheaper than Tundra, smaller |
| D: Used Presio | €42,000 | **-€27,000** | medium | cheapest buy, mechanical risk |
| E: Rent | **€54,000** | -€54,000 | none | no asset, no resale |

### Recommendation

**Option D: Used Presio @ €30K** is the best NPV. Lower capital, lower operating, OK resale. Mechanical risk is real but manageable (POV inspection, service history check).

**Runner-up: Option B (Used Tundra)** if Wes wants the Tundra specifically for the harder terrain / higher load (Fase 2+).

**Avoid: Option E (Rent)** — costs more than buying a used vehicle. Rent only for short durations (1-3 months, e.g. for the initial 2-week drone LiDAR survey).

### Cost-benefit
**Buying a used Presio for €30K instead of renting for 3 years (€54K): saves €24K.**

That's enough to fund the drone LiDAR survey (€1.5-3K) + the fire safety plan (€3-5K) + most of the 5-onderwerpen materials research.

### Phasing recommendation
- **Fase 1 (Year 1):** Buy used Presio (€30K)
- **Fase 2 (Year 2-3):** Keep using it OR upgrade to Tundra if terrain demands
- **Year 3:** Sell the Presio (recover €15K = 50% retention)
- **Year 4+:** Re-evaluate based on Fase 2/3 build needs

### Risk mitigation
- Buy from a dealer with warranty (1-year minimum)
- Full inspection before purchase
- Budget €2-3K for any post-purchase repairs in year 1
- Insurance: PY mandatory liability + comprehensive (~€1,500/year)
