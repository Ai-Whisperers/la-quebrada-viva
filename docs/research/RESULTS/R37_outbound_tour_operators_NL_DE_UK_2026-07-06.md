## Executive Summary

The outbound tour-operator industry has **3 layers** relevant to LQV: large tour operators (Sawadee, Kras Reizen NL), specialist ecotourism operators (Wild Frontiers UK, Natural Habitat Adventures US), and wellness-specific operators (BookWellness, Healing Hotels). Typical commission structure is **15-25% net** to operator + **2-5% marketing fund**. Approaching them requires a **press kit, FAM trip invitation + 2-week hosted visit**. Volume expectations: small operator partnership = 5-20 guests/year per destination; large operator = 30-100 guests/year. **PY is currently underserved by outbound eco-tourism operators** — opportunity for LQV to be among first movers.

### (f) How to Approach Them

**Step 1: Discovery**
- LinkedIn search + tour operator Trade Show (WTM London, ITB Berlin)
- Direct email to tourism director (NOT generic sales@)
- PY embassy in NL/DE trade partnership sometimes brokers

**Step 2: Initial Pitch (~10-page deck)**
- Property overview (location, units, experience)
- 4-season availability calendar
- Operator margin (15-25% net commission)
- Marketing materials agreement (booking.partner.com access)
- 2 weeks FAM-trip invitation
- Payment terms (often 90 days post-stay)

**Step 3: Formal Contract**
- Standard operator agreement template (3-year renewable)
- Booking terms (deposits, cancellations)
- Audit rights
- Volume commitments (subject to minimum commitment requirements)

**Step 4: FAM Trip (Most-Loved Visit)**
- 2-week hosted visit for 2-3 operators' product managers
- All travel + lodging covered by LQV
- Roving experiences: cabanas + restaurant + wellness pool + ceremonies
- Cost: $2,500-4,000 per operator FAM visit (significant but high-ROI)

Typical tour operators want **2-5% marketing fund** on top of base commission, used by them for digital content + guide book listings + travel show posters.

For LQV Phase 1: budget 4-6% of bookings as marketing co-op fund.
