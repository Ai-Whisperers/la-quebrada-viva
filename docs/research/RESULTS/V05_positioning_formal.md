# V05 — Resort + events + nature blend positioning (formal)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM + Wes's recordings. The strategic positioning.

## What LQV is (positioning)

**Per Wes's recordings (AUDIO E + DREAMLIST_D14):** "Resort + events + nature blend"

**Three segments, one property:**

### 1. Resort (the core)
- 30 cabins + restaurant + amenities
- 2p Basic to Family Luxe Spa spectrum
- $150-450/night ADR range
- Slow-stay, family-anchored, eco-quality

### 2. Events (high-margin)
- Weddings (Fase 2-3)
- Corporate retreats (Fase 2)
- Family celebrations (birthdays, anniversaries)
- Cultural events (Mennonite + German community partnerships)

### 3. Nature (the story)
- Reforestation project
- Atlantic Forest regeneration
- Wildlife corridor
- Eco-tourism experiences
- Volunteer tourism

## Why this 3-segment model works

| Segment | Season | Margin | Customers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resort | Year-round | Steady | Couples, families |
| Events | Weekend + holiday | Premium | Brides, corporate, families |
| Nature | Year-round | Marketing | All guests + volunteers |

**Resort = steady base load**
**Events = premium margin (weekends and high-demand periods)**
**Nature = story + marketing + ESG narrative**

**Synergy:** resort guests buy events, event clients refer resort, nature experience becomes a differentiator vs hotels.

## How the segments differ in service

### Resort guest
- Self-sufficient stay
- Restaurant, pool, gardens, walks
- Minimal staff interaction (cleaning + meals)

### Event client
- Custom planning + coordination
- Catering, decoration, activities
- Higher staff interaction (chefs, coordinators)
- Higher per-day revenue (€500-1,500 for weddings)

### Nature experience
- Educational (Kuikopee forester or similar)
- Volunteering (reforestation)
- Guided walks
- Storytelling for marketing

## Strategic value

**Brand differentiation:** LQV is "more than a hotel." It's a destination that has a story.
**Pricing power:** Events can charge 2-3x standard ADR
**Loyalty:** Nature experience creates emotional connection, repeat visits
**Media coverage:** Weddings + reforestation = ongoing press
