# M15 — Online review management strategy

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** Strategic outline.

## Why reviews matter for RV

**Per V04 (European market):**
- European travelers check TripAdvisor + Booking.com + Google Maps reviews
- 85%+ of European bookings start with a review check
- A 4.5+ rating is the threshold for "consider" — below 4.0 kills conversion
- A few "great" reviews with personal stories outperform many 5-stars

**RV starts with no reviews.** First 6-12 months are critical to build the review base.

## Review management plan

### Phase 1 (Y1): Foundation
- Set up profiles on: Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Google Maps, Airbnb
- **Post-stay review request** to every guest (timing: 24-48 hours after checkout, while memory is fresh)
- Aim for: 5+ reviews per cabin per month (total ~25/month)
- Average target rating: 4.5+ stars
- **Hand-write replies** to every review (free, time-consuming, high-impact)
- **Photographer in cabin** for guest photos (encourages them to share)

### Phase 2 (Y2): Optimization
- 50+ reviews per month
- Aim for 4.7+ stars
- Reviews posted to RV website + Booking.com + Google Maps (synced)
- Respond within 24 hours
- Use reviews in marketing (with permission)

### Phase 3 (Y3): Authority
- 100+ reviews per month
- Aim for 4.8+ stars
- Reviews as marketing assets (testimonial quotes, "what guests love" videos)
- Sonja's 60th weekend = milestone for 50+ testimonials

## How to get great reviews

**The secret:** ask for the review IN the moment of delight
- Mid-stay (day 3-4 of a 7-day stay): "How's your stay? Anything we can improve?"
- End of stay (checkout day): brief, warm goodbye, ask for honest review
- **Avoid:** "please give us 5 stars" (looks desperate, gets 1-star reviews)
- **Encourage:** specific moments (the pool, the cabin view, the food, the staff)

**Avoid asking for review at stressful moments:**
- Check-out time pressure
- If there was a complaint
- If the guest seems rushed

## What to do with negative reviews

**The truth:** 1 in 10 reviews will be 1-3 stars. Some are legitimate (a real issue), some are unfair.

**RV's response approach:**
1. Read carefully + identify the core issue
2. Reply within 24 hours (always)
3. Acknowledge + apologize + explain
4. Offer concrete fix
5. Take the conversation offline (email, not in the reply)
6. **Do NOT argue** in public

**Statistical reality:** 5+ reviews with 1-2 negative + 1+ addressed well = actually +0.1 stars on average (reviewers see the response, give credit for handling)

## Marketing-side use of reviews

**Best testimonials:**
- Specific stories (not just "great stay")
- Visual content (their photos, your photos)
- Name + country (builds trust)
- Detail about why they chose RV (e.g. "I came for the natural pool, stayed for the staff")

**For RV's marketing deck:**
- 10-15 best quotes per year (with permission)
- 1-2 "guest journey" videos per year
- Reference to specific amenities (pool, food, family, view)
