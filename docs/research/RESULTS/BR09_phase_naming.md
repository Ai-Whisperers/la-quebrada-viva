# BR09 — Phase naming convention (Fase 1, 2, 3)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Naming convention for RV's build phases.

## What BR09 is

**RV's 3-phase build needs clear naming for:**
- Marketing (different offers per phase)
- Investor communication (different opportunity per phase)
- Operational (different teams, different ops per phase)
- Branding (consistent)

## Recommended naming

**Phase 1: "ALBA"** (Latin for "dawn")
- 5 cabins + reception + restaurant
- Y1: open + first guests
- The "beginning" theme
- 2026-2027

**Phase 2: "DÍA"** (Spanish for "day")
- 10 more cabins + restaurant expansion + wellness pool
- Y2: full Fase 1
- The "growth" theme
- 2027-2028

**Phase 3: "NOCHE"** (Spanish for "night")
- 15 more cabins + full amenities + events venue
- Y3+: complete project
- The "abundance" theme
- 2028-2029

**Why this naming:**
- Latin for first (international/Wes + Thijs's heritage)
- Spanish for next two (PY + LATAM)
- Progression: dawn → day → night
- Easy to remember
- Brand-friendly

**Marketing integration:**
- RV ALBA: "The beginning" - for early adopters, low ADR, high community
- RV DÍA: "The growth" - middle phase, mid-ADR
- RV NOCHE: "The abundance" - mature phase, high-ADR + events

**Alternative naming (if user prefers English):**
- The Dawn
- The Day
- The Night

## What this enables

- Different marketing for each phase (early vs late)
- Different investor opportunities (lower entry, higher upside, etc.)
- Different team compositions (early = lean, late = specialized)
- Clear milestone communication
