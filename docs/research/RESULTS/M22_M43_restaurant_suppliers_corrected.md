## Update to earlier M22 RESULT file

The earlier M22 file mentioned "Gastro-Haus (Asunción, German-owned, top PY restaurant supplier)" — **this was a hallucination on my part.** GastroHaus is a **Brazilian** restaurant chain (in Horizontina RS, Panambi RS, etc.), not a PY restaurant equipment supplier.

**Correction:** The actual PY restaurant supply chain is more fragmented and less web-indexed. The main sources for commercial kitchen equipment in PY are:

1. **Brazilian importers** (the main channel)
2. **Local Asunción workshops** (smaller, custom fabrication)
3. **Hotel/restaurant liquidation** (used equipment)
4. **Direct import from Europe/China** (for high-end)

## Recommended sourcing strategy for LQV

**For Fase 1 (5 cabins, ~20-30 covers/day):**
- **Walk-in CDE (Ciudad del Este)** for general equipment (Gastromaq, Tedimetal, Metalúrgica Real)
- 1-2 local Asunción kitchen designers for layout + small custom work
- 1 used-equipment dealer for backup (e.g. when a Brazilian hotel closes)
- **CDE trip cost:** 1 day, ~€80 (fuel + tolls + 1 night hotel if needed)

**For Fase 2-3 (full restaurant, 100+ covers):**
- Hire a commercial kitchen designer (Asunción) for the full spec
- Direct import from Brazil/Italy for premium equipment
- Consider 1-2 specialty items from Europe (La Marzocco, Rational)
