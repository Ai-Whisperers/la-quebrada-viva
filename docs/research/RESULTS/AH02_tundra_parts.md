## Hilux / Presio parts in PY

**Common parts available:**
- Filters (oil, air, fuel, cabin)
- Brake pads + rotors
- Suspension (shocks, springs, bushings)
- Engine components (pistons, valves, gaskets)
- Body panels (limited supply, often import-only)
- Lights + electrical
- Glass (windshield, windows)
- Tires (standard sizes, multiple brands)

**Where to source:**
- **Toyota dealer (Asunción):** OEM parts, 1-3 day delivery
- **Independent Toyota specialists (Asunción + CDE):** cheaper, often good quality
- **MERCOSUR cross-border:** Brazil, Argentina dealers ship parts
- **Brazilian dealers via CDE:** biggest supply, often cheaper than Toyota dealer

**Lead times:**
- Common parts: 1-7 days (in stock at Toyota dealer)
- Less common: 1-3 weeks (need to order from BR or AR)
- Body panels: 4-6 weeks (special order)
- Engine major components: 2-4 weeks

**Cost multiplier vs Toyota dealer:**
- Independent specialist: 30-50% cheaper
- Cross-border (BR): 20-40% cheaper

## Tundra parts in PY

**Tundra is the harder one:**
- Few in PY (Tundra is rare)
- Most Tundra parts would be special order
- Toyota dealer can order from US (4-8 weeks lead time)
- Aftermarket parts (aftermarket brands, not Toyota OEM): 8-12 weeks
- Body panels: 12+ weeks
- Engine major: 8-12 weeks

**Cost:**
- Toyota dealer OEM: 2-3x Hilux equivalent (just for shipping + scarcity)
- Aftermarket: 1.5-2x
- **Total Tundra ownership cost over 5 years: ~$8,000-15,000 more than Hilux equivalent**

**Tundra's parts scarcity in PY is the strongest argument for choosing Hilux.**

## Operational reality

**Hilux is the workhorse of rural PY:**
- Used by ANDE, police, agricultural cooperatives
- Multiple specialists in every region
- 2nd-hand parts (from wrecked vehicles) readily available
- Common knowledge among mechanics

**Tundra is mostly unknown in rural PY:**
- Few mechanics know the platform
- Parts wait time: 2-4x longer than Hilux
- Specialist knowledge: rare
- **For LQV bouwfase (remote property, lots of off-road) → Hilux is the right tool**

## Recommendation (per F20 / AH03)

**Used Hilux SRV from CDE import (MERCOSUR 0% tax):**
- $28,000-32,000
- 2-3 year old
- 4WD double cab
- Parts: abundant, common, cheap
- Mechanics: know it well
- **For LQV's use case (remote property, rough roads, frequent off-road) → ideal tool**

**Don't choose Tundra for LQV bouwfase.** The cost of slow repairs + parts wait + specialist fees would offset any Tundra power advantage (which LQV doesn't need).
