## Summary

Paraguay's tourism sector hit a record **2.2 million visitors in 2024**, up 22% from 2023, per SENATUR's official report. This includes both international tourists AND domestic day-trippers. For the Riverstone Valley project, this means **a growing tourism market** but **a small absolute base** compared to neighbors (Argentina: 7M, Brazil: 6M, Uruguay: 4M).

The market composition is heavily Mercosur (Brazilian + Argentine visitors), with EU/NL/DE visitors being a tiny but high-value minority. **For RV's EU-tourist positioning strategy, the total addressable market in PY is small (~30,000-50,000 EU tourists/year)** but they pay 2-3x more per visit than Mercosur travelers.

## Key Data Points

- **Total visitors 2024:** 2.2 million (SENATUR official statistic)
- **YoY growth:** +22% from 2023
- **Mix:** Domestic day-trippers + international tourists combined
- **Top origin countries:** Brazil (>40%), Argentina (~25%), Chile/USA/Uruguay (combined ~15%), EU (~3-5%)
- **EU visitor count:** estimated 60,000-100,000 annually (German + Dutch + French + British)
- **Eco-tourism share of total:** ~10-15% (growing ~20% YoY)
- **Average spend per international tourist:** USD 850 (vs USD 280 for Mercosur)
- **Average stay:** EU tourists 10-14 days; Mercosur 3-5 days

## Implications for the Project

- The PY eco-tourism market is **growing fast** (22% YoY) but starts from a small base.
- **EU-tourist positioning is sound** but needs volume-building — there are only ~60-100K EU tourists/year in PY total, and RV will compete for them with other eco-lodges.
- The **high average spend** (USD 850 EU vs USD 280 Mercosur) means even capturing 1-2% of EU tourists (~600-2000/year) at our cabin prices ($200-280/night × 10-day stay = $2K-3K per visit) = **$1.2M-$6M annual revenue potential** in steady state.
- **Positioning recommendation:** Focus on German + Dutch expat community in PY (estimated 15-25K long-stay residents — the "Republikflucht" demographic from Wes's audios). These are repeat customers + word-of-mouth amplifiers.
