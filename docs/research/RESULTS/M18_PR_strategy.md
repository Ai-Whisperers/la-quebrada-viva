# M18 — PR strategy (Asunción + international)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Strategic plan.

## What M18 is

**RV's PR strategy covers:**
- Local press (Asunción + San Bernardino + Encarnación)
- International press (Europe, especially NL + DE)
- Online presence (TripAdvisor, Booking.com reviews, social)
- Word-of-mouth (Wes's network + Sonja's community)

## Local PR (Asunción + San Bernardino + Encarnación)

**Outlets:**
- **ABC Color** (largest PY daily): travel + lifestyle section
- **Última Hora**: travel + lifestyle
- **La Nación**: travel
- **5días** (Sunday supplement): lifestyle
- **Crónica** (lifestyle weekly)
- **PY magazines:** La Lupa, Eme

**Audience:** Asunción residents (potential weekend market) + expat community (potential word-of-mouth)

**Pitch angle:** "New eco-vacation park opens in Paraguarí, German + Dutch owners, 30 cabins planned over 3 years, family-focused, natural pool, reforestation."

**How to pitch:**
- 1 press release (200-300 words) at the time of the first cabin opening
- 1 personalized pitch to a specific journalist (lifestyle, travel, or environment)
- 1 follow-up with photos and an offer of a free stay in exchange for coverage
- 1-2 follow-up stories as the project evolves

**Cost:** $200-500 for the press release + small host fees (or comp stay)

## International PR (Europe)

**Outlets:**
- **Dutch lifestyle magazines:** LINDA, LINDA.nld, Quest, Happinez
- **Dutch travel media:** Columbus, Reizen, Travel + Escape
- **German lifestyle:** Brigitte, Geo, Merian, Schöner Wohnen
- **German travel:** Reise + Kultur, ADAC Reisemagazin
- **Belgian:** Knack Weekend, De Standaard Magazine

**Audience:** European eco-conscious travelers

**Pitch angle:** "European-owned eco-park in Paraguay, family-focused, 30 cabins, 2030 anniversary target, reforestation story."

**How to pitch:**
- 1-2 press releases per year (Fase 1 opening + 2030 anniversary)
- Pitch to 1-2 specific journalists per year
- Offer comp stay for journalists who cover
- Coordinate with AHK Paraguay (German-Argentine-International Chamber of Commerce) for German media

**Cost:** $500-1,500/year (including comp stays + travel for journalists)

## Word-of-mouth strategy

**Most important for RV:**

**1. Wes + Sonja's personal network (largest):**
- 50-200 personal contacts each
- Wes's European expat + German + Dutch community
- Sonja's local community
- Each contact is a potential guest
- Personal invitations = first 5-10 bookings

**2. RV guests (post-stay):**
- 30 cabins × 50% occupancy = 15 guests/week = 750/year
- 10% refer someone = 75 referrals/year
- Each referral = 1 new guest

**3. PY expat community:**
- Asunción expats (50-100K community)
- San Bernardino German community
- Mennonite network
- Recommend through networks

**4. Travel agents (LTV partner):**
- NL: TUI, Corendon, Sawadee
- DE: TUI, DER Touristik
- AR: local agencies with PY destinations
- Select 2-3 to partner with

**5. Online travel communities (TripAdvisor forums, etc.):**
- Active engagement in forums
- Reviews (per M15)
- Answers to traveler questions about PY
