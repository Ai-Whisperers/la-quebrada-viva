## Executive summary

PY auto insurance is **mandatory** (SOA = Seguro Obligatorio de Automotores for liability, comprehensive optional). For LQV operations: 2-3 vehicles (SUV preferred for LQV terrain) + 1-2 commercial vehicles (cooking supply deliveries, airport transfers). Annual cost: **$3,500-6,500/year** for comprehensive coverage on 3 vehicles.

## Cross-border coverage (NL+EU→PY)

LQV guests will arrive mostly by air (Asu airport), but some may drive from BR (Iguazú, ~6 hr drive) or AR (Buenos Aires, ~18 hr drive). The LQV needs cross-border coverage for vehicles that may cross.

| Border crossing | Cost premium | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **PY → AR (Posadas)** | +$200/yr | Frequent crossings for Awasi referral guests |
| **PY → BR (Foz do Iguaçu)** | +$200/yr | For Brazilian guests visiting Iguazú then LQV |
| **PY → UY (Rivera)** | +$200/yr | Rare |

**Recommended**: include AR + BR coverage; skip UY (low traffic).

## Foreign owner specifics

For Wes (NL resident):
- **Driving license**: NL + EU + international driver's license valid in PY
- **Vehicle registration**: PY resident or temporary importer's license required
- **Radicación (residency)**: required for >5 yr vehicle ownership without re-importing

**For LQV corp-owned vehicles**: register under the BV3 / IB (per HG-2 ownership decision). Easier for fleet management.

**For Wes's personal vehicle**: temporary import (until radicación) or permanent import after radicación.

## Driver's license reciprocity

NL/DE/FR/IT/ES/PT/UK/US/CA driver's licenses: **valid for 90 days** as tourist in PY. After 90 days, need PY license.

**Steps for PY license**:
1. Pass PY theory exam (PY-specific traffic laws)
2. Pass PY practical exam
3. PY driving license (5 yr validity)
4. Total cost: $100-200 + 1-2 wk process

**Recommendation**: get the PY license early (within the 90-day window or after radicación).
