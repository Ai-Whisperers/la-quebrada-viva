# R13 — Asunción Expat Demographics + Communities

> **For Wesley van de Camp + Ivan.** Sources: planbexpat.com, expatsettle.com, getresidencyparaguay.com, independentterritory.com, as of 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

Asunción's expat community is concentrated in **6 adjacent upscale neighborhoods** (Villa Morra, Carmelitas, Recoleta, Las Lomas, Las Mercedes, Mburucuyá) within 20 min of each other. **Total expat population estimated ~50,000-80,000** (no official figure), with the **US/Mexican Mennonite diaspora being the largest single group** (~30,000-40,000 in Chaco alone). NL/DE/FR expats are smaller but growing — NL estimated 800-1,500 residents. **San Bernardino** (40 min from Asunción) is the established weekend retreat + secondary home spot for Asunción expats.

### Expat organizations

- **AHK Paraguay** — German-Argentine Chamber of Commerce (covers PY too). Active in business networking.
- **Cámara de Comercio Paraguayo-Alemana** — direct German-PY chamber
- **Cámara de Industria y Comercio Paraguayo-Norteamericana** (CIPCA) — US business network
- **Alliance Française** — French cultural center + French classes
- **St. Mary's College / ASA / Goethe** — expat schools serve as community hubs
- **San Bernardino expat network** — informal, weekend retreat community
- **No formal Dutch Chamber** in PY as of 2026 (small NL population)
- **Mennonite cooperative (ACOME)** — the dominant economic institution in Chaco

### Schools that cater to expats

- **American School of Asunción (ASA)** — US curriculum, $8,806/yr tuition, $700 registration
- **Colegio Goethe** — German curriculum, IB offered, 130-year history
- **St. Mary's College** — UK-curriculum (Anglican)
- **Colegio del Sol** — bilingual (ES/EN)
- See R32 for full breakdown

### Hobbies / restaurants / social clubs

- **Golf**: Asunción Golf Club (private, Villa Morra), Yacht y Golf Club Paraguayo
- **Sailing**: Yacht y Golf Club on the Paraguay River
- **Tennis**: Several private clubs
- **Hiking**: Caacupé hills, Cerro Tres Kandú (842m, highest point in PY accessible region)
- **Restaurants**: La Cuadrita (Recoleta), Lido Bar (Villa Morra), Talleyrand (Carmelitas), dozens of Asian/European options
- **Coworking**: Workas, Impact Hub Asunción, several private spaces
- **Languages**: Alliance Française, Goethe-Institut, many private English teachers

### Tourism operators already serving EU-expat market

- **San Ber weekend retreats** — multiple B&Bs, hostels, small hotels (15-30 min from Asunción)
- **Estancia tourism** — several traditional estancias (e.g., Estancia Santa Sofía, near Concepción)
- **Jesuit missions tours** (Trinidad, Jesús de Tavarangue) — UNESCO sites
- **Iguazú Falls day-trips** from Ciudad del Este (ARG/BRA side more popular)
- **Pantanal excursions** (limited, more accessible from BR side)

**Implication for LQV**: direct competitors in the EU-expat weekend retreat market are the San Bernardino B&Bs (cheaper but more basic). LQV's differentiation: cob-built + wellness pool + curated experience + Sonja cultural anchor.
