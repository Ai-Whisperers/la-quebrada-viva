## NL tax system (2026, simplified)

**3 boxes for income/wealth tax:**

| Box | What | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Box 1** | Work, home ownership | 37.07-49.50% (progressive) |
| **Box 2** | Substantial interest (5%+ in BV) | 26.9% (flat) |
| **Box 3** | Savings, investments, other assets | 36% deemed return on net wealth (after €57K exemption) |
