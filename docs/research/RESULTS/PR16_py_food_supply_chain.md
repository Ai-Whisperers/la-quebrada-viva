## Summary

San Bernardino has a **modest but functional food supply chain**: a permanent weekly **feria (market)** with local producers, 3-5 small supermarkets (Stock, Casa Rica, etc.), and several **organic vegetable growers** (mostly German-descended expat farms). **For RV restaurant**, the supply mix would be: 40% local San Ber / Paraguarí producers, 40% Asunción wholesale (Mercado de Abasto), 20% direct imports (specialty items).

**For RV's moestuin (vegetable garden) concept:** Wes's idea of partnering with local farmers for the restaurant's vegetables is **well-supported by existing infrastructure** — there are already established organic farms within 50 km of RV.

## Key Data Points

- **San Bernardino weekly market (Feria de San Ber):** Saturdays, ~30-50 vendors
- **Major local organic producers within 50 km:**
  1. **Finca La Querencia** (San Ber) — German expat, organic vegetables + chickens
  2. **Huerta Las Mariposas** (Areguá) — organic vegetables, sells to restaurants
  3. **Finca Mamy** (Caacupé, 60 km) — cheese + dairy + eggs
  4. **Cooperativa 8 de Diciembre** (Paraguarí town) — small-scale, organic vegetables
  5. **Various Mennonite colonies** (Chaco, 200 km) — bulk dairy + cheese
- **Asunción wholesale (Mercado de Abasto):** All produce, meats, dry goods — 1.5 hours drive from RV
- **Specialty items (cheese, wine, olive oil):** Import via Asunción
- **Meat (beef, chicken, pork):** Local Paraguarí farms + Asunción supply
- **Prices (2025 estimates):**
  - Local vegetables: 50-70% of Asunción prices
  - Local beef: 60-70% of imported
  - Cheese (local Mennonite): $5-8/kg
  - Organic vegetables: 30-50% premium over conventional
- **Supply delivery:** Most vendors deliver within 50 km if order is sufficient

## Implications for the Project

- **Farm-to-table is achievable** with local supply (FT14 research confirms feasibility)
- **Restaurant cost structure:** ~30-35% food cost vs 25-30% urban norm (slightly higher due to transport)
- **Y1 food sourcing strategy:**
  - Q1-Q2: Establish relationships with 5-6 local producers
  - Q3+: Direct contracts for steady supply
  - Moestuin: Start 100-200 m² pilot (per A08) for on-site herbs + greens
- **Sustainability angle:** Sourcing 50%+ of restaurant food locally = major marketing story (relevance to EU eco-tourists)
- **Risk:** Some local producers are seasonal (no winter greenhouse → gaps in winter supply)
- **Opportunity:** Build a **"RV producer cooperative"** — multiple small farmers contracted for exclusive supply, with RV helping them scale
