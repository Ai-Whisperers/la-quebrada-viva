## Standards & regulations

**DINAPI** (Dirección Nacional de Propiedad Intelectual, not the right body for septic) — actually the relevant regulator is **MADES** (Ministerio del Ambiente y Desarrollo Sostenible) + **SENASA** (animal health) + **Municipalidad de Escobar** for discharge permits.

**PY septic standards:**
- **Ley 716/95 + Decreto 18.188/97** — environmental protection, including wastewater
- **Resolución 222/02** — discharge standards for liquid effluents to water bodies
- **Municipalidad** — local permit for septic installation + operation

**For a 5-cabin Phase 1:** likely 5 × 4 = 20-person equivalent load. Standard design = biodigester + reed-bed polishing.

## Septic system design for LQV

**Biodigester (primary treatment):**
- Capacity: 1,500-2,000 L per cabin (4-person avg occupancy)
- Retention time: 3-5 days
- Outlet: clarified effluent (BOD 60-70% removed)
- Cost per cabin: Gs. 2,500,000-4,000,000 (incl. excavation, tank, piping)

**Reed-bed (polishing):**
- Constructed wetland, 2-3 m² per person
- Substrate: gravel + reed plants (Phragmites) + sand cap
- Effluent: BOD 85-90% removed → meets PY discharge standards
- Cost per m²: Gs. 350,000-600,000 (construction + plants)
- For 20 persons: 50 m² wetland = Gs. 17,500,000-30,000,000

**Combined cost for 5 cabins:**
- 5 × biodigester @ Gs. 3M = Gs. 15M
- 1 shared reed-bed (50 m²) @ Gs. 20M = Gs. 20M
- **Total: Gs. 35M ≈ $4,900 USD**

**For full 30-cabin Phase 1 (120 person equivalent):**
- 30 × biodigester @ Gs. 3M = Gs. 90M
- 3 shared reed-beds (50 m² each, 150 m² total) @ Gs. 60M = Gs. 60M
- **Total: Gs. 150M ≈ $21,000 USD**

This matches the €60,000 (~$65K) "Water/septic/sewer" line item in Wes's financial model — but at the high end, so the model might be under-budgeted for the full 30-cabin scope. Phase 1 (5 cabins) is well within budget.

## Compliance checklist

For LQV Phase 1 permit:
- [ ] Septic design by licensed engineer (required for >20 persons)
- [ ] Discharge permit from Municipalidad de Escobar
- [ ] MADES notification (for >50 m² wetland construction)
- [ ] Annual sludge pumping contract (1,000 Gs/cabin/year local rate)
- [ ] Discharge water quality testing (BOD, COD, fecal coliforms — annual)
- [ ] Sludge disposal at municipal treatment facility or licensed site

## Comparison: biodigester + reed-bed vs sewer connection

**Biodigester + reed-bed (off-grid):**
- Pros: Off-grid, no monthly fee, no municipal dependency
- Cons: Capital cost, maintenance (annual pumping), biological process can fail if misused
- Best for: Phase 1, rural sites far from municipal sewer

**Municipal sewer connection (if available):**
- Pros: Off-site responsibility, no on-site maintenance
- Cons: Connection fee (often Gs. 1-3M per cabin), monthly bill (Gs. 50-150k/cabin/month), requires municipal infrastructure
- Best for: Phase 2-3 if Escobar municipal sewer reaches the property

**For LQV (rural Escobar, 120km from Asunción):** municipal sewer unlikely. **Off-grid biodigester + reed-bed is the only viable option.** Wes should plan this in from day 1.
