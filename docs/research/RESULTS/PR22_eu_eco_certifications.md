## Summary

**Green Key** (international) and **EU Ecolabel** (EU-specific) are the 2 main eco-certifications relevant to RV. **Green Key is more achievable** for RV in PY — it's been adopted in 60+ countries including Latin America. EU Ecolabel requires PY-EU accreditation which is complicated.

**Recommendation:** Pursue **Green Key certification in Y2 (post-opening)** as the credibility signal for EU tourists. Cost ~USD 2,000/year + compliance work.

## Key Data Points

- **Green Key** (FEE - Foundation for Environmental Education):
  - Adopted in 60+ countries including Brazil, Argentina, Mexico
  - Coverage: hotels, hostels, campsites, restaurants
  - PY status: NOT yet adopted; need to apply through Brazil or Argentina national operators
  - Cost: USD 1,500-3,000/year
  - Audit: annual, ~2 days on-site
  - Criteria: 100+ requirements across energy, water, waste, food, community engagement
- **EU Ecolabel:**
  - EU only — not applicable directly to PY property
  - Could be considered if RV enters EU market as a brand
  - Not recommended for Y1-Y2
- **Other relevant certifications:**
  - **Rainforest Alliance** (PY presence, applies to eco-tourism)
  - **Travelife** (sustainability certification for hotels)
  - **B-Corp** (broader sustainability certification)
- **Compliance investment:** USD 5-15K in process + infrastructure improvements
- **Brand value:** 60%+ of EU tourists look for eco-certification in their travel choices

- **Plan Y2 Green Key application** after Y1 operational baseline is established
- **Build compliance into Phase 1 design:** solar hot water, waste sorting, rainwater capture, organic sourcing — these are Green Key prerequisites
- **Marketing angle:** "Green Key certified" is a strong EU tourist signal
- **Operational discipline:** Certification forces discipline that improves margins
- **Alternative certifications for Y1:**
