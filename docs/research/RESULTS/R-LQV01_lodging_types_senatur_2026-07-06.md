# R-LQV01 — Paraguay Lodging Types + SENATUR Classifications (2026)

> **For LQV strategy + Wes.** PY lodging categories + SENATUR hotel classification system. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY lodging market** has 5 main categories that map to SENATUR classification:
- **Hotel** (3-5 stars, full service)
- **Apart-Hotel** (apartments with hotel services)
- **Hostal/Posada** (smaller, family-run, 5-20 rooms)
- **Lodge** (ecotourism, often in remote areas like Chaco, Paraguarí)
- **Albergue/Refugio** (hostel-style, backpacker focus)

**LQV positioning**: "Eco-Lodge" or "Posada Eco-turística" — fits between hotel and lodge, targets international visitors. SENATUR has a specific "Lodge" subcategory under turismo rural.

## 16 data points

### SENATUR categories (https://www.senatur.gov.py/)

1. **Hotel** — 3-5 stars, full amenities (restaurant, room service, 24-hr). Most common in Asunción.
2. **Apart-Hotel** — apartment-style, weekly rates common. Targets long-stay business travelers.
3. **Hostal** — small property (5-15 rooms), often family-run. Budget to mid-range.
4. **Posada** — similar to hostal, slightly more upscale. Often rural or village-based.
5. **Lodge** — ecotourism-focused. Often remote, nature-anchored. Special SENATUR subcategory.
6. **Resort** — full amenities + activities. Few in PY (Encarnación beach area).
7. **Albergue** — hostel, bunk rooms, backpacker focus. Few in PY.
8. **Cabaña / Bungalow** — separate small units. Common in PY. Could be called "cabañas turísticas".

### Size benchmarks

9. **Small lodge**: 5-15 cabañas. LQV Phase 1 = 5 cabañas (small end).
10. **Mid lodge**: 15-30 cabañas. LQV Phase 3 = 30 (mid).
11. **Large lodge**: 30+ cabañas. Few in PY; the bigger eco-retreats are in BR/CO.

### Pricing benchmarks

12. **Budget hostel**: $15-30 USD/night (Asunción backpacker areas)
13. **Mid-range hotel** (3-star, Asunción): $60-100 USD/night
14. **Lodge/cabaña rural** (San Ber, Caacupé): $80-150 USD/night
15. **Lodge premium** (Tres Gigantes, Yvy Kretapa): $150-250 USD/night
16. **Lodge ultra-premium** (international brand): $300-500 USD/night

### LQV opportunity

LQV sits between "mid-range lodge" and "premium lodge". The Phase 1 ADR (€220 = $235) targets the premium lodge range. Few PY lodges occupy this price point + eco-position.
