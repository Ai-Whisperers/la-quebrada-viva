## Summary

There are **no direct competitors** to RV's positioning (cob/bamboo eco-cabin retreat in PY's eastern region with quebrada + lapacho forest). There are **8-12 indirect competitors** in 3 categories: (1) eco-lodges in PY (different locations), (2) San Bernardino-area lodgings (weekend retreat market), (3) MERCOSUR eco-tourism destinations (Brazil Bonito, Argentina Ibera).

**Key insight:** RV's positioning is **not competitive — it's distinctive**. No one is doing cob + bamboo + quebrada + lapacho + EU tourist positioning. The risk is not "competitors take our market" — it's "we don't find the market fast enough".

## Key Data Points

- **Direct competitors in PY:** 0 (none doing cob + bamboo + quebrada + EU positioning)
- **PY eco-lodges (different positioning):**
  - **Reserva Natural del Bosque Mbaracayú** (Canindeyú) — NGO reserve, no lodging
  - **Luna del Sur** (Caazapá) — eco-lodge, $90-150/night, basic cabins
  - **Yvytu Guazú** (Misiones) — bird-watching lodge, $120-180/night
  - **Posada Guaraní** (San Ber/Areguá) — German-style inn, $80-130/night
  - **5-6 small eco-camps** in Chaco Mennonite colonies, $60-100/night
- **San Bernardino lodging:** ~10-15 hotels + Airbnbs, $50-150/night, primarily Asunción weekend market
- **MERCOSUR eco-competition:**
  - **Bonito (Brazil, MS):** Major eco-tourism hub, $200-400/night, 2.5 hr flight from Asunción
  - **Iberá (Argentina):** Estancia + wildlife, $300-500/night, 4 hr drive + ferry
  - **Iguazú (Brazil/AR):** Famous falls, $150-300/night, 5 hr drive
- **RV pricing positioning:** $200-280/night — mid-to-upper range vs PY market, but lower than Bonito/Iberá
- **Market gap:** No PY property combines cob + bamboo + quebrada + EU positioning
