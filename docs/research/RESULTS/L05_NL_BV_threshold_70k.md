## Answer

The €70,000 threshold refers to the Dutch **box 1 income tax** boundary where it becomes mandatory to structure income through a Besloten Vennootschap (BV) instead of an Eenmanszaak (sole proprietorship).

**Current rule (as of 2026-01-01, subject to change):**
- **Eenmanszaak (IB):** Income taxed progressively at box 1 rates (37.07% in bracket 1 up to 49.50% in top bracket, 2026 rates)
- **BV:** Corporate income tax at **19%** on first €200,000 profit, 25.8% above
- **No legal threshold** for *having* a BV; the €70k number is a practical breakeven point where the BV's lower corporate tax + dividend tax becomes more efficient than the IB's high progressive rates
