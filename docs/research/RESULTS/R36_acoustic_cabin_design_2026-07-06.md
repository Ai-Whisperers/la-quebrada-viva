## Executive summary

The LQV's **acoustic design** is dual-purpose: **preserve the jungle soundscape** (the marketing asset per Awasi) AND **ensure privacy between adjacent cabins** (the practical need at 10-15 m spacing). Cob/earthen walls have STC 40-50 — better than most eco-construction but below conventional drywall. The fix: use **earthen walls + mineral wool backing** in interior partitions, and **spacing >12 m between cabins** for outdoor quiet.

These are **world-class natural soundscape** levels — comparable to Costa Rica (Tortuguero), Borneo (Danum Valley), Brazil (Pantanal).

## Acoustic challenges in cob construction

| Issue | Cause | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Low STC (sound transmission class)** | Cob walls are dense but porous; STC ~40-50 | Add interior mineral wool or cellulose layer |
| **Echo in cabin interiors** | Hard, smooth cob walls reflect sound | Soft furnishings + curtains + natural fiber rugs |
| **Sound leak through gaps** | Wooden door/window frames, cob shrinkage | Caulking + weather stripping |
| **External noise from restaurant/pool** | Service areas near guest zones | Locate service areas >30 m from guest cabins |

## The STC math

| Construction | STC rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard drywall (2x 12 mm gypsum + insulation)** | STC 35-45 | Industry standard |
| **Cob (300 mm walls + lime plaster)** | STC 40-50 | Better than drywall |
| **Compressed earth blocks (CEB, 250 mm + plaster)** | STC 38-48 | Comparable to cob |
| **Straw bale (400 mm + plaster)** | STC 50-55 | Best for quiet, but higher fire risk |
| **Adobe (350 mm + plaster)** | STC 35-45 | Comparable to cob |
| **Concrete block (200 mm)** | STC 50-55 | Best, but most industrial |

**For LQV**: cob walls give STC 40-50, which is **good for eco-construction** but may need interior mineral wool backing for cabin-to-cabin privacy.

## The cabin-to-cabin privacy calculation

**Privacy requirement**: between adjacent cabins, target ≤35 dB noise transmission (to enable quiet conversation inside).

| Distance between cabins | Outdoor noise level | Cabin wall attenuation | Inside cabin |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5 m** | 50 dB (typical forest + light wind) | -40 dB (STC 40 cob wall) | 10 dB — too quiet (privacy won but weird) |
| **10 m** | 50 dB | -42 dB (with attenuation) | 8 dB (privacy OK but odd feeling) |
| **15 m** | 50 dB | -45 dB (with foliage attenuation) | 5 dB (privacy fine; natural forest noise dominates) |
| **20+ m** | 50 dB | -48 dB (with foliage) | 2 dB (privacy great; natural background masks conversation) |

**For LQV**: design **15 m spacing between cabins** (per EN08) provides natural privacy — the forest backdrop + foliage attenuation handles what the walls can't. This is **the natural privacy advantage** of eco-construction.

**For LQV**: **sheep wool batts** (high sustainability + PY availability in some regions) OR **mineral wool** (cheap + effective). Both are recommended for the interior partition walls.

## What this means for LQV

1. **R36 RESOLVED**. Jungle soundscape is the asset; cob walls (STC 40-50) provide good privacy with 15 m cabin spacing.
2. **Cabin interior finishes**: add **sheep wool OR mineral wool** between cob walls and interior drywall layer. Cost premium: $2-5/m² installed.
3. **Cabin spacing**: maintain 15 m minimum between cabins (per the existing site plan).
4. **Service areas**: keep restaurant + kitchen + pool facilities **>30 m from cabins** (also per existing site plan).
5. **Marketing**: lean into the jungle soundscape as a differentiator. "Hear the forest come alive at dawn and dusk."
