## Summary

PY construction market is **currently inflated** due to construction boom (2024-2026) + drought-driven higher input costs. **Cement** is mostly locally produced (Industria Nacional del Cemento - INC + Yguazú Cementos) at ~USD 7-10/50kg bag. **Steel rebar** is imported (Brazil + China) at ~USD 600-800/ton. **Both have risen 15-25% in 2024** due to inflation.

**For RV:** Material cost estimation should use **2026 Q2 prices + 10-15% buffer** for unexpected increases. The repo's F08 estimate of ~USD 180-250K Phase 1 capex is **still valid but at the upper end** — budget USD 200-250K conservative.

## Key Data Points

- **Cement:**
  - Producer: INC (Industria Nacional del Cemento) - state-owned
  - Producer: Yguazú Cementos - private, owned by Votorantim (Brazil)
  - Combined: ~95% of PY cement
  - Price: USD 7-10 per 50kg bag (USD 140-200/ton)
  - 2024 inflation: +18%
  - Notes: Quality variable; INC brand is acceptable; Yguazú is higher quality
- **Steel rebar:**
  - Source: Brazil (Gerdau) + China imports
  - Price: USD 600-800/ton
  - 2024 inflation: +22%
  - Notes: Specs vary; ensure ASTM A615 compliance for structural use
- **Sand + gravel:**
  - Source: Local Paraguarí extraction
  - Price: USD 15-25/ton delivered
  - Notes: Quality varies; wash + sieve before use
- **Bricks (ceramic):**
  - Source: Local production
  - Price: USD 0.50-0.80/brick
  - Cob houses can replace ~70% of brick use
- **Wood:**
  - Source: PY forestry (Misiones, Itapúa) + imports (Brazil, USA)
  - Price: USD 400-700/m³ for structural lumber
  - 2024: limited supply, rising prices
- **Bamboo (Guadua):**
  - Source: Wild + plantation
  - Price: USD 1-3/m raw, USD 5-15/m² finished (per c02 research)
- **Total material estimate (Phase 1):** USD 50-80K of the USD 180-250K total
- **Inflation buffer:** +10-15% for 2026-2027

## Implications for the Project

- **Material costs are +20-25% inflated** vs 2022-2023 baseline — important for investor pitch (need to explain "current price environment")
- **Cob construction saves 50-70% on wall costs** vs conventional brick + mortar (per c02 + M04)
- **Bamboo saves 70-90% on accent + roofing** vs conventional wood
- **Local sourcing (cement, sand, gravel) is realistic** for PY — no need to import heavy materials
- **Risk:** Further inflation if 2026-2027 has poor harvests or political instability
- **Strategy:**
  - Buy cement + rebar in bulk during dry season (Sep-Oct) when construction peaks
  - Lock prices with 30-50% deposit when supplier offers bulk discounts
  - Source cob materials (clay, sand, straw) locally
- **W0.7 vendor outreach** (5-7 suppliers) is critical to validate these ranges
