## Tax treaty status (NL ↔ PY)

**Important caveat:** As of 2026, the Netherlands and Paraguay do **NOT** have a comprehensive bilateral tax treaty. This means:
- Dutch investors in PY companies face default NL rules + default PY rules
- No treaty reduces withholding taxes
- Double taxation may occur on dividends/interest

**However:**
- NL has many bilateral treaties (90+ countries)
- PY has fewer (10-15 countries)
- **PY domestic law** has some relief (e.g. reduced withholding on dividends to non-residents in some cases)
- **NL unilateral relief** (deelnemingsvrijstelling) may apply for direct investments

## What this means for RV

### For Wes (NL resident, owns 50-100% of RV BVs)

**Direct holding of PY BVs:**
- Income from PY BVs (dividends, interest) taxed in PY
- PY withholding tax on dividends to non-residents: 5-15% (depends on participation, type)
- NL taxes Wes on world income (box 3: savings + box 2: substantial interest, box 1: salary)
- **Double taxation risk** on PY-source income

**Mitigation:**
- BV2 (NL holding) sits between Wes and BV3-5
- Wes invests in BV2 (NL) → BV2 invests in BV3-5 (PY)
- PY withholding applies at BV2 → BV3-5 level
- NL taxes BV2's income (participation exemption may apply)

**Or:** Use Dutch BV2 with **participation exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling)** — if Wes's BV2 holds ≥5% in BV3-5 and BV3-5 are operating companies, the dividend from BV3-5 to BV2 may be tax-free in NL.

**Important conditions:**
- BV3-5 must not be "passive" (must have real business activity)
- BV2 must be subject to NL corporate tax
- Some holding tests apply

### For Thijs

Thijs is a different person (likely NL or PY resident). His tax position is separate. May be Wes's 25% partner or separate. Get Dutch + PY tax advice for Thijs's specific situation.

### For Dutch investors in BV2

Same as Wes (NL resident, BV2 holds PY BVs).

## Specific considerations

**1. Withholding tax on dividends from PY to NL:**
- Default: 5% (on net income if participation > 30%) or 15%
- Treaty reduction: N/A (no treaty)
- Domestic reduction: possible if BV2 holds >75% of PY BV3-5
- **Effective rate: ~5-15% on dividends**

**2. NL corporate tax on BV2 income:**
- Standard rate: 19% (on first €200K) + 25.8% (above)
- Withholding credit for foreign tax: available
- Participation exemption: 100% if conditions met

**3. PY capital gains tax on property sale:**
- Property sale: 5% on gross sale price (PY law)
- Or 15% on net gain (whichever higher)
- No treaty relief available
- **Material for RV when Wes + Thijs sell**

**4. PY IRE on operational profits:**
- 10% IRE (commercial activities)
- 5% IRE (agricultural, real estate may qualify as small landowner)
- Most RV operations will be 10% IRE

**5. NL box 3 (savings) for Wes personally:**
- Taxed on deemed return from net wealth
- Real estate in NL: not relevant if RV is PY
- PY real estate: may be relevant for Dutch box 3 if Wes directly owns PY assets
- **Important: BV2 (NL holding) avoids this for Wes**
