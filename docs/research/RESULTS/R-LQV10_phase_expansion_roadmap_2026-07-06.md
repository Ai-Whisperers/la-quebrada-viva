# R-LQV10 — Construction Phase 2 + Expansion Strategy

> **For Wes.** Phase 2/3 expansion roadmap + phase-gate decisions. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**Phase 1 (Year 1)**: 5 cabins + restaurant + pool. €216K construction + €4M operational runway.
**Phase 2 (Year 2-3)**: 10-cabin total = +5 cabins. Cost: €90-120K. Trigger: Phase 1 reaches 50%+ occupancy + has positive word-of-mouth.
**Phase 3 (Year 3-5)**: 30-cabin total = +20 cabins. Cost: €3-4M. Trigger: Sonja's 2030 60th milestone + buyer debt-free.

**Key insight**: Phase 2/3 economics improve dramatically at scale. Fixed costs (manager, chef, marketing) spread across more cabins = ~€215/cabin/year at 30 cabins vs €1,500 at 5.

## 14 data points

### Phase expansion gates

1. **Phase 2 trigger**: 50%+ avg occupancy, 4+ consecutive months, + €100K+ bank reserve
2. **Phase 3 trigger**: 60%+ avg occupancy + Sonja milestone + €500K+ bank reserve + investor capital or formal debt
3. **Slowdown trigger**: <30% avg occupancy for 6 months → pause expansion, optimize Phase 1

### Capacity per phase

4. **Phase 1 (5 cab)**: 3,650 cabin-nights/year max. Realistic ~30-40% occ = 1,100-1,500 nights
5. **Phase 2 (10 cab)**: 7,300 nights max. 1,600-2,200 nights realistic
6. **Phase 3 (30 cab)**: 21,900 nights max. 6,500-9,000 nights realistic

### Capex per phase

7. **Phase 1**: €216K construction + €4M operational runway = €4.2M total
8. **Phase 2**: 5 additional cob cabins at €18K each = €90K. Plus 1 more restaurant addon if needed = €100-150K
9. **Phase 3**: 20 more cob cabins = €360K. Plus major upgrades (wellness center, events infrastructure) = €3-4M total
10. **Per-cabin cost decreases with scale**: cob cabin at 30 units = €12-15K each (vs €18K at 5 units)

### Revenue per phase (per FINANCIAL_MODEL.xlsx)

11. **Phase 1 Year 1**: ~$100K cabin revenue (low occupancy ramp-up) + ~$35K F&B = €120K
12. **Phase 1 Year 5** (running 5 cab, mature): ~$150-200K cabin + $70K F&B = $250-300K = €235-280K
13. **Phase 3 Year 5** (30 cab, mature): ~$1.3M cabin + $455K F&B = $1.75M = €1.65M
14. **EBITDA at Phase 3 mature**: 35-45% margin (industry standard for lodging) = €580-745K/year

## Strategic decisions for Wes

- **Don't over-build**: Phase 1 success MUST come before Phase 2 commitment
- **Use Phase 2 cash flow**: reinvest 80% of Phase 1 profit in Phase 2 capex
- **Phase 3 likely requires debt**: estimated €1-1.5M financing need. Wes's options: NL mortgage on PY land (hard), private investor, bank loan against cash flow
- **Plan for the bump**: Phase 1 → Phase 3 has operational stress + cash risk. Have 6-12 mo runway buffer
