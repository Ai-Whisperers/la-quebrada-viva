## Pool design (natural pool, professional)

### Pool shell
- Liner: EPDM rubber (most reliable)
- Alternative: concrete + bentonite + waterproof plaster
- Substrate: clean washed gravel + sand
- Depth: 1.5-2.0m (deep enough for swimming, shallow enough for safety)

### Filtration system
- Skimmer boxes (collect surface debris)
- Pump basket pre-filters
- **Biological filter zone** (reeds + gravel + sand, 30% of pool area)
- UV sterilizer (kills pathogens without chemicals)
- Optional: sand pre-filter (for sediment)

### Reeds (the bio-filter)
- Common reed (Phragmites australis)
- Cattail (Typha domingensis)
- Water mint
- Pickerel weed

### Water source
- Rain-fed (cisterns per F15)
- Backup: well (if dry season)
- Replenish: ~10% of pool volume/month (evaporation + splash)

## Why natural pools (vs chlorinated)

**Marketing differentiation:**
- "Swim in a living pond" = unique experience
- No chlorine smell, no red eyes
- Healing mineral waters vibe
- Higher ADR justified

**Operational cost difference:**
- Chlorine: $50-100/week in chemicals + testing
- Natural: $50-100/year in UV lamps + testing
- **$2,000-4,000/year savings**

**Risks:**
- Without chlorine + UV: pathogen risk (E. coli, etc.)
- Critical: weekly bacteria testing
- UV is the main insurance
- If bacteria test fails: pool closed until cleared

## Recommendation: build natural pool for Fase 1

- 1 pool (50-80m²), natural water, no chlorine, reed-bed
- UV + weekly bacteria testing
- Operator training (the pool maintenance is different from chlorinated)
- $5-9K build + $700-1,200/year operating
- ADR justification: 2-4 month payback (per M21 result file analysis)
