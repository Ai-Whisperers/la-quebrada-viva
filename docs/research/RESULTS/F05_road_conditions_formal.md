# F05 — Road conditions + seasonal issues (formal)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Needs W1.2 site visit for verification.

## Why this matters

**RV's site access affects:**
- Construction deliveries (timber, concrete, equipment)
- Guest arrivals (first impression)
- Emergency response (medical, fire)
- Daily staff commute

**The 7 km of dirt road from the main road to the property** is the single most fragile part of the access chain.

## Known road patterns in rural Paraguay

**Dry season (May-Oct):**
- Road is passable
- Construction traffic OK
- Guests can use 2WD cars
- Dust issues (residents typically water the road)

**Wet season (Nov-Mar):**
- Road may become impassable for 2WD
- Construction delays common
- 4WD needed for guests
- Some roads flood (river crossings)

**Transitional periods (Apr-May, Oct-Nov):**
- Mixed conditions
- Occasional closures
- Best to plan major deliveries before or after
