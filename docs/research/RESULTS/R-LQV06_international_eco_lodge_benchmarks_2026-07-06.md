# R-LQV06 — International Eco-Lodge Benchmarks (competitor pricing + positioning)

> **For Wes.** Comparable eco-lodges in LATAM + Europe + Caribbean. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**Eco-lodges pricing** (per cabin/couple/night, 2026):
- **Nicaragua/Costa Rica mid-range**: $80-150 USD
- **Colombia premium**: $120-250 USD
- **Mexico Yucatan**: $150-300 USD
- **Argentina/Chile Patagonia**: $100-220 USD
- **Caribbean premium**: $300-600 USD
- **Indonesia (Bali)**: $40-100 USD

**LQV ADR €220 = $235 USD** sits in the **Latin premium** range. Achievable for differentiated positioning but requires the "premium mid-market" experience (food, guides, unique activities).

## 14 data points

### Latin America eco-lodges (most relevant benchmarks)

1. **Hacienda Mérida / Yucatan**: luxury eco-lodge. $200-400 USD/night
2. **Luna's Lodge / Boquete Panama**: mountains eco. $150-250 USD
3. **Casa Galán / Medellín**: Colombian boutique eco. $120-200 USD
4. **Chácara das Pedras / Bonito BR**: Pantanal-style eco-lodge. $100-180 USD
5. **Estancia La Oriental / Patagonia AR**: estancia-style. $150-300 USD
6. **Bahía Lodge / Salvador BR**: beach eco. $80-140 USD

### Caribbean (premium-but-relevant for positioning reference)

7. **Banyan Tree Mayakoba** (Riviera Maya): $700-1500 USD. Ultra-premium reference
8. **Tubohotel / Mexico**: $80-150 USD. Capsule-style unique design
9. **Awasi Costa Rica**: $700-1200 USD. Premium eco-lodge reference

### Europe (different market but comparable in ethos)

10. **Treehotel / Sweden**: $500-1000 USD. Unique tree-cabin concept
11. **Whitepod / Switzerland**: $400-700 USD. Eco-luxury pods
12. **Hecla / Iceland**: $200-400 USD. Lava-formed eco
13. **Cabin Hotels / Norway**: $200-400 USD. Fjord-side cabin
14. **Eden Project / UK**: $200-350 USD. Educational eco-tourism

### Best benchmarks for LQV positioning

**Pricing-wise**: Luna's Lodge, Casa Galán, Estancia La Oriental ($120-300 USD range)
**Experience-wise**: Whitepod, Treehotel (unique cabin concept)
**Marketing-wise**: Awasi, Banyan Tree (premium positioning without compromise)

LQV's competitive advantage vs all of these: **less developed (whitespace), lower land cost, lower labor cost**. Should be able to deliver $200-300 experience at $100-150 cost.
