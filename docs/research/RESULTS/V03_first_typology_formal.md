## Why this is the prototype

**V03 (the cob house) defines:**
- RV's aesthetic (cob + bamboo + bottle + lime + stone)
- RV's technical approach (low-tech, sustainable, breathable)
- RV's cost model (per m², including premium finishes)
- RV's local sourcing strategy (PY materials + Wes's NL+BR craft network)
- RV's master-rule (no cement plaster, no chlorine, no right angles)

**Any new typology in RV derives from this prototype's principles.**

## The prototype's 10 design rules (per CLAUDE.md)

1. **No right angles in cob walls** — organic sculpted forms only
2. **No cement plaster on cob** — always lime
3. **No standing water** — dengue protocol
4. **Earthen walls never touch ground** — raised stone foundation
5. **Wide overhangs (90cm+)** on all sides
6. **Passive cooling (≤35°C)** — corredor + cross-ventilation
7. **Outage-proof critical systems** — micro-hydro + LiFePO4
8. **Culturally Paraguayan** — corredor, tatakuá, courtyard, lapacho
9. **Solar on separate steel frame** — never on living roof
10. **All cisterns mosquito-proofed** — 0.5mm stainless mesh

- **Total: $18,040 USD**
