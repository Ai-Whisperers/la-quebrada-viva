# R12 — SENATUR Tourism Statistics 2018-2025

> **For Wesley van de Camp + Ivan.** Sources: SENATUR, Asunción Times, IP Paraguay, UN Tourism, as of 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

Paraguay tourism hit **3.66 million international visitors in 2025** (+91% YoY), generating **$1.42B USD** in revenue. Argentina dominates the source market (80%), Brazil second (10%), then US (1.1%), rest of world 9%. **EU market is under-penetrated** — no EU country in the top 4. This is both the opportunity (whitespace) and the challenge (no existing EU tourist pipeline to tap into).

### Source market mix (2025)

| **Argentina** | **79.98%** | Dominant — Iguazú falls + cheap shopping + border proximity |

**Critical for LQV**: EU tourists are **<1% of PY arrivals**. This means:
- White space for an EU-targeted eco-retreat (no existing supply/demand balance)
- But marketing cost is higher (need to build the market)
- Wes's bet is on capturing a slice of the medical tourism + eco-tourism EU niche

### Hotel occupancy by region

- **Asunción**: 60-70% average, 80%+ in business hotels
- **San Bernardino**: 40-60% weekends, 20-30% weekdays (seasonal)
- **Caacupé**: peaks Dec 8 (Virgen de Caacupé pilgrimage, 500K visitors in 1 week)
- **Paraguarí department** (where Escobar is): no specific data, estimated <20% outside weekends/holidays
