# W20 — Staff retention strategy (formal)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Critical for long-term success.

## What W20 is

**RV's staff retention challenge:**
- Phase 1-2 staff hired in 2026-2027
- Need them to stay 5+ years (per Sonja's 60th weekend)
- Hospitality turnover in PY is typically 30-50% per year
- Need strategies beyond salary to keep good staff

## Retention strategies

### 1. Above-market salary (foundation)
- Pay 10-20% above local market rate
- Per W01-W08: Gs. 3-4M/month for general staff, 5-7M for chef
- Slightly above average: attracts + keeps good people
- Cost: $2-4K/year per staff member above market

### 2. Aguinaldo (1 month salary at year-end)
- Per W11 - mandatory
- 8.3% payroll overhead (already in cost model)

### 3. Vacaciones (12-30 days)
- Per W12 - mandatory
- Better than most PY hospitality (which often only gives 6-12 days)

### 4. Profit-sharing (post-Y3)
- 1-2% of net profit distributed annually
- Per L32 jubilación: equity-partner program
- Aligns staff with project success

### 5. On-site housing (Fase 1+)
- 1-2 casitas for key staff (per W18)
- Rent + utilities free
- Big retention value
- Cost: $5-15K/year

### 6. Equity-partner (Y2+)
- 1-2% BV2 equity per key staff
- Vesting over 4-5 years
- Big retention value
- Cost: 10-30% of RV's profit (but only if successful)

### 7. Training + development (per W15)
- Hospitality training in PY (formal programs)
- Career growth path
- Subsidies for external courses
- Cost: $500-2,000/year per staff

### 8. Health insurance supplement (per L31)
- Private health insurance (~Gs. 300-500K/month per person)
- Better than IPS alone
- Cost: $1,200-2,400/year per staff

### 9. Family-friendly policies
- Sick child days (mandatory per PY law but RV more generous)
- School transportation (if staff family nearby)
- Vacation during school breaks
- Cost: variable, mostly goodwill

### 10. Long-term career path
- Year 1: line staff
- Year 2-3: senior staff / shift lead
- Year 3-5: department manager
- Year 5+: operations partner
- Cost: structured compensation, not direct

## ROI calculation

**Per W18 + W20 (combined staff engagement):**

If RV's retention rate is:
- 50% (PY hospitality average): 13 staff leave/year, $200K replacement cost (recruit + train + lost productivity)
- 80% (target with W20 strategies): 5 staff leave/year, $80K replacement cost
- 90% (excellent retention): 2-3 staff leave/year, $50K replacement cost

**Investment in W20 strategies:** ~$5,000/year (Fase 1) to ~$100K/year (full Phase 1)
**Savings in replacement cost:** $100-150K/year at 80% retention, $150-200K/year at 90% retention

**ROI: 10-20x** on the W20 investment
