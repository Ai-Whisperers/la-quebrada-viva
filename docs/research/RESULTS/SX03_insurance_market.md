## Insurance types LQV needs

**Per L22 (insurance minimums):**
- Property (fire + storm)
- Liability (3rd party)
- Business interruption
- Workers comp (IPS + supplement)
- Vehicle
- Cyber
- Umbrella

**For each, what's actually available in PY:**

### 1. Property (fire + storm)
- **Local PY insurers (Mapfre, La Consolidada, Sancor):** offer but with restrictions
  - High deductibles (10-15% of value)
  - Coverage limits (often < €500K)
  - Fire coverage excludes some natural events
  - Premium: 0.5-1% of value/year
- **International (Marsh, Aon, WTW):** can write but with high premiums
  - Coverage: full
  - Premium: 1-2% of value/year
  - Deductible: 5-10% of value
- **Recommended:** International broker for primary + local for backup
- **Total annual cost: $15-25K for LQV Fase 1**

### 2. Liability
- **All major insurers** offer 3rd party liability
- Coverage: €1-5M per occurrence
- Premium: €1-3K/year
- Recommended: €2M coverage, $1.5K/year

### 3. Business interruption
- **PY insurers:** limited coverage
- **International:** better
- Premium: $1-3K/year
- Recommended: 6-12 months revenue coverage, $2K/year

### 4. Workers comp (IPS supplement)
- **IPS:** mandatory, covers basics
- **Private supplement:** available, $50-200/month per staff
- Recommended: per-staff private health insurance, $1-2K/year per staff

### 5. Vehicle
- **PY insurers:** standard
- Coverage: full (collision + liability)
- Premium: 3-5% of vehicle value/year
- Recommended: per L17 (used Hilux @ $30K), $1K/year

### 6. Cyber
- **Most insurers** offer
- Coverage: €500K-1M
- Premium: $300-600/year
- Recommended: $500/year

### 7. Umbrella
- **Available from international brokers**
- Coverage: €5M+
- Premium: $1-2K/year
- Recommended: $1.5K/year

## Critical gaps in PY insurance market

**Gap 1: Eco-lodge specialty insurance**
- PY insurers don't have actuarial models for eco-tourism
- International insurers do, but with high premiums
- Workaround: 4-BV cascade isolates risk

**Gap 2: Reforestation / habitat insurance**
- No specific product
- Workaround: Insurance against operational interruption

**Gap 3: Wedding event insurance (Fase 2-3)**
- Limited availability in PY
- International brokers can write
- Workaround: Self-insurance reserve

**Gap 4: Pandemic insurance (L22 - NOT COVERED)**
- Standard exclusion
- Workaround: 3-6 month USD buffer + flexible business model
