# M14 — Guest journey optimization

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Operational design.

## What M14 is

**RV's guest experience from discovery → booking → arrival → stay → checkout → repeat:**

## The full journey

### 1. Discovery (awareness → interest)
**Where guests find RV:**
- Booking.com / Airbnb search
- Instagram posts (per M16)
- Google search ("eco resort Paraguay")
- TripAdvisor recommendations
- Personal referrals (Wes's network, San Bernardino)
- Travel blogs / YouTube

**Conversion goal:** click-through to RV.com
**RV's tools:** SEO-optimized website, social media, Booking.com listing, TripAdvisor presence

### 2. Research (consideration → evaluation)
**What guests do:**
- Read the RV website (cabins, food, location)
- Check reviews on Booking, TripAdvisor, Google
- Compare with other PY eco-lodges
- Contact RV (WhatsApp, email) for questions
- Talk to friends who may have visited

**Conversion goal:** booking
**RV's tools:** high-quality photos, clear pricing, instant chat response, FAQ

### 3. Booking (decision → commitment)
**Path options:**
- Booking.com (commissions to Booking)
- Direct (no commission, but no platform visibility)
- Airbnb (mid-commission, different audience)

**RV's tools:** Cloudbeds for channel management, direct booking via own website, multi-channel sync

### 4. Pre-arrival (anticipation → preparation)
**Communication:**
- Booking confirmation + welcome packet
- 1 week before arrival: detailed info (driving directions, what to bring, weather, activities)
- 24 hours before: check-in time, contact for any questions
- Optional: pre-arrival consultation (dietary, accessibility, special requests)

**RV's tools:** automated emails (Cloudbeds), WhatsApp Business API, pre-arrival guide PDF

### 5. Arrival (first impression → orientation)
**RV's protocol:**
- Welcome at the gate/entrance
- 15-minute orientation tour
- Cabin walk-through
- Property map + activity guide
- Welcome drink/snack (local fruit)
- Schedule review (optional activities, meal times)

**Tools:** welcome packet, property map, schedule, friendly staff

### 6. Stay (engagement → delight)
**Daily experience:**
- Breakfast (homemade, PY + European options)
- Optional morning activity (forest walk, swim, volunteer)
- Lunch (fresh, local)
- Afternoon: explore, relax, pool, family time
- Optional evening activity (campfire, stargazing, music)
- Dinner (multi-course, wine pairing optional)
- Quiet hours after 22:00

**Tools:** schedule, activity guide, dinner reservation system (Cloudbeds)

### 7. Checkout (last impression → review)
**RV's protocol:**
- Final breakfast
- Quick property check-out
- Thank you + photo opportunity
- Email follow-up with photo from stay
- 1 week later: review request (per M15)

**Tools:** checkout form, photo capture, follow-up email

### 8. Post-stay (engagement → loyalty)
**After stay:**
- Review request (per M15)
- Newsletter signup
- 1-year anniversary email
- Special offer for return visit
- Sonja's personal touch (birthday wishes, etc.)

**Tools:** email marketing, CRM (Cloudbeds has it)

## Per-stage optimization

### Discovery → booking
- High-quality photos (every cabin, food, pool)
- SEO-friendly blog posts (eco-tourism, PY travel, family destinations)
- Active social media (per M16)
- TripAdvisor + Google Maps presence

### Pre-arrival
- Multi-channel confirmation (email + WhatsApp + SMS)
- Personal touch (Sonja's voice for local guests)
- Driving directions in local language + GPS
- Anticipate questions (food allergies, kids, accessibility)

### Arrival
- First 5 minutes: warmest welcome
- First hour: orientation, no pressure
- First meal: showcase PY food + European quality
- First night: rest, no programming

### Stay
- Quality (not quantity) of interactions
- Responsive to requests (within 1 hour)
- Surprise + delight moments (treats, special activities)
- Privacy respected (no forced programming)

### Checkout
- Genuine thank you (not transactional)
- Photo opportunity
- Easy payment (no surprise fees)
- Clear way to follow up

### Post-stay
- Review request (per M15)
- Newsletter (monthly, not weekly)
- 1-year anniversary email
- Loyalty program (return discount, family discount)
