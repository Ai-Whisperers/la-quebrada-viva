# BD04 — On-site wastewater treatment options (Fase 1)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Operational detail for septic + reed-bed.

## What BD04 is

**Per M08 (septic) + L21 (SENATUR) + R01 (fire plan):**
- RV's 5 cabins + restaurant + reception = ~25-30 people equivalent
- PY regulations require proper wastewater treatment
- On-site (no municipal sewer) is the only option

## Treatment options

### Option 1: Traditional biodigester + reed bed
- Per M08: biodigester (anaerobic) + reed bed (aerobic + plant filtration)
- PY regulations satisfied
- Maintenance: periodic sludge pumping
- Cost: $3-5K initial + $500/year maintenance
- **Recommended for RV**

### Option 2: Biorock / advanced biofilter
- Biorock: small chamber with media
- Lower maintenance than septic + reed
- Higher cost: $8-12K
- Less natural aesthetic
- Not recommended for RV's eco-positioning

### Option 3: Constructed wetland (large)
- Larger reed bed (no tank)
- Lower cost ($2-3K) + more natural
- More land needed (30-50m²)
- Better for Fase 1 with 5 cabins
- **Could combine with Option 1**

### Option 4: Composting toilets (alternative)
- No water needed per toilet
- Less wastewater generated
- Lower cost ($2-4K for 5 cabin set)
- But: maintenance-intensive, cultural perception
- Not recommended for RV's target market (Europeans expect flush)

## Detailed design for RV

**Per M08 + L21:**

**For 5 cabins + restaurant + reception (25-30 people):**
- 1 biodigester (4,000-6,000L capacity)
- 1 reed bed (40-60m², plants: phragmites, cattail)
- 1 pump (1.5HP) for circulation
- UV sterilizer (final polish, optional but recommended)
- Discharge: subsurface drain field (not surface)

**Cost estimate:**
- Biodigester (fiberglass tank): $3,000-4,000
- Reed bed construction: $1,000-2,000 (labor + plants)
- UV sterilizer: $500-800
- Piping + drainage: $500-1,000
- Total: $5,000-7,800

**Maintenance:**
- Annual sludge pumping: $300-500
- UV lamp replacement: $100/year
- Plant care (annual trim): $100-200
- **Total: $500-800/year**

## Regulatory compliance

**Per L21 (SENATUR) + W0.7 (insurance):**
- MADES notification required for treatment system
- Bromatología approval for restaurant
- Periodic water quality testing (BOD, fecal coliforms)
- Annual report to MADES

**Cost of compliance:**
- One-time setup: $500-1,000
- Annual testing: $300-500
- Annual report: $100-200
- **Total: $400-700/year**

## Operational details

**Daily operations:**
- Automatic flow (no daily operator)
- Weekly visual check
- Monthly UV lamp check
- Quarterly BOD testing
- Annual sludge pumping (every 1-3 years depending on use)

**Failure modes:**
- Sludge accumulation (every 3-5 years)
- UV lamp burnout (replace yearly)
- Plant die-off (replace as needed)
- Drain field clogging (rare, professional cleaning)

**Backup plan:**
- Bypass valve to a holding tank (if main system fails)
- Trucked waste removal
- $500-1,000 emergency

## Specific to RV's site

**Per L21 (water permit) + L21 (SENATUR):**
