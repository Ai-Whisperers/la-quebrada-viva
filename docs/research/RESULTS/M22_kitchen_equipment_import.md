## PY customs basics

**Import tax structure (general):**
- **DIA (Declaración de Importación)** required for goods >$1,000 FOB
- **IVA (10%)** on CIF value
- **Ad valorem rates** by HS code: 0-22% depending on category
- **Antidumping duties** on some categories (China steel, certain textiles)
- **Despachante (customs broker) fee:** Gs. 200,000-500,000 per shipment

**Restaurant equipment (HS code 8419.81):**
- Standard ad valorem: ~14%
- Plus IVA 10%
- Plus despachante fee
- **Total import cost: CIF + 24% + broker**

## Recommended sourcing strategy

**Phase 1 (5 cabins, 1 small restaurant):**
- Buy **commercial-grade from PY local dealers** (not import):
  - Gastro-Haus (Asunción, German-owned, top PY restaurant supplier)
  - Brasitermo (Brazilian imports stocked in PY)
  - Most "PY" restaurant equipment is actually Brazil-sourced via Ciudad del Este
- Lead time: 1-3 weeks
- Cost: 15-30% above direct Brazil import, but no customs risk + local warranty

**Specific items worth importing (where local stock is poor):**
- High-end espresso machine (La Marzocco, etc.) — Brazil stock or direct import
- Commercial convection oven — Brazil stock sufficient
- Salamander/grill — local stock fine

**Phase 2-3 (full restaurant, 100+ covers):**
- At scale, import direct from manufacturer (China, Italy, Brazil) makes more sense
- Plan ahead 3-4 months for major equipment orders
- Consider a local commercial kitchen designer (Asunción: 2-3 firms)
