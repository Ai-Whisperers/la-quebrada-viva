## Summary

Paraguay has **30+ years of reforestation experience** through NGOs (Guyra Paraguay, WWF PY, Itaipu Binational), Mennonite colonies, and government programs. **Native species** are preferred over introduced species (Eucalyptus + Grevillea dominate commercial plantations but are ecologically inferior). **Costs:** USD 1,500-3,500/ha for native species restoration (includes nursery, planting, 3-year maintenance). **Funding sources:** World Bank, IDB (Inter-American Development Bank), EU green funds, voluntary carbon markets.

**For RV:** A phased reforestation plan is highly recommended — even 10 ha of native reforestation on the cleared portions of the 62 ha would provide significant biodiversity value + marketing story.

## Key Data Points

- **PY reforestation ecosystem:**
  - **Government:** INFONA (Instituto Forestal Nacional), MADES
  - **NGOs:** Guyra Paraguay (largest), WWF PY, ProNaturaleza
  - **Private:** Itaipu Binational (massive reforestation in eastern PY)
  - **Mennonite colonies:** Demonstrated large-scale reforestation
- **Native species priorities for PY eastern region:**
  - **Lapacho** (Tabebuia aurea + T. heptaphylla) — flagship tree
  - **Yvyra pytá** (Peltophorum dubium) — fast-growing shade tree
  - **Guatambú** (Balfourodendron riedelianum) — premium timber
  - **Cedro** (Cedrela odorata) — CITES-protected, high value
  - **Petereby** (Cordia trichotoma) — restoration species
- **Restoration cost:**
  - Native species: USD 1,500-3,500/ha (incl. nursery + planting + 3-yr maintenance)
  - Eucalyptus (commercial): USD 800-1,200/ha
  - Mixed approach (native understory + commercial canopy): USD 1,200-2,000/ha
- **Funding sources:**
  - **EU GREEN FORUM:** 2026 call for PY reforestation projects
  - **Voluntary carbon market:** USD 5-15/ton CO2 (verified credits)
  - **IDB sustainable forestry loans:** USD 50-500K per project
  - **Guyra Paraguay partnerships:** Cost-share + technical assistance
- **Income from reforestation (Y5+):**
  - Cedro + Guatambú timber: USD 200-400/m³ (at maturity)
  - Native seed + seedling sales: USD 50-200K/year potential
  - Carbon credits (Y3+): USD 50-150/ha/year (low end)
  - Eco-tourism value: PRICELESS for marketing
