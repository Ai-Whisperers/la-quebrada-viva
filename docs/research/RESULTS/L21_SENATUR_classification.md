## SENATUR classification process

**Step 1: Classify the property**

**Options:**
- **Alojamiento turístico rural (rural tourism lodging):** Best fit for RV
- **Hotel:** Urban-based, not RV
- **Hospedaje:** Smaller scale
- **Apartamento turístico:** Urban apartment, not RV

**RV's best fit:** Alojamiento turístico rural

**Step 2: Apply for SENATUR registration**

**Documents needed:**
- RUC for the operational BV
- Property title (escritura)
- Insurance policy (per F05)
- Fire safety plan (per R01)
- Tourist information (description, capacity, services)
- Photos of property
- Health/sanitation permits (SENAC + Bromatología for restaurant)
- Power of attorney for representative (Wes or Kiki)

**Fees:** Gs. 500,000-1,000,000 for application + lawyer
**Lead time:** 1-3 months

**Step 3: Annual operational compliance**
- Monthly tax filing (IVA + IRE)
- Annual financial statement
- Guest registration (tourism police requirement)
- Periodic inspection (sanitation + fire)

**Step 4: Apply for tourism-related tax benefits**

- **Tourism IVA:** 5% (lower than 10% general)
- **Tourism IRE:** 10% (same as general)
- **Tourism tax holiday:** Some categories get first-2-years tax exemption (verify with lawyer)
