# L26 — Anticipos (PY withholding tax) for vendor payments

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Confirmed for vendor payments.

## What anticipos are

**Anticipos = PY withholding tax on payments to non-PY residents or specific PY residents.**
- Applies when the operational BV pays vendors, contractors, or service providers
- Different rates based on type of payment
- The BV withholds and remits to SET

## Anticipos rates (2026, relevant to LQV)

| Type of payment | Anticipos rate |
|---|---:|
| **Services (general)** | 2% |
| **Construction services** | 2% |
| **Professional services (legal, accounting)** | 10% |
| **Rent (commercial)** | 6% |
| **Interest on loans** | varies, 1.5-15% |
| **Dividends to PY residents** | varies, 8% (gross-up) |
| **Dividends to non-PY residents** | 15% (often reducible) |
| **Sale of real estate (PY source)** | 5-15% |
| **Sale of shares (PY source)** | varies |

## How anticipos work in practice (LQV example)

**Scenario: LQV pays a Paraguayan carpenter Gs. 5,000,000 for a 3-month build phase**

1. **Invoice received:** Gs. 5,000,000 (gross)
2. **Anticipos calc:** 2% (construction services) × 5,000,000 = Gs. 100,000
3. **Withhold from payment:** Gs. 100,000 to SET, Gs. 4,900,000 to carpenter (net)
4. **Carpenter's gross income:** 5,000,000 (declared in his annual IRP)
5. **Anticipos credit:** the 100,000 counts toward his annual IRP

**Net effect on LQV:** no impact (it's a pass-through, just timing of cash flow)

**Cash flow impact on LQV:** the withheld amount stays with LQV for ~1-2 months before remittance to SET. Working capital effect.

## Operational SOP (LQV)

**For every vendor payment > Gs. 1,000,000:**
1. Issue invoice to vendor
2. Calculate anticipos based on service type
3. Withhold anticipos at payment
4. Remit to SET by the 15th of next month
5. File monthly with Marangatu

**For payments to non-PY residents (Wes personally, NL investors, EU suppliers):**
- Anticipos on dividends: 15% (often reducible to 0% with treaty)
- Anticipos on interest: 1.5-15%
- Anticipos on services: 2-10%
