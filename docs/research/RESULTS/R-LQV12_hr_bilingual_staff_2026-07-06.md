# R-LQV12 — LQV Bilingual Staff Sourcing + PY HR Market

> **For Wes.** Where to find + train NL/EN/ES bilingual staff for LQV. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY has a small but growing pool of trilingual staff** (ES + EN + NL/DE/FR) thanks to expat community + missionary presence. **LQV's Phase 1 hiring needs 3-4 bilingual frontline staff** + 6-8 PY-monolingual staff. Strategy: pay slightly above market + train in-house + recruit through specific channels (Universidad Católica, expat Facebook groups, Mennonite communities).

## 13 data points

### Channels to source bilingual staff

1. **Universidad Católica de Asunción** — strongest language programs. Career fairs + alumni network
2. **Universidad Americana** — private uni, business + hospitality focus. Good for managers
3. **Alumni PY** — LinkedIn alumni search. 2,000+ PY bilingual NL/EN professionals
4. **Expat Facebook groups** — "Asunción Expats", "NL in Paraguay", "DE in Paraguay". Reach 3,000+ active members
5. **CPT (Confederación de Padres/Tutores)** — expat family networks. Many bilingual spouses seeking remote/hybrid work
6. **Internships** — UCA + Americana + ESERP (Barcelona business school PY campus). 6-mo internships → hire
7. **Mennonite colonies** — Filadelfia, Loma Plata. Bilingual (DE + ES) workers eager for new opportunities
8. **Hospitality schools** — IHTT (Instituto de Hotelería, Turismo y Traducción) + Escuela de Hotelería del Litoral

### Hard-to-fill roles for LQV

9. **Bilingual guide/naturalist** (ES + EN, knowledge of nature/culture). Estimated 30-50 qualified in PY. Salary: Gs. 4-7M/month ($533-933)
10. **Yoga instructor** (EN, certified): freelance. Gs. 300-500K per class
11. **Bilingual receptionist + booking manager** (ES + EN, hospitality software). Estimated 50-100 qualified. Salary: Gs. 4-6M/month
12. **Head chef** (ES + EN, farm-to-table + Paraguayan cuisine): rare + expensive. Gs. 4-7M/month. Consider partnership with local chefs
13. **F&B manager** (ES + EN, restaurant ops): also rare. Gs. 5-8M

### Training plan

- **Pre-opening** (3 months before): bilingual staff trained in hospitality software + LQV product knowledge + safety protocols
- **Soft opening** (1 month before): shadow existing eco-lodges (Chaco, Tres Gigantes, Yvy Kretapa)
- **In-house**: monthly training + annual off-site retreat
