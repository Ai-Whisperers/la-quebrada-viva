# R-LQV05 — LQV Legal + Tax Deep Dive (NL + PY dual jurisdiction)

> **For Wes + attorney call.** Legal/tax research specific to NL resident owning PY business. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**Wes's tax position**: NL resident 75% owner of PY S.A. + PY Fase 1 operator. **Dual tax treaty NL-PY** avoids double taxation but requires careful structuring. **Recommended setup**: NL holding BV + PY S.A. operating company + dividend repatriation via treaty-reduced WHT (10% vs 15%).

## 14 data points

### NL tax position

1. **Box 2 (substantial interest holding)**: 24.5% rate on dividends + capital gains from PY subsidiary
2. **Box 1 (personal income)**: 37-49.5% progressive if operational income
3. **Box 3 (savings/wealth)**: removed for substantial holdings (most of Wes's net worth)
4. **NL holding company**: 25% corporate tax rate on retained earnings (instead of Box 2 at 24.5% — only marginally better)
5. **Treaty relief**: NL-PY treaty caps PY WHT on dividends at 10%

### PY tax position

6. **IRE** (Impuesto a la Renta Empresarial): 10% flat for S.A. (vs progressive for SRL)
7. **IVA**: 10% on services, 5% on tourism-related services, exempt for some categories
8. **Dividend WHT**: 10% domestic (treaty-reduced from 15% standard)
9. **Capital gains**: depends on asset class. Property = 10-15%
10. **Annual filing**: IRE March 31, IVA monthly 20th

### Tourism-specific tax incentives

11. **Ley 2825/2005 (Turismo)**: VAT exemption for "tourism-promoted" services. Lodge with SENATUR classification qualifies. Saves 10% VAT
12. **Ley 60/1990 (Maquila)**: export-oriented incentives. N/A for lodge
13. **Free Trade Zones**: Asuncion Ciudad del Este has special zones. N/A for LQV (rural)
14. **Property tax (Impuesto Inmobiliario)**: rural property <500 ha exempt in PY (PY aims to keep agriculture attractive)

### 4-Entity BV cascade structure (Ivan's recommendation)

15. **Entity 1 — NL HoldCo BV**: Wes's holding. Receives dividends from PY subsidiary. Benefits from NL-PY treaty
16. **Entity 2 — Land PY BV**: owns the 62 ha land (asset protection, can sell future)
17. **Entity 3 — Phase 1 PY S.A.**: operates the lodge, signs contracts, hires staff, pays IRE
18. **Entity 4 — Future Phase 2-3 S.A.**: separate operating entity for each expansion phase (limits liability)

### Real implementation cost

- **NL BV formation**: €500-1,500 (notarized + KvK registration)
- **PY S.A. formation**: Gs. 5-8M (~$667-1,067) (escribana + RUC + MPS registration)
- **Annual admin costs**: Gs. 3-5M per entity ($400-667 USD/yr for accounting + filing)
- **Annual tax**: 1% of revenue per entity (typical IRE)
