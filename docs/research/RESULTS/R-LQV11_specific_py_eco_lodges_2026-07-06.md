# R-LQV11 — Specific PY Eco-Lodges + Comparable Sites

> **For Wes + LQV team.** Visitable PY eco-lodges + competing properties. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY has ~10-15 eco-lodges/retreats worth visiting** to benchmark against LQV Phase 1 vision. Most are in **Alto Paraná (Iguazú area)**, **Misiones (Jesuit missions)**, **Concepción (Estancias)**, and **Chaco (wetlands)**. **No comparable eco-lodges exist in Escobar/Paraguarí specifically** — LQV has true whitespace.

## 14 data points

### Tier 1: Visit-worthy PY eco-lodges

1. **Chaco Lodge** (Filadelfia, Chaco): wetlands lodge with mirador. Focuses on birding + flamingo observation (May-Sep). Lodging Gs. 800-1500K/person/day
2. **Tres Gigantes** (Concepción): estancia-style with birding + horseback. Higher-end. USD $200-300/cabin/night
3. **Yvy Kretapa** (San Pedro): eco-lodge in indigenous community context. Cultural depth. USD $100-180/cabin
4. **Estancia Santa Sofía** (Concepción): traditional estancia with horse + farm experiences. $150-250/night full board
5. **Costa Verde Lodge** (Alto Paraná): Iguazú area. Eco + jungle. $150-250/night
6. **Tapy Villeta** (Central): closer to Asunción. Wellness + yoga. $100-150/night
7. **La Soñada** (Caacupé): rural lodge near religious tourist site. Mid-range $80-130/night
8. **Estancia del Sol** (San Ber area): small + intimate. $120-180/night

### Tier 2: Boutique + wellness-focused

9. **Casa del Sol** (San Ber): small luxury B&B. $100-150/night. Restaurant-driven
10. **Cabañas Don Darío** (Caacupé): family-run, low scale. ~$70-100/night
11. **Posada del Sol** (Itá): rustic, popular with cyclists. ~$60-90/night

### Tier 3: Sacred + spiritual

12. **Tatakuá Lodge** (Paraguarí): spiritual + ceremonial focus. Mid-range. Some events
13. **Posada La Herencia** (Concepción): family heritage property. Premium
14. **Bagual Lodge** (Alto Paraná): estancia, horseback + birding. Higher-end eco

### What LQV should NOT directly compete with

- **Premium Iguazú lodges** (different eco-tourism model — jungle-based)
- **Religious tourism in Caacupé** (different customer base)

### LQV's differentiation gaps (whitespace)

- **Escobar/Paraguarí has no comparable eco-lodges** — nearby competitors are tourist hotels in Caacupé/Cordillera
- **Py wellness + yoga retreat** with cob-built eco-position is rare
- **Combining quebrada (creek) + cob + farming + Sonja cultural anchor** = unique combo
