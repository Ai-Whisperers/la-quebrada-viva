## Executive summary

Asunción has **3 international schools** that serve the NL/DE/US/UK expat community: **American School of Asunción (ASA)** — US curriculum $6K-10K/yr, **Colegio Goethe** — German curriculum with IB, **St. Mary's College** — UK/Anglican. Typical tuition: **$6K-12K/yr** for K-12. Enrollment requires 3-12 month lead time + entrance exam. **Distance learning / boarding school abroad** is what some NL/DE expat families choose for older kids (12+). San Bernardino has no international school — San Ber expat families commute to Asunción or use distance learning.

### Boarding school vs home-schooling

- **Boarding school in NL/DE/CH**: popular for kids 14+ when families are bicultural
