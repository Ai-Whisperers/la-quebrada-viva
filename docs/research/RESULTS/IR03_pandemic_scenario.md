# IR03 — Pandemic recurrence scenario

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Risk scenario for long-term planning.

## What IR03 is

**The risk:** What if a pandemic or major health crisis hits again (like COVID)?

**Per LQV's nature-based positioning:**
- LQV is well-suited for pandemic scenarios (open-air, isolated, low-density)
- But the booking curve and travel patterns could change

## Pandemic scenarios

### Scenario 1: Mild recurrence (regional outbreak, 1-2 quarter impact)
- Reduced bookings for 1-2 quarters
- Operational disruption
- Staff may need to be temporarily laid off
- Insurance may not cover pandemics
- **Probability ~30% over 5 years**

### Scenario 2: Major pandemic (COVID-like, 2-4 quarters impact)
- Significant booking loss
- Operational closure or reduced capacity
- Cash flow crisis
- May need emergency funding
- **Probability ~10% over 5 years**

### Scenario 3: Prolonged crisis (1-2+ year impact)
- Total project halt
- Major restructuring needed
- Insurance may not cover
- Major loss of investment value
- **Probability ~5% over 5 years**

## LQV's natural resilience

**Per V04 + V05 (positioning):**
- LQV is in **rural Paraguarí** (low-density, isolated)
- LQV is **open-air** (cabin + pool + garden, minimal indoor)
- LQV is **family-anchored** (people who trust families escape cities)
- LQV is **eco-positioned** (people who value nature over cities)
- LQV is **European** (visa + remittance flexibility)

**LQV is well-suited to attract post-pandemic travelers who prefer:**
- Isolation
- Nature
- Family bonding
- Open-air activities
- Escape from urban density

**LQV may actually benefit** from a mild pandemic if positioned correctly.

## Risk mitigation plan

**Y1 (Fase 1):**
- 3-6 month operating buffer in USD/EUR (per L18)
- Multi-channel marketing (reduce Booking.com dependency)
- Direct booking incentive (avoid commission)
- Cancellation policy: 48 hours full refund

**Y2-3:**
- Build out the cabin-style model (already in plan)
- Add long-stay packages (1-2 weeks, lower density)
- Insurance with international broker (per W0.7)
- Diversified marketing (not over-dependent on any channel)

**Insurance specific:**
- Event cancellation (limited coverage, ~$1-2K/year)
- Business interruption (some coverage, ~$2-3K/year)
- Pandemic (NOT covered, per L22)
- Self-insurance reserve: ~$20-50K (for pandemic scenarios)

**What LQV does NOT do:**
- Rely on standard insurance for pandemic
- Rely on a single market (Booking.com only)
- Rely on a single currency for long-term reserves
