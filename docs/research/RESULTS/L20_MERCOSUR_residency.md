## Options for Wes's legal presence in PY

### Option 1: Tourist visa (current, default)
- 90 days per entry (renewable with border run)
- Suitable for: Fase 1 build supervision + W1.2 site visits
- NOT suitable for: long-term operations manager
- Free
- Just show passport at entry

### Option 2: Temporary resident visa (most common for foreigners)
- For investors, retirees, workers, family
- 1-2 year initial, renewable
- Allows: bank accounts, RUC, vehicle ownership
- Requires: proof of income (~$1,500-2,500/month for retirees) OR investment ($50,000+ in PY)
- Cost: $500-1,500 for the application + lawyer
- Lead time: 2-6 months
- **Recommended for Wes** (matches his status as investor + builder)

### Option 3: Permanent resident visa
- After 2-3 years of temporary residence
- Allows: same as temporary + work rights
- Cost: similar application fees
- More permanent, less renewal

### Option 4: PY citizenship
- After 3 years of legal residence
- Requires: Spanish language proficiency + renunciation of NL citizenship OR dual (PY allows dual with most countries, including NL via treaty)
- Cost: legal fees
- **Long-term option** (Y2030+ for Wes, if he wants)

### Option 5: Don't formalize
- Continue with tourist visa + sign contracts through Kiki as PY-resident
- Simpler but operationally awkward
- **Not recommended for long-term**
