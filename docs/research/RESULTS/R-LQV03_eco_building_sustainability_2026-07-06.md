# R-LQV03 — Eco-Building Materials + Sustainability (PY + global)

> **For LQV.** Sustainability certifications + materials for eco-positioning. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY has no robust eco-certification system** for buildings. International certs (EU Ecolabel, LEED, BREEAM, Green Key) are the standards for marketing to EU travelers. **Cost of certification**: $5-15K USD per building for LEED + $2-5K for Green Key. **LQV recommendation**: focus on Green Key (tourism-specific, EU-recognized, low cost) + participate in EU-funded sustainability programs.

## 14 data points

### International certifications

1. **EU Ecolabel** — EU's official eco-label. Tourism accommodation eligible. ~€2-5K to certify
2. **LEED** (US, USGBC) — most recognized globally. ~$15-50K USD to certify a single lodge
3. **BREEAM** (UK) — older than LEED. ~$10-30K USD
4. **Green Key** — tourism-specific, EU-recognized, cheapest. ~€1-3K + annual fee
5. **EarthCheck** — tourism-specific, science-based, popular in LATAM
6. **Rainforest Alliance** — nature-anchored, tourism-eligible

### PY-specific context

7. **SENATUR eco-certification** — proposed but not formalized. Some lodges self-declare "ecoturismo" without formal cert
8. **MADES (Medio Ambiente)** — requires environmental impact study (EIA) for developments >1 ha built
9. **INFONA** (Instituto Forestal Nacional) — regulates reforestation + native forest protection

### Materials with sustainability angles

10. **Cob** — local, low-embodied-energy, carbon-negative (lime plaster absorbs CO2). 100% on-spec for eco
11. **Eucalyptus structural wood** — fast-growing, locally sourced, FSC-certifiable
12. **Bamboo** — grows in PY (Misiones, Alto Paraná). Strong tensile strength, fast regrowth
13. **Recycled glass** — available in PY for mosaics, decorative elements
14. **Hempcrete / hemp insulation** — emerging in LATAM, limited PY supply

### Energy + water sustainability

15. **Solar PV** — 1.0-1.5 kWh/kWp/day in PY. 9.6 kW system = $15,430 (per F09)
16. **Rainwater capture** — required for eco-cert. 10,000 L cistern typical for 5 cabins
17. **Greywater reed-bed** — replaces septic, lower maintenance
18. **Composting toilets** — waterless, eco-friendly. Some tourist discomfort

## LQV certification recommendation

**Year 1-2**: focus on Green Key application (cheapest, EU-recognized). Costs €2-3K total
**Year 3-4**: consider EarthCheck (LATAM-popular, stronger eco-claims)
**Year 5**: optional LEED certification if targeting corporate retreats
