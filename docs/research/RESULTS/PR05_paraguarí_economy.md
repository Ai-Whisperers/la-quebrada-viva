## Summary

Paraguarí is the **8th-largest department in Paraguay** (8,705 km², ~250,000 people). **Economy is agricultural:** soybean, corn, sugarcane, yerba mate, cattle. Escobar is one of 17 districts within Paraguarí, population ~10,000, economy dominated by small-scale agriculture + cattle ranching. The department is **historically poorer than the eastern departments** (Alto Paraná, Itapúa) but has been catching up due to agricultural expansion.

**For RV:** Paraguarí's economy is **land-rich, labor-cheap, capital-scarce**. The local labor pool knows farming + ranching but not cob/bamboo construction. **Wes will need to train workers** or import them. The region is well-positioned for the **agritourism + eco-tourism** positioning that distinguishes RV from beach tourism in Alto Paraná.

## Key Data Points

- **Paraguarí population:** ~250,000 (vs 3.5M in Central, 800K in Alto Paraná)
- **Main crops:** Soybean (largest), corn, sugarcane, yerba mate, tobacco
- **Cattle:** ~500,000 head in the department
- **Industry:** Limited (mostly small agro-processing)
- **GDP per capita:** ~USD 3,500 (vs national avg ~USD 6,000)
- **Escobar district:** ~10,000 people, agricultural economy, 6% of Paraguarí department
- **Land prices:** USD 1,500-3,500/ha for agricultural land (RV's USD 3,500/ha acquisition is consistent)
- **Roads:** Mostly unpaved; the road to RV is partially paved, partially dirt
- **Police presence:** Minimal (1 comisaría per district)
- **Health:** Small public hospital in Paraguarí town (40 km from RV), no clinic in Escobar
