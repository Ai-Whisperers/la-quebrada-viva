## Summary

Paraguay's insurance market is dominated by **Grupo Asegurador La Rural** (market leader), **Sancor Seguros** (Argentine-owned), **Mapa Worldwide** (Spanish group), **Chaco Internacional**, and **Caja de Seguros Sociales (IPS)** for public health. **Eco-tourism + rural property coverage is a niche** — most PY insurance companies prefer urban property + commercial risks. **For RV, we will need a broker who specializes in hospitality/eco-tourism**, not a generic PY insurance broker.

**Key finding:** The PY insurance market has **limited capacity for eco-tourism-specific risks** (fire + liability + business interruption + property + key-person coverage as a bundled product). Most brokers will quote these as separate policies, often with **non-standard exclusions for rural/agricultural land**. **Expect to pay 1.5-2x** the equivalent NL coverage for Phase 1.

## Key Data Points

- **Top PY insurers (general):**
  1. Grupo Asegurador La Rural — ~30% market share, strongest in rural property
  2. Sancor Seguros — Argentine-owned, ~15% share
  3. Mapa Worldwide — Spanish group, strong in commercial
  4. Chaco Internacional — local Paraguayan, ~10%
  5. Caja de Seguros Sociales (IPS) — government health insurance only
- **Eco-tourism / hospitality specialists:** Few. Need to engage a broker.
- **Typical Phase 1 annual premium estimate:** USD 8,000-15,000 (5 cabins + restaurant + pool + property)
- **Coverage gaps PY insurers don't like:**
  - Flood (often excluded or 50% sub-limit)
  - Wildfire in rural areas (excluded in some policies)
  - Business interruption during low season
  - International guest liability
- **Recommended broker approach:** Engage **2-3 brokers** (PY local + Brazilian + Argentine) for competitive quotes
- **PY regulations:** Insurance is regulated by **SIS** (Superintendencia de Seguros), part of BCP (Banco Central del Paraguay)
