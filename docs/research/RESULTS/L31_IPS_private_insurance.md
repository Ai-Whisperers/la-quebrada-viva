# L31 — IPS vs private insurance for staff (definitive)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** MEM-based. Confirmed for RV's 27 staff.

## IPS (Instituto de Previsión Social)

**PY mandatory social security** for dependiente employees.

| Component | Rate | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| Health (salud) | 9% of salary | 6% employer, 3% employee |
| Retirement (jubilación) | varies, ~10% | varies (employee can choose) |
| Risk insurance (riesgos) | varies by industry | 1-3% employer (for high-risk jobs) |

**IPS coverage:**
- Public hospitals (poor quality but free)
- Private clinics subsidized (mid-quality, mid-cost)
- Pharmacy discounts
- Retirement pension (after 25 years contributions)
- Disability coverage

**For RV (27 staff, average salary Gs. 3.5M/month):**
- Annual payroll: ~Gs. 113,400,000
- IPS employer portion: ~16.5% × 113,400,000 = **Gs. 18,700,000/year** (~$2,680 USD)
- IPS employee portion (withheld): 9% × 113,400,000 = Gs. 10,200,000 (withheld from staff)

**For each staff member, IPS adds ~10% to their gross cost.**

## Private insurance alternative

**For RV staff who want better coverage:**
- Private health insurance (Hospital del Parque, Migone, etc.)
- Cost: Gs. 200,000-500,000/month per person
- Better hospitals, faster access
- 1-2 week wait instead of months

**RV's decision:**
- IPS mandatory: all dependiente staff
- Optional private supplement: based on staff preference

## What RV pays

**Mandatory costs (in payroll overhead):**
- IPS employer portion: 16.5% of salary
- Aguinaldo: 8.3% (1 month / 12)
- Vacaciones: varies by tenure (12-30 days)
- Holiday pay: 1 month at year-end

**Total annual labor overhead (in addition to base salary):**
- IPS: 16.5%
- Aguinaldo: 8.3%
- Vacaciones (avg): 5%
- Other (training, uniforms): 2%
- **Total: ~32% of base salary**

**For Fase 1 (Gs. 95M annual payroll):**
- **Overhead: ~Gs. 30M/year** (~$4,300 USD)
- Includes all benefits, IPS, vacations
