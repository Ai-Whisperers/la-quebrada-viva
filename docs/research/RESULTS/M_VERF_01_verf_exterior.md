## Climate specs required (PY 80%+ RH, 38-45°C summer, UV tropical)

For all exterior coatings, the minimum technical specs:

1. **Anti-fungal/biocide:** EPA-approved isothiazolinone at 0.3% minimum
2. **UV resistance:** 5+ year fade rating on accelerated weathering tests (ASTM G154)
3. **Moisture vapor transmission:** ≥5 perms (breathable to avoid trapping moisture in PY walls)
4. **Temperature cycling:** -5°C to +50°C without cracking
5. **Adhesion on PY substrates:** ≥2 MPa (post-cure, ASTM D4541)
6. **Mildew resistance:** 5+ year rating (ASTM D3273)

All PY-distributed premium brands meet these specs. **Budget brands (Suvinil, Colorín mid-tier) may fail** on anti-fungal rating within 24 months.

## Recommended brand picks per substrate

**Cob walls:** Pinturas Tropical **cal premium** + Sherwin Williams **Silicato Mate** topcoat (best PY + imported blend).
**Bamboo:** **Casa Bek spar varnish line** + UV inhibitor. Coral epoxy alternative.
**Wood trim:** **Alba tung oil + wood preservative**.
**Concrete + foundation:** **Sherwin Williams Loxon** or **Pinturas Tropical Premium Exterior**.

## Key Risks

1. **PY humidity accelerates anti-fungal depletion**; budget annual biocide boost (~$0.50/m²/yr).
2. **Silicate paint on cob requires primer** — skip the primer and the lime base may chalk within 2 years.
3. **Bamboo joints swell/shrink**; spar varnish only at proper humidity, otherwise cracks.
4. **Sherwin Williams Loxon monopoly pricing** in PY; consider Pinturas Tropical equivalent for 20% cheaper.
5. **Colorín + Alba** are sometimes grey-imported; warranty coverage gaps in PY.
