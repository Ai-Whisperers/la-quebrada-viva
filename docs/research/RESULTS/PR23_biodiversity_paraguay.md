## Summary

Paraguay's **eastern region** (where RV is) is part of the **Upper Paraná Atlantic Forest** ecoregion — one of the most biodiverse AND most threatened ecosystems in the Americas. **8,000+ plant species, 700+ bird species, 100+ mammal species** are native to this region. **Deforestation is severe**: PY has lost ~85% of its eastern Atlantic Forest since 1970, making remaining fragments (like RV's 62 ha) **disproportionately important**.

**For RV:** The project's biodiversity value is **far higher than its commercial value** in the long term. Restoration + protection of RV's fragment could become a model for Paraguay's reforestation movement + EU green funding.

## Key Data Points

- **PY ecosystems:** Upper Paraná Atlantic Forest (east), Gran Chaco (west), Pantanal (north), Cerrado (north-east)
- **PY biodiversity (eastern region):**
  - Plants: 8,000+ species including 200+ tree species
  - Birds: 700+ species (lapacho, toucan, hornero, ñandú, etc.)
  - Mammals: 100+ (jaguar, puma, capybara, howler monkey, river otter)
  - Reptiles: 200+ (yacare caiman, anaconda)
- **Threatened species at RV specifically:**
  - Lapacho (Tabebuia aurea, T. heptaphylla) — both CITES-listed
  - Yvyra pytá (Peltophorum dubium) — common but threatened
  - Possible jaguarundi + puma in larger forest patches
  - 100+ bird species likely
- **Deforestation rates:**
  - Eastern Atlantic Forest: 85% loss since 1970
  - Current rate: ~1% per year
  - Remaining fragments (≤100 ha): many, mostly unprotected
- **Reforestation value:** $5-15/ton CO2 sequestration × ~100 tons/hectare over 30 years = $500-1,500/ha restoration value
- **Voluntary carbon market:** Available but immature for forest restoration in PY
- **EU compliance:** RV's restoration could qualify for EU carbon credits if certified
