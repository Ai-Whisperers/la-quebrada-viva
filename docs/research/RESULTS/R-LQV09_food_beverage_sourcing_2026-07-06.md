# R-LQV09 — Food + Beverage Sourcing + Menu Planning (PY rural)

> **For LQV restaurant operations.** Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**LQV restaurant sourcing strategy**:
- **70% local + seasonal** (PY + Paraguari area producers)
- **20% regional** (Misiones, Alto Paraná)
- **10% import** (BR wine, EU specialty, NL cheese if targeted at expats)

**Menu structure**: 60-cover restaurant, 4 daily specials + 8 standard dishes + 6 desserts. **Food cost target**: 30-38% of F&B revenue (industry standard for fine dining). **Avg check**: Gs. 80-150K ($11-20 USD) lunch, Gs. 120-250K ($16-33 USD) dinner.

## 14 data points

### Local sourcing (<100 km, 0-50 USD per delivery)

1. **Fresh vegetables** — Caacupé + Piribebuy + Paraguarí farmers. Daily delivery, Gs. 5-20K/kg depending on veg
2. **Eggs** — local farms. Gs. 800K/30 units ($1.07 per dozen equivalent)
3. **Dairy (fresh + cheese)** — Mennonite colonies in Chaco + Paraguarí. Gs. 25-50K/L (milk), 80-150K/kg (artisanal cheese)
4. **Pork + chicken** — local. Gs. 35-50K/kg
5. **Fresh herbs** — grow on-site. Initial: Gs. 200-500K setup, ongoing: low
6. **Honey** — Missions area + Caaguazú. Gs. 80-150/kg

### Regional (100-400 km)

7. **Beef (PY)**: Chaco + Alto Paraná. World-class grass-fed beef. Gs. 50-80K/kg
8. **Fish**: Paraná River (Encarnación). Gs. 60-120/kg
9. **Specialty sausages**: from AR/BR suppliers via CDE. Gs. 80-120/kg
10. **Wine (BR + AR)**: CDE imports. Gs. 60-200K/bottle
11. **Coffee**: BR specialty (Mogiana, Sul de Minas). Gs. 30-60K/250g

### Imports (>400 km)

12. **EU specialty (cheese, olive oil)**: limited NL expat market ~3-5% of guests
13. **Asian/Wasabi/French techniques**: trained chef can source via specialty importers
14. **Seafood**: Buenos Aires imports (Mar del Plata). Gs. 80-200/kg
