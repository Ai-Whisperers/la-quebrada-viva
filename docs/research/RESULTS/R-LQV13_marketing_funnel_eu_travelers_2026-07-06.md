# R-LQV13 — Marketing Funnel: EU/NL Travelers → LQV Bookings

> **For Wes.** How EU + NL travelers discover + book PY eco-lodges. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**EU/NL traveler funnel to LQV has 6 stages**: awareness → interest → consideration → intent → booking → advocacy. **Each stage requires different marketing tactics**. **Total conversion benchmark**: <0.5% awareness-to-booking. **LQV's realistic TAM**: 50-100 EU travelers/year in Phase 1, scaling to 200-400 by Year 3.

## 14 data points

### Stage 1 — Awareness (top of funnel)

1. **Instagram/Google paid ads**: target NL/DE/FR audiences interested in "travel Paraguay", "eco tourism", "off the beaten path"
2. **SEO blog content**: "best eco lodges in Paraguay", "Paraguay wellness retreats", "Paraguay vs Costa Rica"
3. **Travel PR**: publish stories in NL Lonely Planet-equivalent (Reizen Magazine NL, GEO Saison DE), Conde Nast Traveler
4. **NL travel influencers**: 3-5 macro-influencers (@lessthanamonth, @i_amsterdam-style travel) for a hosted stay
5. **Travel trade shows**: Vakantiebeurs (Utrecht), ITB Berlin, WTM London

### Stage 2 — Interest

6. **Compelling imagery**: hero photo of cabin + quebrada. Bilingual website
7. **Storytelling**: "Wes + Sonja's 20-year journey to build this", "How 62 ha of forest became a retreat"
8. **USP clarity**: "Only cob-built + quebrada + bilingual retreat in Paraguay"
9. **Email capture**: "Sign up for opening updates + early-bird rates"

### Stage 3 — Consideration

10. **Pricing transparency**: clear ADR + bundled offers
11. **Trust signals**: TripAdvisor reviews, Google reviews, Booking.com reviews
12. **Sample itinerary**: "7-day sample itinerary" page on website

### Stage 4 — Intent

13. **Messaging click-to-chat**: most NL/DE prefer Messaging over email
14. **Quick response time**: <2 hr during business hours
15. **Open questions answered via direct message**

### Stage 5 — Booking

16. **Cloudbeds booking engine**: real-time availability + bookable
17. **Multiple payment options**: iDEAL (NL), Bancontact (BE), credit card, SEPA bank transfer
18. **Cancellation policy**: clear, fair

### Stage 6 — Advocacy

19. **Pre-arrival**: welcome email + PDF itinerary
20. **During stay**: build Instagrammable moments, encourage tagging
21. **Post-stay**: thank-you + review request + offer referral discount
22. **Referral code**: "Tell a friend, both get 10% off next stay"

### Budget allocation (Year 1, Gs. 2-3M/month $267-400)

23. **35% Instagram/Meta Ads**: NL + DE targeting
24. **25% Google Ads**: brand + non-brand keywords
25. **15% Content production**: photography + videography
26. **15% Website + Cloudbeds**: maintenance + SEO
27. **10% PR + influencer activations**: occasional hostings
