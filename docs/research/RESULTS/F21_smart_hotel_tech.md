# F21 — AI-driven operational optimization (smart hotel tech)

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** Forward-looking. Fase 2-3 implementation.

## What F21 is

**Smart hotel technology for RV:**
- AI-driven guest experience (chatbots, smart rooms)
- Operational optimization (energy, water, staff scheduling)
- Predictive maintenance (sensor-based)
- Revenue management (AI pricing per night)

## Categories

### Guest-facing
- **AI chatbot** (WhatsApp Business API) for inquiries
- **Smart room controls** (lights, temperature, locks via app)
- **Personalized recommendations** (which cabin, which activities)
- **Digital concierge** (multi-language)
- **Voice assistant** (Spanish + Dutch + German)

### Operations
- **Energy management** (smart thermostats, solar integration)
- **Water management** (leak detection, smart irrigation)
- **Waste monitoring** (smart bins, recycle tracking)
- **Predictive maintenance** (sensor on AC, pool, kitchen)
- **Staff scheduling** (AI-optimized)
- **Inventory tracking** (auto-restock)

### Revenue
- **Dynamic pricing** (AI-driven per night, season, demand)
- **Channel management** (Booking, Airbnb, direct)
- **Upsell optimization** (spa, excursions)
- **Loyalty program** (data-driven)

## Cost-benefit

**Total smart hotel tech investment (Y1-Y3): $25-40K**

**Benefits:**
- 10-15% energy savings: $2-3K/year
- 5-10% occupancy increase: $30-100K/year (depending on volume)
- 20-30% labor productivity: $5-10K/year
- Better reviews (AI-personalized experience): +0.1-0.3 stars
- **Total benefits: $40-115K/year**

**ROI: 1-3 years payback** (depending on volume + execution)
