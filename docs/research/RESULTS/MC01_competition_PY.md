## RV's competitive position

### Strengths
- **30 cabins planned** — among the largest eco-lodges
- **NL + German ownership** — unique in PY (most eco-lodges are local-owned)
- **Family-anchored community** — differentiator (most are couples-only or families by chance)
- **Wellness pool (natural water, no chlorine)** — unique eco-feature
- **Atlantic Forest setting** — specific biodiversity (vs generic forest)
- **Slow-stay positioning** — vs. quick weekend trips
- **4-BV structure** — institutional-grade investor structure
- **Mature market timing** (post-COVID) — eco-travel demand is high

### Weaknesses
- **New brand** — no track record yet
- **Remote location** — 2-3 hour drive from Asunción
- **PY infrastructure challenges** — roads, power, water, etc.
- **Small staff base** — initially dependent on Wes + Sonja
- **Permit complexity** — multi-jurisdiction
- **Insurance uncertainty** — eco-lodges hard to insure

### Opportunities
- **PY has very few large eco-lodges** — RV fills a gap
- **European market** — Wes's network + family connections
- **PY is "undiscovered" by Europeans** — first-mover advantage
- **Sonja's 60th in 2030** — built-in anchor for momentum
- **Volcano + eco-tourism** — regional cluster
- **Family travel growth** — post-pandemic trend

### Threats
- **New eco-lodges popping up** — competition growing
- **Awasi Iguazú expansion** — high-end competitor
- **Direct EUR pricing** — Wes can compete on this
- **Brazilian competitors** (Bonito region) — nearby competition
- **Argentine competitors** (Iberá) — same continent

## RV's differentiation strategy

**1. Scale:**
- 30 cabins vs. typical 4-12
- 1 of the largest eco-lodges in PY

**2. Quality:**
- European-quality build + service
- Sustainable (eco-cert by Y2-3)
- Wellness + nature combined

**3. Family focus:**
- Multi-gen travel
- Cabin clusters + community hub
- Not couples-only

**4. Story:**
- Sonja's 60th = personal anchor
- Wes + Sonja's journey = relatable
- 4-BV cascade = institutional quality
- PY first, European quality = unique positioning

**5. Location:**
- Atlantic Forest (specific ecosystem)
- 2-3 hour from Asunción (accessible, not isolated)
- Near San Bernardino + Mennonite colonies (cross-promotion)

**If RV fails:**
