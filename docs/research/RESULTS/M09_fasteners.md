## Scope of fasteners for RV (Fase 1, 5 cabins)

Per cabin: 1,000-2,000 fasteners total
- Wood screws (decking, framing, finish): 60% of count
- Nails (framing, sheathing): 25%
- Bolts (structural, log connections): 10%
- Specialty (joist hangers, brackets, anchors): 5%

**Fase 1 total:** ~7,500 fasteners across 5 cabins + reception building
**Full Phase 1 (30 cabins):** ~45,000 fasteners

## Sourcing strategy

### Option 1: Local ferretería in Asunción (recommended for Fase 1)
- **Major PY chains:** Ferretería Americana, Ferretería Santa María, El Amigo Ferretero
- CDE (Ciudad del Este) importers: best prices for bulk fasteners, ~3-4 hours from Asunción
- **Lead time:** 1-3 days
- **Cost per fastener:** Gs. 50-500 depending on type
- **Per-cabin fasteners cost:** ~Gs. 1,000,000-2,000,000 = $140-280
- **Fase 1 total:** ~Gs. 5-10M = $700-1,400

### Option 2: Bulk import from Brazil (for Fase 2+ or if scale justifies)
- Brazilian fastener manufacturers: Ciser, Gerdau, GBN
- Import via CDE (no import tax, Mercosur benefit)
- Lead time: 4-6 weeks
- **Minimum order:** 50+ kg (worth it)
- **Per kg cost:** 50-70% lower than local

### Option 3: Direct from China (for very high volume)
- Only makes sense at >100 cabin scale
- 8-14 weeks + customs
- Need PY import broker

## Common gotchas

- **Outdoor screws:** Must be stainless steel (A2/A4) or hot-dip galvanized. Cheap zinc-plated screws rust in 2-3 years in PY humidity
- **Stainless vs galvanized cost difference:** 3-4x
- **Joist hangers:** Use stainless for outdoor decks; galvanized OK for indoor framing
- **Roofing screws:** EPDM-sealed, with neoprene washers, to prevent leaks
- **Concrete anchors:** Use wedge anchors, not sleeve anchors, for heavy loads
