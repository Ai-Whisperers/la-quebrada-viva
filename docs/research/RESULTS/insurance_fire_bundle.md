## I05 — Worker injury insurance (IPS-related)

**Status:** Already in the Sonja questionnaire (Q9-Q10). Will be answered in that call.

### Brief
- **PY IPS (Instituto de Previsión Social)** is the mandatory social security for dependiente employees
- Employer contribution: ~16.5% of salary
- Employee contribution: ~9%
- **Voluntary insurance (seguro particular)** for non-IPS workers (independiente, contractor, etc.)
- For Fase 1 (5-8 workers), the IPS cost = 16.5% × total payroll ≈ **Gs. 4-6M/year** (~€500-800)

### Sources
- IPS official: https://www.ips.gov.py/
- Sonja questionnaire (Q9): confirms exact calculation method for RV's worker mix

## R01 — Fire safety plan

**Status:** **URGENT. Insurance underwriting requirement.**

### Why it's critical
- RV is 82% Atlantic Forest canopy
- Paraguay's dry season (Aug-Oct) + accidental ignition = high fire risk
- A single lightning strike in dry season can wipe out hectares in hours
- **Insurance underwriters will not cover a wooden-structure park in forested area without a fire safety plan**

### What the plan must include
1. **Firebreaks:** cleared strips of land, 5-10m wide, around all structures and at property boundaries
2. **Water points:** hydrants or water storage at strategic points (combined with F15 cistern sizing)
3. **Access roads:** maintained for fire truck access (F05 road conditions)
4. **Detection:** smoke detectors in each cabin + watchtower in Fase 2
5. **Response:** contact with local volunteer fire brigade (if exists) + nearest municipal fire station
6. **Building standards:** fire-resistant roof materials, defensible space around each structure

### Cost estimate
- Firebreak clearing: 1-2 weeks of tractor work = $1,000-2,000
- Water points: 4-6 hydrants × $200 = $800-1,200
- Smoke detectors (1 per cabin + common): ~$50 each
- **Total Fase 1 fire safety: ~$3,000-5,000**

### Source
- Wes's audio D explicitly: "Alles op het gebied van verzekeringen checken, voor een park, voor een bosbrand."
- His mitigation instinct: clear a firebreak (the train line is on one side of the property, but not the other)
